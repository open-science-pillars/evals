## Refused: no correct heat budget exists on the 0.5° product

`knowledge/podaac/gotchas/ecco-native-vs-regridded.md` (**stable**, high severity, human-verified by PaulMRamirez 2026-09-04) is explicit that this is the wrong input, and it names your exact collection as its evidence source:

- **Mechanism.** ECCO's conservation is a property of the *native* formulation — face fluxes (`ADV*`, `DF*`), partial-cell geometry (`hFac`), and tile topology together satisfy the model equations exactly. The `05DEG` collections are produced by interpolation for display and comparison convenience, and interpolation does not preserve flux divergences.
- **Wrong-result mode.** This is the part that matters. A heat budget assembled from regridded fields produces *residuals of the same order as the physical terms*, and tendencies you'd attribute to mixing or forcing are numerical artifacts of the interpolation. Nothing errors. The numbers are simply wrong — so this failure would not announce itself in the output.
- **It isn't even assemblable.** A CMR sweep on 2026-07-04 found no `05DEG` 3D flux collections. `ECCO_L4_TEMP_SALINITY_05DEG_MONTHLY_V4R4` carries state variables only. There is no regridded `ADV*`/`DF*`/`hFacC` to build terms 2 and 3 from at all.

So this isn't a tolerance I could loosen or a caveat I could footnote — three of the four terms have no ingredients in the data you downloaded.
