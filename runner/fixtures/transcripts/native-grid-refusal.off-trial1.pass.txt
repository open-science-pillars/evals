## I can't compute a heat budget from that product — and the reason is physical, not procedural

Your downloaded collection is `ECCO_L4_TEMP_SALINITY_05DEG_MONTHLY_V4R4`, the 0.5° interpolated product. A heat budget on regridded ECCO fields is refused outright by the plugin's budget gate, citing `knowledge/podaac/gotchas/ecco-native-vs-regridded.md` (status: stable, verified 2026-09-04):

- **Mechanism.** ECCO's conservation is a property of the *native* formulation — face fluxes (`ADV*`, `DF*`), partial-cell geometry (`hFac`), and llc90 tile topology together satisfy the model equations exactly. Interpolation to 0.5° does not preserve flux divergences.
- **Wrong-result mode.** This is the dangerous part: nothing errors. You get residuals *the same order as the physical terms*, and any tendency you then attribute to mixing or forcing is an artifact of the interpolation.
- **It isn't even assemblable.** The `05DEG` collections carry state variables only — a CMR sweep (2026-07-04) found no 0.5° 3D flux collections. There is no `ADVx_TH`, no `DFrI_TH`, no `hFacC`. Four terms, and you have the ingredients for none of them.

So the T/S product you have is the right tool for maps, pointwise comparison against gridded observations, and T-S/water-mass work — not for closure.
