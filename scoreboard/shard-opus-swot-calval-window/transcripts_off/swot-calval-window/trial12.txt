## The time series I'd build

Method, not dataset facts — none of this depends on the blocked concepts.

**The time axis isn't calendar time.** Granules are whole passes, so a regional search returns every pass touching the box; the region is sampled by a fixed set of passes, each repeating once per cycle, unevenly spaced within the cycle. So the primary object is a **per-pass series**: one record per (cycle, pass, swath side), stamped at the pass's mid-time over the region — not a daily or monthly mean. A per-cycle aggregate sits on top of that, and it's honest only because a cycle is the repeat unit; a monthly resample would blend passes that sample different parts of the box.

**Per record, over flag-gated pixels only**, left and right swath kept separate with the nadir gap intact and no gridding at load time:

- valid-pixel count and masked fraction, with the dominant flag reasons
- area-weighted mean `ssha`, crossover correction applied per the gotcha's prescribed field and gate, stated explicitly as applied
- spatial variance of `ssha` within the box — for the Agulhas this is the more informative series than the mean
- the uncertainty variables the dataset concept's Uncertainty section names, carried alongside
- the `crid` of every granule contributing, because baseline drift within a collection is a recorded known issue and consistency claims across the year depend on that record

**Two things would dominate the interpretation.** First, any series crossing the mid-2023 transition has a **sampling discontinuity at the transition** — revisit interval and swath coverage change — which will look like a geophysical shift in a regional mean and isn't one. I'd plot the phases as separate segments rather than one continuous line. Second, the Agulhas retroflection is about as mesoscale-dominated as the ocean gets: a box-mean `ssha` series is largely a record of which rings and meanders happened to fall under the swath on each pass, so I'd lead with the variance/EKE-like statistic and the per-pass spatial fields, and treat the box mean as a diagnostic rather than the result. If you want a genuinely continuous regional mean across the full 13 months, SWOT alone will alias badly there and gridded multi-mission SSH is the better backbone, with SWOT supplying the structure the gridded product can't resolve.
