## The time series, as far as method goes

This part is general method, not dataset fact, and the sampling cadence — the thing that actually defines the series — is the piece I still can't confirm:

- **The sample is a pass crossing, not a date.** SWOT gives you a swath sweeping the box at intervals set by the repeat orbit, so the native index is (cycle, pass), irregularly spaced in time. I'd build the series on that index and resample to a calendar only as a deliberate, separate step.
- **It is not one homogeneous record.** The cadence changes mid-window at the orbit transition. I'd expect to build it as two segments and characterize each before splicing — a single regression through the join would mix two different sampling regimes.
- **Order of operations:** decode the bit-packed quality flags and mask → apply the crossover correction (it arrives unapplied, with a quality gate the gotcha specifies) → trim whole passes to the box → *then* aggregate. Subset before you aggregate.
- **Swaths stay separate, gap stays open.** Left and right swaths carry independent error profiles, so I'd summarize per swath. The nadir gap is a real data void; filling it is fabrication, not interpolation.
- **Per sample:** area-weighted SSHA mean over good pixels in the box, valid-pixel count, flagged fraction with dominant reasons, and the product's own uncertainty variables carried along.
- **Caveat worth stating up front:** in the Agulhas, an SSHA box mean is dominated by retroflection ring shedding and meander position. It's a mesoscale-activity index more than a regional sea level signal, and with irregular sampling it will alias. If you want the eddy story, an eddy-kinetic-energy or SSHA-variance series per pass crossing will be more interpretable than the mean.
