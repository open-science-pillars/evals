## The time series I'd build — and the part that's genuinely open

Fixed by method, independent of what the concepts say:

- **Search whole passes, trim after.** Cycle/pass is the acquisition unit, so it's a spatial search over whole passes against your box, then a trim to the region — subset before aggregating, never the reverse.
- **Swaths stay separate.** Left and right swath carry independent error profiles, so every statistic is reported per-swath, and the nadir gap stays a gap. No interpolation across it, no flattening to a regular grid as part of the load.
- **Flags gate every pixel** before any number is computed, with the masked fraction and dominant flag reasons reported alongside.
- **The crossover correction gets applied** per the crossover concept's field and quality gate, and the summary says so explicitly — an `ssha` series built without it is the failure mode that concept is about.
- **The series itself:** one point per pass-over-region, carrying area-weighted mean SSHA over good pixels, spatial variance (the interesting signal in the Agulhas — eddy shedding at the retroflection), valid-pixel count, and the propagated uncertainty from the native per-sample fields.
