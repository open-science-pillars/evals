## The time series I'd build — design only, labeled as general method

Dataset-specific parameters below are marked *pending concept*; the structural reasoning is mine and doesn't depend on the bundle.

- **The sample unit is a pass crossing, not a date.** SWOT doesn't give you a daily field over a box; it gives you a handful of swath crossings per repeat cycle. The series index is `(cycle, pass, timestamp)`, irregularly spaced — not a calendar grid. Resampling to monthly is an analysis choice made afterward, with the aliasing stated.
- **It is almost certainly two series, not one.** The cadence and the pass geometry over the box both change at the mid-2023 phase transition. Concatenating across it into a single record is the silent failure the orbit-phase concept exists to prevent. I'd build them as two segments, plot them adjacent, and only merge if that concept says the quantity is comparable.
- **Per-point reduction.** Box-mean of flag-passing `ssha` pixels, computed **separately for left and right swath**, nadir gap left empty, no gridding and no gap-filling at load time. Each point carries valid-pixel count, flagged fraction with dominant flag reasons, per-swath split, the processing baseline the granule actually reported, and the uncertainty variable.
- **Crossover correction applied before any statistic** — the dataset concept names the field and its quality gate (*pending concept*), and the summary must state explicitly that it was applied.
- **Baseline drift is a trend hazard.** Granules within one collection can carry different processing baselines; a drift across your 13 months can masquerade as signal. I'd record the baseline per point and check it before quoting any slope.

**One scientific caveat worth raising now:** the Agulhas retroflection is among the highest-EKE regions in the ocean. A box-mean SSHA there is dominated by mesoscale rings entering and leaving the box — tens of cm of eddy signal aliased into the mean — not by a seasonal or secular cycle. If your question is about variability rather than a regional mean level, a box-mean series will mostly measure eddy bookkeeping. An SSHA-variance (EKE-proxy) series per cycle, or a retroflection-position / ring-shedding series, would be the more honest product from swath data over this box. Worth deciding before we pull 13 months.
