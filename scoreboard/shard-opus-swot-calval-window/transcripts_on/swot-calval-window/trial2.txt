## The time series I'd actually build

**Two series, not one.** My recommendation is the science phase alone — **2023-07-21 → 2024-03-31, cycles 001–~013** — as the continuous record, with the cal/val window analyzed separately as a high-cadence case study if the sparse 1-day tracks happen to cross the Agulhas at all. That last point is exactly what the blocked granule search would settle; I'm not going to assert cal/val coverage of 10–45°E, 45–25°S without it.

**Its shape.** Not a regular daily or monthly series. The sampling unit is a *pass crossing the box*, so what comes out is an irregular series indexed by (cycle, pass, swath side) — several distinct passes per 21-day cycle, each sampling a different part of the region. Two honest renderings:

- per-pass region-mean SSHA at native pass times (irregular, but preserves what was sampled where);
- a cycle-aggregated regional mean at 21-day resolution (~12 points over the window) — regular, but each point is a mosaic of passes from different days within the cycle.

**What constrains every value in it.** Per `knowledge/podaac/gotchas/swot-crossover-unapplied.md` (stable, human-verified, severity high): the crossover calibration is *not* pre-applied. Each value must be `ssha_karin + height_cor_xover`, gated by `height_cor_xover_qual`, and the series must say so. On the uncorrected field, flag-gated statistics carry a spurious cross-track ramp spanning about ±2.9 m — against the ~0.17 m std of real mesoscale structure on the same scene. Flag gating alone does not make a regional mean safe.

Then: left and right swaths kept separate (independent error profiles), the ~20 km nadir gap left as a void, trim to the box before aggregating (CMR matches whole pole-to-pole passes; some matched passes carry zero in-box pixels), and record each granule's `crid` — a 13-month subset will likely mix baselines, and the dataset concept's known-issues section makes consistency claims across the series depend on that record.

**Error bars.** `ssh_karin_uncert` is per-sample *random* noise only. It excludes the correlated swath-scale systematics (roll, phase, timing, wet-troposphere residuals) that dominate a regional mean, so uncertainty cannot be `uncert/√N`. That's the concept's Uncertainty section, and it's the part most likely to be quietly wrong in a finished plot.

**Agulhas caveat, on general method — no installed concept covers this region.** A region-mean SSHA over 10–45°E, 45–25°S in a high-EKE regime is dominated by ring passage through the box rather than by any regional signal, and a 21-day repeat is marginal against ring timescales. If the target is Agulhas rings or retroflection variability, per-pass swath structure or an EKE diagnostic will carry more than the mean will. Worth deciding before the load, since it may change the tier.
