**Series A — cal/val, ~2023-03-30 to 2023-07-10** *(only if Agulhas is under a fixed track)*. Near-daily revisit on a handful of tracks. This is a high-cadence, tiny-footprint record: good for eddy propagation, internal tides, and sub-inertial variability along one line. **Series B — science, 2023-07-21 to 2024-03-31.** Roughly **12 to 13 twenty-one-day cycles**, each giving near-complete 2-D coverage of the box. That is the governing constraint on everything downstream: **about twelve points.** One value per cycle, plotted at the cycle midpoint.

For the per-cycle quantity, in order of how well they survive SWOT's error structure in this region:

1. **Per-cycle SSH variance or EKE over a fixed mask** — a mesoscale-activity index. Best choice for the Agulhas, because it's built from spatial *differences* and is far less sensitive to the residual swath-scale systematics that bias any mean.
2. **Cross-current SSH gradient across a fixed section** (e.g. near the ASCA line, ~34°S) as a geostrophic transport proxy. Plays to KaRIn's strength, but this is precisely the quantity the unapplied crossover ramp destroys if you skip `height_cor_xover`.
3. **Area-weighted mean SSHA over the box** — the obvious choice, and the weakest. Use the *same* valid-pixel mask every cycle, or changing coverage masquerades as signal.

Per-swath throughout, nadir gap left intact, no interpolation across it.

**What this series can and cannot say.** Twelve points over eight months, in a region where Agulhas rings shed every one to two months and Natal pulses recur at 50–70 days: 21-day cycles marginally resolve ring shedding and completely alias the faster current dynamics. **No sea level trend should be quoted from it** — 13 months of mesoscale variability of O(20 cm) in the strongest eddy field in the ocean will produce a slope that means nothing.

**Error bars cannot come from the product.** `ssh_karin_uncert` is per-sample *random* noise at 1-sigma. Averaged over the millions of pixels in a cycle-box, that term collapses to near zero — so the real uncertainty on each point is dominated entirely by the correlated swath-scale systematics (roll, phase, timing, wet-troposphere residuals) that the concept states `*_uncert` does **not** include. The error bar has to be built from cross-swath and crossover-difference statistics, not from the uncertainty variable.

Two more things to carry as provenance: capture the `crid` of every granule loaded, because a step between cycles 001 and 013 can be a processing-baseline change rather than ocean signal; and note that `ssha_karin` is referenced to `mean_sea_surface_cnescls`, which cancels in cycle-to-cycle differences but not in an absolute mean.

Use SWOT for the 2-D structure *within* cycles, where nothing else comes close.
