## The issue that shapes your answer

March 2023 → March 2024 straddles SWOT's transition from the 1-day cal/val orbit to the 21-day science orbit. That is the single most consequential fact about this window, and it is exactly what `gotchas/swot-calval-orbit-phases.md` covers — the concept I couldn't read. From general mission knowledge, unverified here: the two phases differ in repeat period, in which passes cover any given box, and in cycle numbering, and they're held in different version families — so one collection queried across the whole window can come back covering only part of it, with no error. **Read that concept before committing to the range.** If the window is what you care about, expect to treat it as two records, not one.

## The time series I'd build

Assuming the Agulhas box as **10°E–40°E, 45°S–30°S** (current, retroflection, return current) — say if you want the retroflection alone.

The honest time axis is **one sample per swath overflight of the box**, irregularly spaced, not a daily or weekly series. KaRIn L2 SSH is swath data on a cycle/pass grid; any regular cadence is something you'd impose, not something the data has.

On that axis I'd build two quantities, and I'd *not* build a third:

1. **Cross-front SSH difference → geostrophic transport proxy.** This is the strongest use of KaRIn here. A fixed section across the Agulhas Current, with the transport proxy from the SSH drop across the front. Unlike nadir altimetry you get the full two-dimensional cross-front gradient per overflight, so the front's position and width are observed rather than assumed — which matters in a current that meanders.
2. **Box SSHA variance / EKE per overflight.** The Agulhas signal is mesoscale — rings shed at the retroflection — and variance captures it.

I'd skip a **box-mean SSHA** series: the mean over this box averages across the current's strong dipole and mostly cancels the signal you came for.

Construction rules, from the loading contract in the skill:
- Statistics per swath, left and right kept separate — independent error profiles.
- The nadir gap stays a void. A section that lands on it loses its centre; that overflight is a gap in the series, not a value to interpolate.
- Quality flags decoded and pixels masked before any statistic, with the masked fraction and dominant reasons reported alongside each point.
- The crossover correction arrives unapplied and is applied via `height_cor_xover` (`skills/swot/SKILL.md:108`); its quality gate is specified in the gotcha I couldn't read, and since your goal is statistics, that concept is not optional.
- **Processing baseline drift is a real risk over 13 months.** Granules spanning a year may carry different baselines, which can put a step or a spurious trend into a transport series. The dataset concept records this as a known issue; the fix is to record what each granule actually carries and check for steps at baseline boundaries.

Net: expect **two segmented records split at the orbit transition, on irregular per-overflight time axes**, reported with their sampling explicit — not one continuous 13-month curve. Splicing them into a single trend would be the error this window invites.
