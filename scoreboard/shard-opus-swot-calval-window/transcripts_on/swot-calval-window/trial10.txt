## The time series I'd build

Assuming the blockers clear, here's the design — this part is method, and doesn't depend on the missing concepts.

**The unit is (cycle, pass), not "a day."** The Agulhas box is crossed by a handful of passes; each gives two swaths with the nadir gap between them. So the natural series is one value per pass per cycle — irregularly spaced, clustered within each repeat period, not a daily or monthly series. I'd resist resampling to a regular grid at load time; that's an analysis choice made later and deliberately.

**The quantity.** A box-mean SSHA is the obvious first cut and the weakest one. The Agulhas is among the highest-EKE regions in the ocean, and a regional mean over partial, varying swath coverage is dominated by which eddies happened to fall under the swath — sampling noise, not signal. I'd build three series instead:

- **SSHA variance / eddy kinetic energy per pass**, which is what KaRIn's wide swath actually buys you over nadir altimetry, and is far more robust to partial coverage than the mean.
- **A fixed-footprint mean** — restricted to a sub-box that a given pass covers on every cycle, so successive points are comparable. One series per pass, not one merged series.
- **Retroflection / front position** along each pass, from the SSH gradient, which is a geometric quantity that partial coverage degrades gracefully.

**Construction rules.**

- Left and right swaths stay separate; cross-track systematic error varies with the signed cross-track coordinate, so a whole-swath mean blends pixels with different error profiles. Never interpolate the nadir gap.
- Decode the bit-packed quality flags and mask before any statistic; carry the flagged fraction and dominant flag reasons alongside every point. An unflagged statistic isn't a statistic.
- Apply the crossover correction — it arrives unapplied in L2, and the `swot` skill points at `height_cor_xover` with a quality gate defined in the concept I can't currently read. Without the concept I'd know to apply it but not what gate to apply it under, which matters for a variance series.
- Attach the coverage fraction to every point. A regional mean from 40% swath coverage and one from 90% are not the same measurement.
- Uncertainty: per-pixel `ssha` uncertainty variables travel with the data, but errors are spatially correlated across a swath, so √N averaging will badly overstate precision on a box mean. Block bootstrap over along-track segments is the honest route.

**What the 13 months can and can't support.** One annual cycle, broken in the middle by the orbit transition — so the cal/val-phase and science-phase segments aren't a continuous record and shouldn't be plotted as one line without a visible break. No trend is extractable from this window; in a region with this much mesoscale variance, 13 months of irregular sampling won't separate seasonal from interannual either. It's a variability and structure dataset, not a trend dataset.
