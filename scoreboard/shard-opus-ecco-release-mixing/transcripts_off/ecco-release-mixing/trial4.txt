## Your premise needs one correction

You asked me to "use whatever ECCO SSH collections cover the full period." That framing has a trap in it. ECCO v4r4 *is* 1992–2017, and monthly SSH covers the whole span in a **single** collection. The nine `ECCO_L4_SSH_*` ShortNames aren't complementary pieces to stitch — they're alternative renderings of the same estimate along three axes:

- **Release:** `V4R4` vs `V4R4B`, the latter the corrected geodetic re-release. The shipped seed manifest says verbatim: *"never mix V4R4 and V4R4B in one analysis without saying so."* Concatenating them across 1992–2017 could manufacture a step that a linear fit would absorb as signal — precisely the trend you asked me to test for. (Note `SNAPSHOT` exists only in V4R4.)
- **Grid:** `LLC0090GRID` (native) vs `05DEG` (interpolated). For a regional average the workflow wants native, area-weighted.
- **IB convention:** `SSH`, `SSHIBC`, `SSHNOIBC`, `ETAN` live *inside* the collection and mean different things.

So: one collection, one release, one variant — not a union.

**My recommendation:** `ECCO_L4_SSH_LLC0090GRID_MONTHLY_V4R4` (DOI `10.5067/ECL5M-SSH44`), variable `SSH`, area-weighted natively.

It comes from a *seed* file (`tools/ecco_v4r4_families.yaml`) whose own header says *"treat verify_cmr output as the authority and this file as the seed"*, and from the attested partition's pass bar requiring `ssh_variant` exactly `SSH`. ## The method, once data exists

This part I can state firmly, because the executor ships and I read it. `ecco_trend_ci.py` deseasonalizes by monthly climatology **jointly** with the trend (Frisch–Waugh–Lovell), then OLS, lag-1 residual autocorrelation, `n_eff = n(1−r1)/(1+r1)` capped at `n`, and a two-sided 95% Student-t interval on `n_eff − 2` dof — the Santer et al. (2008) treatment. Your window is 312 months / 26 complete years, so `--deseasonalize climatology` is available.
