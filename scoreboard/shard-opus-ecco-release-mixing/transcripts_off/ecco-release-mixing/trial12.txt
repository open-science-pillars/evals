ECCO_L4_GEOMETRY_LLC0090GRID_V4R4       DOI 10.5067/ECL5A-GRD44   (merged, for native cell areas)

The trap in the phrasing: there is also `ECCO_L4_SSH_LLC0090GRID_MONTHLY_V4R4B` — a *re-release covering the same months*, with its own DOI (`10.5067/ECL5M-SSH4B`).
