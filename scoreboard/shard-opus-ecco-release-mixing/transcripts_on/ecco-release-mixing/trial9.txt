## 1. "Whatever collections cover the full period" is the trap

ECCO v4r4 spans exactly **1992-01 → 2017-12**, so your window is the product's entire record and *one* collection covers it end to end. No stitching is needed — and stitching is specifically dangerous here.

SSH is one of only two ECCO families (with OBP) that ships a corrected **V4R4B** re-release alongside the original **V4R4**:

| ShortName | DOI |
|---|---|
| `ECCO_L4_SSH_LLC0090GRID_MONTHLY_V4R4` | 10.5067/ECL5M-SSH44 |
| `ECCO_L4_SSH_LLC0090GRID_MONTHLY_V4R4B` | 10.5067/ECL5M-SSH4B |

Both claim the full period. Assembling a series from a mix of them folds a baseline geodetic correction into the signal — an apparent step or drift at the release boundary that is an artifact, not the ocean. Nothing errors; the ShortNames differ by one character (`knowledge/podaac/gotchas/ecco-release-mixing.md`, severity **high**, stable, human-verified 2026-09-04). So: one release throughout, named in the methods block.

Two further constraints from the same bundle:

- **Pick one SSH variant and state it.** `SSH` (IB-corrected), `SSHNOIBC`, `SSHIBC` and `ETAN` are distinct quantities, not spellings (`gotchas/ecco-ssh-ib-variants.md`, medium, stable). The sanctioned computation uses `SSH`.
- **Native llc90, not the 0.5° interpolation** — the `05DEG` collections are display-and-comparison only (`fields/ecco-v4r4/ssh.md`, stable, granule-verified 2026-08-30).

One divergence worth your attention: the release-mixing gotcha's "correct approach" says SSH/OBP work should use the corrected **V4R4B**, but the sanctioned executor pins **V4R4** for both fields (`ecco_regional_sea_level.py:86-88`). It doesn't *mix*, so it's internally consistent and attestable — but the provider concept's recommendation and the executor's pin disagree. That's a steward question, not something I'll paper over.

## 3. On "check whether there's a trend"

Whatever we run, the trend gets an effective-sample-size interval. Monthly ocean series have lag-1 autocorrelation typically 0.5–0.9; a textbook standard error is too small by ~√((1+r₁)/(1−r₁)), a factor of three at r₁=0.8 (`gotchas/ecco-trend-without-effective-n.md`, severity **high**, stable). The bundle's own record run shows the bite: r₁ = +0.893 leaves **17.6 effective months out of 312**, and the honest half-width is nearly five times the naive one. The climatology also gets removed *jointly* with the fit, not first.
