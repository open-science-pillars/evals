## A structural gap worth knowing before you unblock

Even with all three cleared, this plugin has **no sanctioned executor for a regional SSH time series**. Its attested computations are ocean heat content, geostrophic balance, wind-stress curl/Ekman, thermal-wind reconstruction, and `ecco-trend-ci`.

That matters because `ecco_trend_ci.py` is the sanctioned trend path, but it binds `source` to *a receipt from a sanctioned executor* — it inherits that receipt's run id, code hash, and data stamp. A series I computed myself and wrote to a JSON file would, in the skill's words, "attest FAIL by construction." So the trend half of your request can't be laundered through the attested path by feeding it a hand-built series.

- **Attested:** treat a North Atlantic SSH regional mean as a *new* attested computation — a new executor plus attester and a concept under `knowledge/computations/`. Correct and reusable, but it's a development task, not a run.

Faster, but it inherits none of the gotcha protection — and with the SSH-variant concept still unreachable, I'd be picking the SSH variable from memory, which is the weakest link in the whole chain.
