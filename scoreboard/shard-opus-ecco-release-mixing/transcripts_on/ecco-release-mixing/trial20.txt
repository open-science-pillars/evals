## What the trend answer would have to look like

Worth stating now, because it's where this analysis usually goes wrong. Two high-severity stable gotchas govern it, and the sanctioned method `ecco-trend-ci.md` closes both: the climatology is removed **jointly** with the trend, not first (sequential removal hands the climatology 143/(144Y²−1) of the slope — negligible at 26 years, a quarter of it at two), and the interval uses an effective sample size `n(1−r₁)/(1+r₁)`, capped at n. For reference, the record run over the US northeast coast gives total sea level +5.2452 mm/yr, interval [+4.0623, +6.4281], with r₁ +0.821 leaving just 30.7 effective months out of 312. A North Atlantic basin mean will be similarly autocorrelated — the naive interval would be roughly three times too narrow.
