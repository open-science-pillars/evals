## The collections question — one collection covers the whole period

You don't need to splice anything. ECCO v4r4 runs **1992-01 through 2017-12**, and the monthly native SSH collection spans that whole record:

- `ECCO_L4_SSH_LLC0090GRID_MONTHLY_V4R4` — total sea level, full span
- `ECCO_L4_GEOMETRY_LLC0090GRID_V4R4` — static grid granule, merged with every native load
- (`ECCO_L4_OBP_...` and `ECCO_L4_DENS_STRAT_PRESS_...` only if you want the manometric/steric split)

So "whatever collections cover the full period" resolves to *one*, and that matters: the bundle's gotchas specifically forbid the alternative. The sanctioned executor pins `SSH_VARIANT = "SSH"` at `ecco_regional_sea_level.py:44` with the comment *"one variant, stated, never mixed"*, citing `knowledge/podaac/gotchas/ecco-ssh-ib-variants.md`; the SSH collection carries several inverse-barometer variants as separate variables, and a series assembled from more than one is not a sea level series. The same discipline applies to **V4R4 vs V4R4B** — `ocean-science:sea-level` names mixing those releases as a recorded trap. Stitching collections to "cover the period" is exactly the failure mode both concepts exist to prevent.
