**The governing concept** is `knowledge/podaac/gotchas/grace-coastal-leakage.md` (status `stable`, severity `high`, human-verified by you on 2026-07-04, fresh until 2027-01-04). Its wrong-result mode names your exact case:

> *"apparent coastal sea level or ocean-mass trends near Greenland, Alaska, or major river basins can be dominated by leakage rather than ocean change. Nothing errors; the series just is not what its coordinates claim."*

### Why southeast Greenland is the worst case, not a marginal one

**The shelf is smaller than the instrument.** Per `datasets/grace-fo-mascons.md`, the solution is 4,551 equal-area 3° spherical caps — about 1.1×10⁵ km² each, ~335 km on a side. The SE Greenland shelf is roughly 50–150 km wide. It is never resolved. The 0.5° grid the product ships on will happily give you cells that look like shelf water, but the concept is explicit that "the native information scale is the mascon, not the grid cell." There is no mascon that *is* shelf water — every candidate is a coastline-straddling mascon, which is precisely the CRI-partitioned population where residual leakage is largest.

**The adjacent land signal is enormous.** A scaling argument, using the concept's own conversion (1 cm water-equivalent over 1 km² = 10⁻⁵ Gt) — treat these as illustrative, not product numbers, since no installed concept carries a SE Greenland rate:

| Leakage residual into the ocean mascon | Assumed neighbouring ice mascon loss | Spurious ocean-mass trend |
|---|---|---|
| 5% | −20 Gt/yr | ≈ −9 mm/yr w.e. |
| 1% | −20 Gt/yr | ≈ −1.8 mm/yr w.e. |

For scale, the bundle's own anchor for the *global* ocean mass term is **+2.299 mm/yr** over 2005–2016 (`ocean-science/knowledge/computations/sea-level-budget.md`). So even a 1% residual after CRI is the size of the entire real signal, and it enters with the wrong sign — ice loss next door reads as ocean mass *loss* on the shelf. The CRI filter "reduces leakage substantially but does not eliminate it." You would get a confident, large, negative trend that is mostly Helheim and Kangerdlugssuaq, not ocean.

Worth adding: the product's formal per-mascon uncertainty grids **would not warn you.** The dataset concept states the formal errors exclude leakage and GIA, "both exceed the formal errors regionally." The error bars would look reassuring and be irrelevant.

### One thing your window gets right

2019–2024 sits entirely after the GRACE/GRACE-FO gap (`gotchas/grace-intermission-gap.md`). No inter-mission bias, no bridging argument needed. That's the window the gap gotcha explicitly recommends — "prefer a window that does not cross it." Whatever we compute, that choice holds up.
