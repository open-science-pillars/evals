What the script does, in the order the concepts require:

1. **Subsets to your bbox first**, and stops with a clear message if the pass carries zero in-box pixels.
2. **Decodes `ssha_karin_qual` bitwise** via its `flag_masks`/`flag_meanings`, never by thresholding the packed integer (core `quality-control`, check 5). Strict gate: all bits clear.
3. **Gates `height_cor_xover` on `height_cor_xover_qual == 0`** (0 good / 1 suspect / 2 bad, per SWOT PDD D-56407 Rev C §4.1.8 as cited by the gotcha).
4. **Adds `height_cor_xover` to `ssha_karin`**, then aggregates — and labels the output as correction-applied.
5. Prints the raw-field mean/std alongside, explicitly marked as a diagnostic, not a result. On a typical scene these should differ by a lot; if they don't, that's worth a look.
6. Reports masked fraction with dominant flag reasons, and gives per-swath statistics with the nadir gap intact.

One calibration point on the masked fraction: the dataset concept records a loaded open-ocean pass at **39% valid `ssha_karin`** after flag and land gating, so heavy masking is normal rather than a defect.
