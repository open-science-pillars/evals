The script reports the corrected *and* uncorrected statistics side by side, so you'll see the ramp directly — if the raw span is order-metres and the corrected std collapses to order-decimetres, that's the gotcha confirmed on your scene.

Three things worth knowing when the numbers come back:

- **Expect ~40% valid.** A loaded open-ocean pass showed 39% valid `ssha_karin` after flag and land gating (`datasets/swot-karin.md`, granule-verified 2026-07-04). A heavy masked fraction is normal, not a defect.
- **Don't put a standard error on your mean as σ/√n.** `ssh_karin_uncert` is per-sample *random* noise only; it excludes the correlated swath-scale systematics (roll, phase, timing, wet-troposphere residuals) that dominate at long cross-track wavelengths. Swath pixels are strongly correlated, so √n badly overstates your precision.
- **Unweighted mean is correct here** — the ~2 km swath posting is near-uniform in area, unlike a lat-lon grid.
