## Flag gating alone will give you a confidently wrong answer

Your plan — mask on the quality flags, then take mean and std of `ssha_karin` — is necessary but not sufficient on this product.

In SWOT KaRIn L2 LR SSH, **the crossover calibration arrives unapplied** in `ssha_karin` / `ssh_karin`. The granule metadata and the Product Description both instruct the user to add `height_cor_xover` themselves (`knowledge/podaac/gotchas/swot-crossover-unapplied.md`, severity high, status stable, human-verified 2026-09-04).

What happens if you skip it: the uncorrected field carries a spurious roll/phase systematic that shows up as a linear **cross-track ramp of order meters** — a span of about ±2.9 m observed on a PGD0 Expert-tier granule, cycle 011. The same scene with the correction applied had std 0.17 m of physically sensible mesoscale structure. So a flag-gated mean and std over your region, computed on the raw field, would be off by an amount that dwarfs the signal — and nothing about the result would look wrong.

The correct sequence is:

1. Gate pixels on `ssha_karin_qual`.
2. Gate `height_cor_xover` on `height_cor_xover_qual` (0 good, 1 suspect, 2 bad).
3. **Add `height_cor_xover` to `ssha_karin`**, then subset to your region, then aggregate.
4. Report the masked fraction. A valid fraction around 39% after flag and land gating is normal on an open-ocean pass, not a defect.

`height_cor_xover` is present in the Basic tier as well as Expert, so this works whichever tier your granule is.

Two further constraints I'll apply once we compute:

- **Subset before you aggregate.** CMR spatial search matches whole pole-to-pole passes, so a matched granule can carry zero pixels in your box. Trim to the region first.
- **Per-swath, gap intact.** The two 50 km swaths have independent error profiles and are separated by a ~20 km nadir gap. I won't interpolate across it, and I'd suggest reporting the swaths separately as well as pooled.

One caveat on the std you'll get: `ssh_karin_uncert` is per-sample *random* noise only. It excludes the correlated swath-scale systematics (roll, phase, timing, wet troposphere residuals), so don't read it as a total error budget on a regional average.
