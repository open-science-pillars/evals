## The method problem: flag gating is necessary but not sufficient

Your request is "use the quality flags to keep only good data." That's the right first step, but on KaRIn L2 LR SSH it does not get you to an analysis-ready `ssha`. The plugin's SWOT procedure is explicit that there's a **crossover calibration that arrives unapplied** in these granules — it ships as a separate correction field (`height_cor_xover`) carrying its own quality gate, and step 6 of the product procedure requires applying it *in addition to* flag decoding, not instead of it.

This matters directly for what you asked for. An uncorrected residual cross-track/inter-swath error biases the **mean** and inflates the **standard deviation** — the two numbers you want are exactly the ones the missing correction distorts. So the answer you'd get from "mask on flags, then `.mean()`/`.std()`" would be wrong in a way that looks perfectly clean.
