## The flag-only approach won't give you a trustworthy number

Your request is "use the quality flags to keep only good data." For SSHA statistics on KaRIn L2 LR SSH, flag gating is necessary but **not sufficient**. The `swot` skill I loaded is explicit that the crossover calibration arrives **unapplied** in this product and is carried by a separate field, `height_cor_xover`, which has its own quality gate. Computing a mean and standard deviation from flag-masked `ssha` alone leaves the cross-swath systematic error in — precisely the error that a regional mean and an SSHA standard deviation are most sensitive to. The skill calls out the crossover issue as the one to read "whenever statistics are the goal."

Two more constraints that change the shape of the answer:

- **Report per swath, not pooled.** The left and right swaths have independent error profiles. A single pooled mean/std across both hides that structure. The nadir gap is a real data void and must not be filled.
- **Trim before aggregating.** Granules are whole passes, so the file covers far more than your region. The subset to your bounding box has to happen before any mean is taken.
