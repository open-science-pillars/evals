## The bigger issue: quality flags alone won't get you a trustworthy mean

This is the part worth reading before you hand me the file. Gating on the bit-packed quality flags is **necessary but not sufficient** for SWOT KaRIn L2 LR SSH statistics.

The KaRIn L2 SSH products ship with the **crossover calibration unapplied**. `ssha_karin` as it sits in the granule still carries the residual cross-track/roll error that the crossover calibration is there to remove. If you flag-mask and then take `mean()` and `std()` directly off that field, you get a regional mean biased by an uncorrected swath-scale tilt and an inflated standard deviation — and neither error announces itself, because every pixel you used passed its quality flag.

The `ocean-science:swot` skill names `height_cor_xover` as the correction field, and the correction has its own quality gate that must be applied alongside it (`skills/swot/SKILL.md:108`, and the procedure at `skills/swot/references/swot-products.md:46`).
