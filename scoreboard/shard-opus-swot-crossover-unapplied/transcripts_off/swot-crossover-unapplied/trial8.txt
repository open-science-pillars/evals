## The thing worth flagging before you compute

**Flag gating alone will not give you a correct mean.** The KaRIn L2 LR SSH `ssha` field ships with the **crossover calibration not applied** — it arrives as a separate `height_cor_xover` field that you apply yourself, with its own quality gate.
