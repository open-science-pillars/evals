## 2. Quality flags alone won't make that mean trustworthy

That bears directly on what you asked for. The crossover calibration is what removes the long-wavelength, cross-track-dependent residual (roll/phase) error. Left unapplied it behaves largely as a per-pass, cross-track-structured bias — which is precisely the error that shifts a **regional mean** and inflates a **standard deviation**. A flag-clean mean off raw `ssha_karin` is a flag-clean mean of a biased field.
