## The part of your request I'd push back on

You asked for mean and std "using the quality flags to keep only good data." Flag gating is necessary but **not sufficient** for this particular statistic. The `swot` skill records that the crossover calibration arrives **unapplied**, carried by `height_cor_xover` with its own quality gate, and that this issue fires specifically *whenever statistics are the goal* — because uncorrected cross-track/crossover error is a systematic offset, exactly the thing that biases a regional **mean** and inflates a **standard deviation**. A flag-gated mean of raw `ssha` is a clean-looking number that is wrong in a way the flags cannot tell you about.

So the answer will be `ssha − height_cor_xover`, gated on both the SSHA quality flag and the crossover quality flag. The exact gate threshold is what I'm missing from the quarantined gotcha — the script takes it as a parameter rather than assuming it.
