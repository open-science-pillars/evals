## The trap: flag gating alone won't give you a trustworthy mean

SWOT KaRIn L2 LR SSH ships the **crossover calibration unapplied**. `ssha_karin` on its own still carries the residual long-wavelength and cross-swath error that the calibration removes. Those errors are large-scale and largely systematic across a swath, so they hit exactly the two numbers you asked for: they bias the regional **mean** and inflate the **standard deviation**. Quality flags don't touch this — a pixel can be perfectly flagged-good and still uncalibrated.

So the correct field is `ssha_karin + height_cor_xover`, with the crossover correction gated on its own quality variable so uncalibrated pixels get dropped rather than silently zero-filled. This is recorded in the plugin's `knowledge/podaac/gotchas/swot-crossover-unapplied.md` concept — see the caveat on that below.

Two other rules I'll hold to: the flag word gets decoded bitwise, never thresholded as a number, and the two swaths stay separate with the nadir gap intact (no interpolation across it — it's a real data void).
