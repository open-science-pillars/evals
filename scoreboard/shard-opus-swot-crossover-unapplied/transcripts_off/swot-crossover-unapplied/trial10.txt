## Flag-gating is necessary but not sufficient

The `ocean-science` SWOT skill is explicit that flag gating "is necessary but not sufficient" (`skills/swot/SKILL.md:72`), and names the reason: the **crossover calibration arrives unapplied** in the product, with `height_cor_xover` as the fix (`SKILL.md:106-108`). The `load-swot` workflow makes it a step, not an option — ssha statistics over good pixels are to be computed *with* the crossover correction applied, "stating explicitly that the correction was applied" (`skills/load-swot/SKILL.md`, step 6).

This bites exactly your use case. A regional mean SSHA is a low-order statistic over a swath, which is precisely where an uncorrected cross-track/inter-pass bias lands. Flag-gating removes bad pixels; it does nothing about a systematic offset in the good ones. Your standard deviation is less exposed, your mean is very exposed.
