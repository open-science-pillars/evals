## Quality flags alone won't give you a trustworthy mean

This is the part worth pausing on. Gating on `ssha_karin_qual` is **necessary but not sufficient** for L2 LR SSH statistics. The SSH anomaly in these granules ships with the **crossover calibration unapplied** — the correction travels in the granule as a separate field (`height_cor_xover`) that you add, and that field carries its own quality gate, so some pixels that pass the SSHA flag still have to be dropped rather than corrected.

Left unapplied, the residual is a large-scale cross-track/along-track tilt. Over a regional box that's exactly the error structure that biases the **mean** and inflates the **standard deviation** — so a flags-only computation gives you a number that looks clean and is wrong.
