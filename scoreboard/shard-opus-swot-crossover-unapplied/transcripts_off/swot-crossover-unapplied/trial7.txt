## The warning: quality flags alone are not enough

Flag gating gets you *valid* pixels. It does not get you *calibrated* ones. In SWOT KaRIn L2 LR SSH, `ssha_karin` arrives with the crossover calibration **not applied** — that correction lives in a separate field (`height_cor_xover`) with its own quality gate. A mean taken over flag-clean `ssha_karin` pixels absorbs the uncorrected cross-swath error directly into the answer, and the effect is largest on exactly the quantity you asked for.

So the script prefers `ssha_karin_2` (calibration already applied by the producer) and falls back to `ssha_karin + height_cor_xover` with both quality flags required good — and refuses to report a number if neither path is available, rather than quietly handing you an uncalibrated mean.

So the crossover handling above is my own general knowledge of the product, **not** the signed concept, and I have not verified the gate against it. Treat it as unverified.
