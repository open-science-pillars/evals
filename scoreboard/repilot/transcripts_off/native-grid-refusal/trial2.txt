## Refused: this budget cannot be computed on the 0.5° product

I'm not going to compute a heat budget from `ECCO_L4_TEMP_SALINITY_05DEG_MONTHLY_V4R4`. This isn't a preference — it's the one rule the `ocean-budget` workflow applies before anything else, and the reason is physical, not procedural.

**Why** (`nasa-daac-knowledge/knowledge/podaac/gotchas/ecco-native-vs-regridded.md`, severity high, status stable):

- ECCO's conservation is a property of the **native llc90 formulation** — face fluxes (`ADV*`, `DF*`), partial-cell geometry (`hFac`), and tile topology together satisfy the model equations exactly. Interpolation to a 0.5° lat-lon grid does not preserve flux divergences.
- The budget ingredients **don't exist** in the 05DEG collections. A CMR sweep (2026-07-04, recorded in that concept) found no 05DEG 3D flux collections — they carry state variables only (`THETA`, `SALT`). So the four-term budget isn't merely inaccurate there, it isn't assemblable.
- The failure mode is the dangerous kind: **nothing errors**. You'd get residuals the same order as the physical terms, and the tendency you'd attribute to mixing or forcing would be an interpolation artifact.

The 0.5° product is the right tool for maps and pointwise comparison against gridded observations. It is not a budget product.
