## The budget can't be computed from that product — and this is a hard rule, not a preference

`ECCO_L4_TEMP_SALINITY_05DEG_MONTHLY_V4R4` is the 0.5° interpolated grid. The `ocean-budget` workflow refuses budgets on regridded fields before any computation, under gotcha **`ecco-native-vs-regridded`** (cited by the skill; the concept file itself lives in the installed bundle outside this session's readable root, so I'm citing it by id rather than quoting it).

The physical reason, not just the policy one: ECCO's budget terms are defined on the Arakawa C-grid as fluxes through cell faces, with the volume element `rA * drF * hFacC`. Interpolation to 0.5° destroys the discrete divergence — the face fluxes no longer telescope against the cell volumes they bounded, and the partial-cell `hFac` scaling is gone. There is no formulation on that grid that closes; any residual you'd get would be an interpolation artifact you'd then be tempted to call "mixing." Regridding also breaks the terms in a way an absolute residual bar alone won't catch, which is exactly the failure mode the regional contract's second (relative) bar exists for.

Also worth knowing: I looked for your files and found no 0.5° granules under `/home/user` — only an eval fixture, `agent-evals/ecco/fixtures/native-grid/ecco_05deg_stub.nc`. Nor is there a native tree (`~/ECCO_V4r4` / `~/ECCO_V4r4_record`). So the inputs check would have stopped this regardless of grid.

## The native path for a subpolar North Atlantic heat budget

The attested route is `ecco-regional-heat-budget` (`knowledge/computations/ecco-regional-heat-budget.md`, status stable), which closes over a control volume by comparing **three independent collections** so agreement is evidence rather than arithmetic. Collections to fetch (LLC90 native, all V4R4):

| Term | Collection |
|---|---|
| Geometry | `ECCO_L4_GEOMETRY_LLC0090GRID_V4R4` |
| Tendency (monthly + bookend snapshots) | `ECCO_L4_TEMP_SALINITY_LLC0090GRID_MONTHLY_V4R4`, `ECCO_L4_TEMP_SALINITY_LLC0090GRID_SNAPSHOT_V4R4`, `ECCO_L4_SSH_LLC0090GRID_SNAPSHOT_V4R4` |
| Transport (raw face fluxes) | `ECCO_L4_OCEAN_3D_TEMPERATURE_FLUX_LLC0090GRID_MONTHLY_V4R4` |
| Forcing | `ECCO_L4_HEAT_FLUX_LLC0090GRID_MONTHLY_V4R4` |

A year needs 12 monthly means bracketed by 13 month-boundary snapshots. After the first fetch the tree must be stamped with `tools/science_record_verify.py` — every attester refuses a receipt from an unstamped tree.

Two things specific to *your* region that I want flagged before you spend the download:

1. **Tile seam.** The v1 contract states the control volume must lie within one tile interior; seam-crossing volumes are refused. The subpolar gyre (~50–65°N, 60–10°W) sits where the LLC90 Atlantic faces meet the Arctic cap, so it may well cross a seam. I'd resolve the mask first and read the disclosed index bounds before assuming a box works.
2. **Geothermal flux.** This is a deep-reaching budget, and the geothermal term at bottom wet cells is the classic silent omission — measured at 1.24e-12 °C/s, *inside* the absolute bar, caught only by the relative bar (`knowledge/podaac/gotchas/ecco-geothermal-flux.md`). If you subset to an upper layer instead, the executor records geothermal as not-applicable with the bottom-cell count.

Your region is also not the registered region (`southeast-atlantic-upper`), so it runs on the explicit-box tier: disclosed by mask digest, no reference anchor. That's supported, just unanchored.
