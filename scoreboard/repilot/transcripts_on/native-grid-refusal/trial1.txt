## Refused: budgets do not close on the regridded product

`ecco-native-vs-regridded` (`nasa-daac-knowledge/knowledge/podaac/gotchas/ecco-native-vs-regridded.md`, severity high, stable) is explicit, and it names your exact collection as its example:

- ECCO's conservation is a property of the **native** formulation — face fluxes (`ADV*`, `DF*`), partial-cell geometry (`hFac`), tile topology. Interpolation to 0.5° does not preserve flux divergences.
- The budget ingredients **do not exist** in the `05DEG` collections. A CMR sweep (2026-07-04) found no 0.5° 3D flux collections; the `05DEG` product carries state variables only. The four-term budget is not assemblable from what you downloaded, at any level of effort.
- Wrong-result mode, which is why this is a refusal and not a caveat: nothing errors. You get residuals the same order as the physical terms, and "mixing" or "forcing" terms that are interpolation artifacts.

Your download is the right product for maps, sections, and pointwise comparison against gridded observations. It is the wrong product for a budget.
