Three findings, in order of severity:

**1. The shelf is sub-mascon.** The JPL solution is 4,551 equal-area 3° spherical caps (~300 km). It is *distributed* on a 0.5° grid, but the dataset concept is explicit that "the native information scale is the mascon, not the grid cell," and its Uncertainty section states that a region whose area approaches or falls below the mascon scale "is resolution- and leakage-dominated." The SE Greenland shelf is ~100–200 km wide. The grid will hand you shelf-shaped cells that carry no independent information. The recipe requires regions be built from *whole mascons* taken from the placement file — and there is no whole-mascon set that is "shelf and not ice sheet."

**2. Leakage would dominate, with the same sign as the signal you'd want.** The coastal-leakage gotcha names this case directly: "apparent coastal sea level or ocean-mass trends **near Greenland** ... can be dominated by leakage rather than ocean change. Nothing errors; the series just is not what its coordinates claim." Every mascon over that shelf straddles the coastline, and its land part contains Helheim/Kangerdlugssuaq — one of the ice sheet's largest dynamic-loss sectors. CRI mitigates, never eliminates.

The trap is sharper than usual here. There *is* a real local ocean-mass decrease near a melting ice sheet (gravitational self-attraction and loading — the sea-level fingerprint makes bottom pressure fall within ~2000 km of Greenland). It has the **same sign** as the leakage error and is roughly an order of magnitude smaller. So a number would look physically plausible and be mostly artifact. That is the worst kind of wrong.

**3. A fix landed on exactly this quantity.** Per the release note via the recipe: a July 2025 correction fixed "the GAD mass added back to the **ocean part of land/ocean mascons**," and only files whose series extends past March 2025 carry it. That is precisely the quantity you asked for, so the file's last month has to be named alongside the version.

Your window is at least clean on one axis: 2019–2024 sits entirely inside GRACE-FO and does not cross the 2017–2018 gap. But RL06.3M v4's accelerometer-transplant bundle touches Jan–Feb 2023 and Jul 2023 onward — about a third of the window — and six years is short against interannual variability in that region.
