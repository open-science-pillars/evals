## The headline

**ECCO's 2010 Atlantic MHT at 26.5N is 0.666 PW, against RAPID/MOCHA's 1.33 ± 0.40 PW — ECCO runs about 0.66 PW low, roughly half the observed transport.**

## What's behind it

| | Value | Scope |
|---|---|---|
| ECCO v4r4, 2010 | **0.666 PW** | Atlantic, `basin_name="atlExt"` — the RAPID section |
| ECCO v4r4, 2010 | 1.098 PW | **global circle** — *not* RAPID-comparable |
| RAPID/MOCHA | 1.33 ± 0.40 PW | Atlantic, 2004–2007 (Johns et al. 2011) |

The global-circle number decomposes exactly as atlExt 0.666 + pacExt 0.430 + indExt 0.002 = 1.098, which is the recipe's method-reproducibility check — and the reason `ecco-mht-basin-scope` (severity: high) exists: *"Quoting 1.098 PW as 'the 26.5N heat transport' against a ~1.3 PW RAPID mean invents a disagreement in one direction and hides the real Atlantic offset in the other."* Using the global number would have made the gap look like −0.23 PW instead of −0.66 PW.

## Four caveats the number doesn't carry on its own

- **Periods don't match.** RAPID's 1.33 PW is a 2004–2007 mean; yours is calendar 2010. The recipe's discipline is explicit: *"compare like periods ... and state the averaging window."*
- **2010 is the AMOC-minimum year.** The recipe flags 0.666 PW as deliberately below its own 0.8–1.4 PW multi-year band, *"consistent with the documented 2009/2010 AMOC minimum."* Some of the 0.66 PW gap is the year, not the model.
- **±0.40 PW is a temporal standard deviation, not an error bar.** Atlantic monthly values in 2010 span +0.09 to +1.14 PW. The offset is outside one σ, but σ here measures variability.
- **A low bias is expected.** *"ECCO v4 Atlantic means sit low of the observed mean, so model-minus-RAPID offsets are expected, not anomalous."*
