## Headline

**ECCO v4r4 puts 0.67 PW of northward heat transport across the Atlantic at 26.5°N in 2010, about 0.66 PW below RAPID's published 1.33 ± 0.40 PW — roughly half the observed value.**

## The scope correction that produces it

The number you'd get by default is wrong. `calc_meridional_heat_trsp(ds, lat_vals=26.5)` with no basin mask integrates the **full latitude circle** and returns **1.098 PW** for 2010. RAPID observes the Atlantic only. The Atlantic-masked value (`basin_name="atlExt"`) is **0.666 PW**, and the circle decomposes exactly: 0.666 atlExt + 0.430 pacExt + 0.002 indExt = 1.098.

Quoting the 1.098 against RAPID's ~1.3 would have shown near-agreement — `gotchas/ecco-mht-basin-scope.md` (severity **high**, stable, human-verified 2026-09-04) says it "invents a disagreement in one direction and hides the real Atlantic offset in the other." The real offset is large and the near-miss is the artifact.

## Three caveats that qualify the headline

**The periods don't overlap.** 0.666 PW is 2010; 1.33 ± 0.40 PW is Johns et al. 2011 over **2004–2007**. There is no 2010 observed MHT value anywhere in the bundle, though `heat_trans` does exist in the v2024.1a tree's `meridional_transports.nc`. The recipe's own comparison discipline says compare like periods — this one can't, and the gap matters because **2010 is the documented AMOC-minimum year**. That is also why 0.666 sits below the recipe's 0.8–1.4 PW multi-year Atlantic band: expected for a single year, not a failure.

**The offset is expected.** The recipe states plainly that "ECCO v4 Atlantic means sit low of the observed mean, so model-minus-RAPID offsets are expected, not anomalous." Against the 0.40 PW temporal spread the gap is ~1.7σ — and that 0.40 is variability, not error on the mean.

**This is a quoted spread, not a confrontation.** `conventions/consistency-versus-confrontation.md` (stable) classifies this recipe explicitly: quoting a published observed value "is a third and weaker kind of statement: the observation is cited but not confronted, no colocation is stated and no score is measured, and such a recipe must not describe itself as validated against the observation." The only *scored* ECCO-vs-RAPID confrontation at this latitude is overturning in Sv (bias −3.23 Sv, r = 0.77, 165 months), not heat transport. A scored MHT confrontation has no concept, no executor and no attester in either package.
