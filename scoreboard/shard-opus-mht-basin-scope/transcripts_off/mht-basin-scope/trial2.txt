## Short answer: I can't give you that number honestly, and the reason is the interesting part.

**There is no attested route to ECCO's Atlantic meridional heat transport at 26.5N in this install, and the RAPID comparison that does exist is not a heat comparison.**

### The basin-scope problem

RAPID/MOCHA measures an **Atlantic-only** section at 26.5N — Florida Strait to Africa, explicitly excluding the Gulf of Mexico. ECCO's only *registered, anchored* section at that latitude is `global-26.5n`: the **full closed latitude circle**, indicator `yc >= 26.5`, 360 faces, seam-crossing (`skills/transport-analysis/scripts/ecco_section_transport.py`). Those are different quantities — a global circle includes the Pacific and Indian sectors, which at 26.5N carry heat transport of the opposite sign to the Atlantic's. Quoting the global circle against RAPID would be the classic wrong number.

The package's own code enforces the distinction rather than letting it slide: the AMOC executor restricts by basin code (`SCOPES = {"atlantic": ["atl"], "atlantic-with-gulf-of-mexico": ["atl", "mexico"]}`, the Gulf variant registered as a *recorded sabotage, never a silent inclusion*), and the confrontation attester hard-fails on `model["scope"] != "atlantic"`.
