## There is no single headline number I can honestly give you

The blocker is scope, and it's not a technicality.

**What ECCO's attested 26.5N heat route actually measures.** The sanctioned executor (`skills/transport-analysis/scripts/ecco_section_transport.py`) registers exactly two sections. The one at 26.5N is `global-26.5n` — and it is literally built as `YC >= 26.5` across all 13 llc90 tiles (`ecco_section_transport.py:92`, `:146`), i.e. the **closed global latitude circle**: Atlantic + Pacific + Indian.

**What RAPID measures.** The Atlantic only, Florida to Africa. So the attested ECCO heat number at 26.5N and RAPID's MHT are not the same quantity, and differencing them would be measuring the Indo-Pacific, not model skill.
