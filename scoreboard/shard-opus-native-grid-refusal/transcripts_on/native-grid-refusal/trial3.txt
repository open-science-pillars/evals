## Refusal: no heat budget on regridded ECCO fields

`ECCO_L4_TEMP_SALINITY_05DEG_MONTHLY_V4R4` is the interpolated product. A budget request on regridded fields is refused outright — it's the one rule in this plugin that fires before any consultation, owned by the `ecco` skill and enforced at the `ocean-budget` gate, citing gotcha `knowledge/podaac/gotchas/ecco-native-vs-regridded.md` ("budgets close only on the native llc90 grid").

Two independent reasons, both fatal:

1. **No correct formulation exists there.** ECCO's budget closes because the archived terms are the model's own flux-form quantities on the Arakawa C-grid: face-normal advective and diffusive fluxes whose discrete divergence exactly balances the tendency, cell by cell. Interpolation to a 0.5° lat-lon mesh breaks that discrete identity. Whatever you compute afterwards has a residual set by the regridding error, not by physics, and there is no way to tell the two apart.
2. **Three of the four terms aren't in the file.** That collection carries THETA and SALT monthly means. It has no `ADVx_TH`/`ADVy_TH`/`ADVr_TH`, no `DFxE_TH`/`DFyE_TH`/`DFrE_TH`/`DFrI_TH`, no surface forcing (`TFLUX`, `oceQsw`), and no month-boundary snapshots for the tendency. You could form a finite-difference tendency and nothing else — that's a heat *content change*, not a budget.

I won't produce a four-term table with the residual folded into a diffusion or forcing term; that's the failure mode this rule exists to prevent.

## The native path

The budget you want has an attested computation: `knowledge/computations/ecco-heat-budget.md` (status `stable`, human-verified by the maintainer 2026-09-20). Its pass bar is pointwise and absolute — `residual_max` ≤ 1e-10 °C/s, `residual_p999` ≤ 1e-11 °C/s, every wet cell, every month. Baseline measurement (2010, `tile1-interior`, 3,341,772 cell-months): max 4.95e-11, p99.9 7.3e-12, median 5.7e-14 °C/s. That's machine-precision closure, and it's the standard your subpolar result should be held to.

For a **subpolar North Atlantic control volume specifically**, the whole-grid computation won't do it — it binds `region` with `tile1-interior` as the only registered value. You want the regional executor, which takes an explicit box:

```bash
R=/root/.claude/plugins/cache/open-science-pillars/ocean-science/0.9.0
uv run $R/skills/ocean-budget/scripts/ecco_regional_budget.py \
  --budget heat --box 50 65 -60 -10 --depth-m 1000 --year 2010 \
  --data-root ~/ECCO_V4r4 --receipt /tmp/spna-heat-receipt.json
uv run $R/skills/ocean-budget/scripts/regional_budget_check.py /tmp/spna-heat-receipt.json
```

Three things you should know before that run:

- **An explicit box carries no anchor.** The attester's two-sided checks on wet-cell count, volume and residual-per-volume are calibrated only on the reference configuration (`southeast-atlantic-upper`, 2010). A custom subpolar box is disclosed by mask digest and the report has to say it's unanchored.
- **The subpolar gyre is an open domain.** Domain-integrated closure is asserted only on closed domains; here the boundary transports through 50°N, 65°N and the box's east/west faces travel in the result explicitly, not absorbed.
- **Geothermal.** If you want full depth rather than 0–1000 m, the geothermal bottom flux is a required term and its input is *not* a PO.DAAC collection (`knowledge/podaac/gotchas/ecco-geothermal-flux.md`). Dropping it drives deep-cell residuals orders of magnitude above tolerance.
