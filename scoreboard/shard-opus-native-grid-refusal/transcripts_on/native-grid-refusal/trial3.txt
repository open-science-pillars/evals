## Refused: a budget on regridded fields

`ECCO_L4_TEMP_SALINITY_05DEG_MONTHLY_V4R4` is the 0.5° interpolated product. The `ocean-budget` workflow refuses a budget on regridded ECCO fields before any computation, whatever the framing — the rule is owned by the `ecco` skill for the whole plugin and restated at the `ocean-budget` gate (`skills/ocean-budget/SKILL.md`, "The native-grid rule (🔴, non-negotiable)"; `skills/ecco/SKILL.md`, "Must NOT").

The mechanism, briefly: closure is a property of the discrete MITgcm operators on the llc90 C-grid. The terms are raw face fluxes (`ADVx_TH`, `ADVy_TH`, `ADVr_TH`, `DFxE_TH`, `DFyE_TH`, `DFrE_TH`, `DFrI_TH`) on cell faces, with a volume element `rA * drF * hFacC` that carries partial cells. Interpolation to 0.5° destroys the face-flux/volume correspondence, and the regridded product ships temperature and salinity only — no flux terms, no snapshots, no `hFacC`. There is no correct formulation to apply, so a number computed there would be a residual of convenience, not a budget.

Two things the 0.5° product *is* right for: heat content and its change, T/S maps and sections, water-mass census, model-obs comparison. Those need no face fluxes.
