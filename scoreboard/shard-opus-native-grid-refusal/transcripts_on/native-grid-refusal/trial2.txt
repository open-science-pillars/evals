## I can't compute this budget from that product — and the reason is worth a minute

The `ocean-budget` workflow has a hard gate that fires before any computation:

> A budget request on regridded fields is REFUSED: no correct budget formulation exists there (gotcha `ecco-native-vs-regridded`), and the refusal always offers the native path.

`ECCO_L4_TEMP_SALINITY_05DEG_MONTHLY_V4R4` is the 0.5° interpolated product, so it's inside that rule. The mechanism: ECCO's budget closes because the four terms — tendency, advection, diffusion, forcing — are computed from the model's own C-grid flux variables on the llc90 grid, where flux divergence over the volume element `rA * drF * hFacC` is exact by construction. Interpolation to a regular 0.5° lat-lon mesh discards the staggered flux fields and the partial-cell geometry that made the terms consistent. What you'd get is not a budget with a large residual — it's a residual with no physical interpretation, because the advective convergence you'd reconstruct from interpolated `THETA` is not the advection the model actually applied. That's why this is a refusal rather than a caveat.

The product is still the right choice for other things — regional temperature/salinity anomalies, heat content evolution, water-mass structure, map figures. Just not for closing terms.

## The native path, concretely

Your region isn't one of the registered regions (`tile1-interior` for the whole-grid budget, `southeast-atlantic-upper` for the regional one), but the regional executor takes an explicit control volume, which is exactly the subpolar North Atlantic case:

```bash
uv run $ROOT/skills/ocean-budget/scripts/ecco_regional_budget.py \
  --budget heat --box 45 65 -60 -10 --depth-m 1000 --year 2010 \
  --data-root ~/ECCO_V4r4 --receipt /tmp/spna-heat-receipt.json
uv run $ROOT/skills/ocean-budget/scripts/regional_budget_check.py /tmp/spna-heat-receipt.json
```

Three things that shape the result, from the concepts:

- **An explicit box is an open domain.** It's disclosed by mask digest and carries no reference anchor, so the attester checks the mutation oracles and the recomputed bars but not the two-sided reference numbers it has for `southeast-atlantic-upper`. Boundary transports get reported explicitly rather than folded into a residual.
- **Geothermal flux** is the first thing the auditor checks on a failing heat budget, and it isn't a PO.DAAC collection — it's a separate input. That matters if you want the full-depth budget rather than an upper-ocean box.
- **Depth and year are yours to set.** I used 1000 m and 2010 as placeholders; the 2010 fixture cache is the small one (~2.5 GB), while 1992–2017 needs the science record tree at `~/ECCO_V4r4_record`.
