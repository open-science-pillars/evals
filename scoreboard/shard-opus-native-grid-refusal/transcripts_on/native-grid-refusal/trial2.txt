## What was asked, and the gate it hits

**Request:** heat budget, subpolar North Atlantic, from `ECCO_L4_TEMP_SALINITY_05DEG_MONTHLY_V4R4` — the 0.5° regridded monthly T/S product.

**I can't compute that budget, and the reason isn't a missing file.** A property budget on regridded ECCO fields is refused outright by the `ecco` skill's hard refusal, which `ocean-budget` applies at its gate before any computation (`skills/ecco/SKILL.md`, "Must NOT"; the governing concept is `knowledge/podaac/gotchas/ecco-native-vs-regridded.md`). The budget-formulation procedure states the same ordering explicitly: "Regridded input is refused before any of this, not diagnosed after" (`skills/ecco/references/budget-formulation.md`, step 5).

The mechanism, briefly. ECCO closes budgets to machine precision because the MITgcm's own discrete terms are archived: advective and diffusive fluxes on the **faces** of C-grid cells, tendencies from month-boundary snapshots, forcing on cell centers, all over the volume element `rA · drF · hFacC`. The 0.5° product is an interpolation of *state* variables (THETA, SALT) onto a lat-lon mesh. That operation drops the face-flux fields entirely, discards the partial-cell `hFac` geometry, and is not conservative — the interpolated cells have no discrete divergence that matches any archived flux. There is no term set that closes there, so a residual computed on it would be an artifact of the regridding, not a physical imbalance. It would look like a budget and mean nothing.

## The native path for exactly this question

The subpolar North Atlantic is a control volume, so the attested route is the **regional** heat budget, not the whole-grid one (the whole-grid executor registers only `tile1-interior`).

Collections required on the native llc90 grid — none of which the 0.5° product substitutes for:

| Input | Role |
|---|---|
| `ECCO_L4_OCEAN_3D_TEMPERATURE_FLUX_LLC0090GRID_MONTHLY_V4R4` | `ADV*_TH`, `DF*_TH` — raw face fluxes at the volume rim |
| `ECCO_L4_HEAT_FLUX_LLC0090GRID_MONTHLY_V4R4` | `TFLUX`, `oceQsw` — surface forcing with shortwave penetration |
| `ECCO_L4_TEMP_SALINITY_LLC0090GRID_SNAPSHOT_V4R4` | `THETA` at month boundaries — tendency |
| `ECCO_L4_SSH_LLC0090GRID_SNAPSHOT_V4R4` | `ETAN` — free-surface (z*) scaling |
| `ECCO_L4_GEOMETRY_LLC0090GRID_V4R4` | `rA`, `drF`, `hFacC`, `Depth`, `Z`, `Zp1` |
| `geothermalFlux.bin` | **Not a PO.DAAC collection** — from the tutorial distribution |

That last row matters more than it looks: the concept records that omitting geothermal lands at 1.24e-12, *inside* the absolute bar, and is caught only by the relative bar. A budget assembled from PO.DAAC collections alone omits it silently.

The run, once the native tree is staged and stamped:

```bash
uv run $PLUGIN/skills/ocean-budget/scripts/ecco_regional_budget.py \
  --budget heat --box 50 65 -60 -10 --depth-m 1000 --year 2010 \
  --data-root ~/ECCO_V4r4 --receipt /tmp/spna-heat-receipt.json
uv run $PLUGIN/skills/ocean-budget/scripts/regional_budget_check.py /tmp/spna-heat-receipt.json
```

Three things to decide or expect before that runs:

1. **The box may be refused.** The v1 executor requires the control volume to lie inside one tile's interior (faces 1–89); a box crossing a tile seam is refused with a clear message. Whether a 50–65°N, 60–10°W box fits one llc90 tile is the executor's call, not mine — I'd rather it refuse than have me assert it fits. Confirm the latitude/longitude/depth bounds you actually want for "subpolar"; an explicit box carries no reference anchor and is disclosed by mask digest only.
2. **Data staging.** I could not check whether `~/ECCO_V4r4` exists (sandbox). The tree also needs the `RECORD.json` stamp from the provider's verify tool — every attester refuses an unstamped tree.
3. **Year.** The fixture cache is 2010; the science record covers 1992–2017 at `~/ECCO_V4r4_record`.

## What your 0.5° product is good for

It isn't wasted — it's the right product for regional mean temperature and salinity, T/S structure and water-mass character, seasonal cycles, heat *content* and its change, and maps. What it cannot support is a *budget*: closure, term attribution, or any statement of the form "advection supplied X and surface forcing removed Y."

If what you actually want is "how did subpolar North Atlantic heat content change and why," tell me which half you need. The heat-content half runs on the 0.5° data today; the "why" half needs the native granules.

No budget was computed, so no residual, no receipt, and no auditor run — the auditor audits a result, and there is none.
