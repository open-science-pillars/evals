## The refusal

A heat budget on `ECCO_L4_TEMP_SALINITY_05DEG_MONTHLY_V4R4` is refused because that's a regridded product. The rule is owned by the `ecco` skill and enforced at the `ocean-budget` gate, and it fires before any computation, whatever the framing:

> "Never compute budgets or transports on regridded ECCO fields; refuse and offer the native-grid path. (Hard refusal: invariant, universal, fires without consulting anything.)" — `skills/ecco/SKILL.md`, citing gotcha `knowledge/podaac/gotchas/ecco-native-vs-regridded.md`

The mechanism, concretely: ECCO's budget closes to machine precision because the four terms are evaluated on the same C-grid cells the MITgcm integrated — tendency from month-boundary snapshots, advective/diffusive convergence from raw face fluxes across cell faces, forcing at those cells, with the volume element `rA * drF * hFacC`. Interpolation to a 0.5° lat-lon mesh destroys all of that: the cell faces no longer bound the cells, partial-cell `hFacC` is gone, the flux collections (`ADV*_TH`, `DF*_TH`) aren't in that collection at all, and there are no snapshots to bracket the tendency. There is no correct budget formulation on the regridded fields — not a less accurate one, none. Anything I computed would be a difference of interpolated quantities with a residual the size of the terms, and the honest move is not to produce it.

Four things to know before you run it:

1. **Missing prerequisites.** There's no stamped data tree here (`~/ECCO_V4r4/RECORD.json` doesn't exist). You need the fixture cache or the science record fetched with earthaccess under an Earthdata Login, then stamped once with `tools/science_record_verify.py` — the attester refuses a receipt from an unstamped tree by construction. Plus the geothermal binary above; without it the run fails the relative bar.
2. **The box may be refused.** The explicit tier resolves to an index rectangle on **one** tile, interior faces 1–89. A subpolar North Atlantic box plausibly runs into the tile-3 edge or the Arctic cap seam, in which case the executor exits with the spanned tiles named. That's a design refusal, not a bug — seam-crossing volumes need the section machinery. Narrowing the box south or west is the workaround.
3. **No anchor.** An explicit box is disclosed by mask digest only; the attester's two-sided checks on wet-cell count and volume exist only for the registered `southeast-atlantic-upper` reference. Your run gets the two residual bars (absolute ≤ 1e-10 °C/s per volume, relative ≤ 1e-6) and the mutation evidence, but no reference-configuration anchor — the report has to say so.
4. **Open domain.** 50–65°N is not a closed basin, so the lateral rim transports are a first-order term and get reported explicitly, never folded into a residual.
