## I can't compute this budget from that product — and it isn't a technicality

The native-grid rule fires before anything else. It's the one hard refusal the `ecco` skill owns for the plugin (`skills/ecco/SKILL.md:52`, "Must NOT" at :237), and `ocean-budget` applies it at its gate (`skills/ocean-budget/SKILL.md`, "The native-grid rule (🔴, non-negotiable)"). Both name the concept `knowledge/podaac/gotchas/ecco-native-vs-regridded.md` as the authority.

Two independent reasons, and the second one is fatal on its own:

1. **Closure is a property of the discrete operators, not of the temperature field.** ECCO's budget closes to machine precision because the MITgcm flux-divergence operators on the LLC90 Arakawa C-grid, with partial-cell `hFacC`, exactly satisfy the discrete conservation law. Interpolating THETA to a 0.5° lat-lon mesh destroys that relationship — the divergence of an interpolated field is not the interpolation of the divergence. The residual you'd get is interpolation error, and you'd be reading it as physics.

2. **The terms simply aren't in the file.** That collection carries THETA and SALT only. A heat budget needs the advective and diffusive temperature fluxes (`ADVx_TH`/`ADVy_TH`/`ADVr_TH`, `DFxE_TH`/`DFyE_TH`/`DFrE_TH`/`DFrI_TH`), the surface forcing (`TFLUX`, `oceQsw` for shortwave penetration), month-boundary **snapshots** for the tendency bookends, `ETAN` for the z\* correction, the geometry granule for `rA·drF·hFacC`, and the geothermal flux at the bottom cell. Monthly means of T alone give you one term out of four. Whatever came out would be the tendency with the residual absorbed into it — which the skill forbids outright.

So this product gives you *heat content*, not a *heat budget*. Those are different deliverables.
