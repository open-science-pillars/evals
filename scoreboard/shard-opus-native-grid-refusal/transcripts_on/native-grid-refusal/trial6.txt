## I can't compute this from the product you have — and the reason is physical, not procedural

A heat budget on `ECCO_L4_TEMP_SALINITY_05DEG_MONTHLY_V4R4` is refused. This isn't a tooling limitation I can work around with a smarter script.

**Mechanism** (`knowledge/podaac/gotchas/ecco-native-vs-regridded.md`, stable, human-verified 2026-09-04, severity high): ECCO's conservation is a property of the *native* llc90 formulation — face fluxes, partial-cell geometry (hFac), and tile topology together satisfy the model equations exactly. The `05DEG` collections are interpolated for display and comparison. Interpolation does not preserve flux divergences.

Two consequences, and the second is the fatal one:

1. **The wrong-result mode is silent.** A budget assembled from regridded fields yields residuals *the same order as the physical terms*. Nothing errors. Tendencies you'd attribute to mixing or forcing would be interpolation artifacts. The formulation convention's traps table lists this as "closure fails everywhere, by construction."
2. **The ingredients don't exist at 0.5°.** A heat budget needs `ADVx/y/r_TH`, `DFxE/yE/rE_TH`, `DFrI_TH` — a CMR sweep (2026-07-04) found **no 05DEG 3D flux collections**. The temperature-flux family concept states it plainly: "No interpolated variant exists; this family is native-grid only." Your product carries `THETA` and `SALT` and nothing else. There is no term set to assemble, let alone close.

So there's no partial version of this I can hand you with a caveat attached. I'd be inventing three of the four terms.

The subpolar North Atlantic budget is entirely doable — it needs different granules. Required:

Plus one thing that is **not** a PO.DAAC collection at all: the **geothermal flux** (`knowledge/podaac/gotchas/ecco-geothermal-flux.md`, stable, human-verified). It's a static model input (`geothermalFlux.bin`, ECCO tutorial repo `misc/`) applied at the bottom wet cell. Omitting it fails deep and bottom cells by order 10–100 mW m⁻² equivalent while the surface still closes — and for the subpolar North Atlantic, where you likely want full depth over deep basins, that matters directly.

Two things to flag about that before you commit to it. An explicit box is disclosed by mask digest and **carries no reference anchor** — the two-sided checks on wet-cell count, volume, and residual-per-volume only apply to the registered `southeast-atlantic-upper` configuration, so your run gets the recomputed bars but not the anchored ones. And a subpolar box at 45–65°N is an **open domain**: domain-integrated closure is asserted only on closed domains, so the lateral heat transports across the box faces travel explicitly in the report rather than being folded into a residual.
