## The request is refused as posed

A property budget on regridded ECCO fields is a hard refusal in this plugin — it fires before any data is touched, whatever the framing. `ECCO_L4_TEMP_SALINITY_05DEG_MONTHLY_V4R4` is the 0.5° interpolated product, so a heat budget computed from it has no correct formulation to be right about.

The mechanism: ECCO's budget closes because the four terms are the MITgcm's own discrete operators on the llc90 C-grid — tendency minus advection, diffusion and forcing, evaluated on staggered faces with the partial-cell factor `hFacC` and the volume element `rA * drF * hFacC`. Interpolation to a lat-lon mesh destroys exactly the thing that makes it close: the fluxes no longer live on the faces of the cells whose volume you are differencing, so the divergence is no longer the divergence of anything. You would get a residual, and that residual would be an interpolation artifact rather than physics — unclosable, and not diagnosable either. The 0.5° product also ships THETA and SALT only; it carries none of the advective/diffusive flux terms (`ADVx_TH`, `DFxE_TH`, …) or `TFLUX`/`oceQsw` a budget needs, so three of the four terms don't exist in that file at all.

## What your 0.5° file is good for

Heat *content* and its change — a volume-weighted THETA inventory — is a legitimate diagnostic on the regridded product, and it is what people usually want when they say heat budget. It is not a budget: it gives you the storage term alone, with no attribution to advection, diffusion or surface forcing, and no closure. Say the word and I'll do that instead; the attested global counterpart is `ecco-ocean-heat-content`. Absolute OHC from ECCO carries a potential-temperature baseline caveat, so the change between months is the quantity, never the absolute value.
