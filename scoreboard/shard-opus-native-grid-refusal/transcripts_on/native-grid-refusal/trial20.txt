## The budget is refused on this product

`ECCO_L4_TEMP_SALINITY_05DEG_MONTHLY_V4R4` is the 0.5° regridded (interpolated) form of the ECCO v4r4 state estimate. A property budget on regridded ECCO fields is refused outright by `skills/ocean-budget/SKILL.md` and `skills/ecco/SKILL.md` — it's the one rule both skills fire without consulting anything, citing `knowledge/podaac/gotchas/ecco-native-vs-regridded.md`.

The mechanism, in short: ECCO's budgets close to machine precision because the terms are the MITgcm's *own* discrete operators on the llc90 C-grid — face fluxes on staggered velocity points, convergences differenced across those exact faces, volumes built from `rA * drF * hFacC` with partial bottom cells. Interpolation to a 0.5° lat-lon mesh destroys the discrete identity: the flux points no longer bound the interpolated cells, partial-cell thickness is gone, and the residual you'd get back is interpolation error, not physics. There is no correct budget formulation on that grid to fall back on — so this isn't "a budget with larger error bars," it's a number with no closure meaning.

Two further blocks, independent of the grid: this collection carries only `THETA` and `SALT` monthly means, so it has no advective or diffusive flux terms and no surface heat flux at all; and a tendency needs **month-boundary snapshots**, not monthly means.

## Three things that stand between you and running that

1. **No native tree on this machine.** Neither `~/ECCO_V4r4` (the 2010 fixture, ~2.5 GB) nor `~/ECCO_V4r4_record` (1992–2017) exists, so there's nothing to attest against yet. 2. **The subpolar North Atlantic may not be a legal volume in v1.** The regional computation states a limit: the control volume must lie within one llc90 tile interior, and seam-crossing volumes are refused until the seam-calibrated section machinery lands. A 50–65°N, 60–10°W box is exactly the kind that risks crossing the llc90 tile structure near the Arctic cap. Worth resolving before the download, not after.

3. **Depth range changes the physics.** If you want full-depth, geothermal is load-bearing: the concept records that omitting it lands at 1.24e-12, *inside* the absolute bar, and is caught only by the relative bar. If you're after the upper ocean only, say so and the bottom-cell term drops out.
