## The refusal

`ECCO_L4_TEMP_SALINITY_05DEG_MONTHLY_V4R4` is the 0.5° regridded product. Budgets on regridded ECCO fields are a hard refusal in this plugin — the one rule that fires without consulting anything (`skills/ecco/SKILL.md`, `skills/ocean-budget/SKILL.md`, citing gotcha `knowledge/podaac/gotchas/ecco-native-vs-regridded.md`: budgets close only on the native llc90 grid). No correct budget formulation exists on the interpolated grid; regridding is a scalar interpolation that does not preserve the flux-divergence identity the closure rests on, and the volume element `rA * drF * hFacC` has no regridded counterpart.

There's also a concrete check you can make on your own download that points the same way: that collection carries `THETA` and `SALT` and nothing else. Three of the four budget terms — advection, diffusion, forcing — are not in the file. A "budget" built from it would be a tendency plus three reconstructed guesses, and the residual would be absorbing the reconstruction error. That's exactly the failure mode the rule exists to prevent.

## The native path

The attested route for a regional heat budget over a control volume is `ecco-regional-heat-budget` (executor `skills/ocean-budget/scripts/ecco_regional_budget.py`, attester `regional_budget_check.py`). Collections it reads, from the executor itself:

| role | collection |
|---|---|
| geometry | `ECCO_L4_GEOMETRY_LLC0090GRID_V4R4` |
| advection + diffusion | `ECCO_L4_OCEAN_3D_TEMPERATURE_FLUX_LLC0090GRID_MONTHLY_V4R4` |
| surface forcing | `ECCO_L4_HEAT_FLUX_LLC0090GRID_MONTHLY_V4R4` |
| tendency | `ECCO_L4_TEMP_SALINITY_LLC0090GRID_SNAPSHOT_V4R4` |
| tendency (free surface) | `ECCO_L4_SSH_LLC0090GRID_SNAPSHOT_V4R4` |
| geothermal | `geothermalFlux.bin` — tutorial distribution, **not** a PO.DAAC collection |

Note the snapshot collections: tendencies need month-boundary snapshots, not monthly means. And the geothermal input is not on PO.DAAC — without it the run fails the relative bar (measured 2026-08-31: omitting it still passes the absolute 1e-10 bar, so only the relative bar catches it).

## Two things that will likely bite on this particular region

**Tile seams.** The executor has a stated v1 limit: the control volume must lie inside one tile's interior (faces 1–89). An explicit box spanning tiles, or one whose resolved rectangle touches a tile edge, is refused by design (`resolve_box`, lines 134–158) — seam-crossing volumes need the seam-calibrated section machinery. The subpolar North Atlantic is a plausible candidate to hit this, but I can't confirm it without the geometry granule; the executor will tell you definitively on first run. If it refuses, the options are a sub-box that fits one tile, or reframing as a transport problem across sections (the `meridional-transport` skill).

**No anchor on an explicit box.** Only the registered region `southeast-atlantic-upper` gets the two-sided attester checks on wet-cell count, volume and residual. An explicit box is disclosed by mask digest and carries no anchor — the report has to say so.

Also: `--data-root` must carry the `RECORD.json` stamp from `tools/science_record_verify.py`, or the attester refuses the receipt outright. And 2010 is the fixture year; the 1992–2017 science record lives at `~/ECCO_V4r4_record`.

The 0.5° product is fine for THETA/SALT maps, T–S structure, and mixed-layer work over the subpolar gyre — anything that doesn't depend on flux convergence. It's only budgets and transports that are off-limits. Flagging that as general method, not from a concept.
