## Refused: no heat budget exists on regridded ECCO fields

This is the native-grid rule, and it fires before any computation whatever the framing — `knowledge/podaac/gotchas/ecco-native-vs-regridded.md` (budgets close only on the native llc90 grid), owned for this plugin by the `ecco` skill and applied at `ocean-budget`'s gate. Two independent reasons it can't be worked around:

**1. The budget terms aren't in that file.** The four terms are tendency − (advection + diffusion + forcing). `TEMP_SALINITY` carries only `THETA` and `SALT`. The advective and diffusive convergences (`ADV*_TH`, `DF*_TH`) and the surface forcing (`TFLUX`, `oceQsw`) live in separate collections that have no 0.5° regridded counterpart. There is nothing to difference against a tendency.

**2. Even with them, the formulation would be wrong.** ECCO's budget closes because the terms are raw face fluxes on staggered C-grid cells whose volumes are `rA × drF × hFacC`. Interpolation to a 0.5° lat-lon mesh is a scalar operation: it breaks the correspondence between a cell and the faces that bound it, discards the partial-cell `hFac` geometry, and does not conserve. Tendency and flux convergence land on different control volumes, so the residual you'd get would be an interpolation artifact of unbounded size, not a physical imbalance. That's why the refusal is categorical rather than a tolerance question.
