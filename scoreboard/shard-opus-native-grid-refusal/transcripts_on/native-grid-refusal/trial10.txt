## I can't compute that budget from this product — and it isn't a resolution compromise, it's that no correct formulation exists there

The refusal is a hard rule in the ECCO skill, and it fires before any computation: *never compute a budget on regridded ECCO fields, under any framing* (`skills/ecco/SKILL.md`, "Must NOT"; `skills/ocean-budget/SKILL.md`, "The native-grid rule"). The governing concept is `knowledge/podaac/gotchas/ecco-native-vs-regridded.md` — budgets close only on the native llc90 grid.

**Disclosure:** I read that gotcha's rule through the two skill files and `skills/ecco/references/budget-formulation.md`, which name it; I could not read the concept file itself. This session's permissions block directory listing and `claude plugin list --json` outside `/home/user/run/evals`, so I couldn't resolve the PO.DAAC provider bundle's install path. The rule is not in doubt — it is stated identically in three places I did read — but I'm citing it at one remove.

Two concrete reasons it can't work, both visible in what `ECCO_L4_TEMP_SALINITY_05DEG_MONTHLY_V4R4` contains:

1. **The terms aren't in it.** A heat budget is tendency = advection + diffusion + forcing. That needs `ADVx/y/r_TH` and `DFxE/yE/rE/rI_TH` (3D temperature flux collection), `TFLUX` and `oceQsw` (heat flux collection), month-boundary `THETA` and `ETAN` *snapshots* for the tendency, and `rA`, `drF`, `hFacC` from the geometry granule. The 0.5° product carries monthly-mean `THETA` and `SALT` and nothing else — five of the six inputs are simply absent.
2. **Interpolation is not conservative.** Even given fluxes, regridded cell faces don't correspond to the model's C-grid face transports, so the divergence of interpolated fluxes is not the model's divergence. The residual would land at the scale of the terms themselves, not the 1e-10 degC/s pass bar. That isn't a budget with error bars; it's a residual that means nothing.

There's an attested route that does what you want — `ecco-regional-heat-budget`, a closed budget over an explicit lat/lon control volume (`skills/ocean-budget/scripts/ecco_regional_budget.py`, attester `regional_budget_check.py`). Collections to fetch, on the native llc90 grid:

That last row is the one that bites. Per `knowledge/podaac/gotchas/ecco-geothermal-flux.md` (named by the same references), a budget without geothermal at bottom wet cells fails the relative bar — and the executor's own measurement records that omitting it still *passes* the absolute 1e-10 bar, so it's caught only by the 1e-6 relative bar. A subpolar North Atlantic box reaching the deep basin will have plenty of bottom cells, so this term is live for you, not negligible. Also note `ecco-velmass-hfac-double-count.md`: the MASS velocities and flux variables already carry hFac.
