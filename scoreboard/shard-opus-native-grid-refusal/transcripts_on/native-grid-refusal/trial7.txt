## I can't compute this budget, and it isn't a close call

A heat budget on `ECCO_L4_TEMP_SALINITY_05DEG_MONTHLY_V4R4` is refused before any computation. This is the one rule in the ECCO workflow that fires without consulting anything else, and the reason is physical, not procedural.

**`knowledge/podaac/gotchas/ecco-native-vs-regridded.md`** (status: stable, high severity, human-verified 2026-09-04):

> ECCO's conservation is a property of the native formulation: face fluxes (ADV*, DF*), partial-cell geometry (hFac), and tile topology together satisfy the model equations exactly. The 0.5 degree `05DEG` collections are produced by interpolation for display and comparison convenience; interpolation does not preserve flux divergences.

> A "heat budget" assembled from regridded fields produces residuals of the same order as the physical terms; tendencies attributed to mixing or forcing are numerical artifacts of the interpolation. **Nothing errors; the numbers are simply wrong.**

That last sentence is why I'm stopping rather than computing-with-caveats. The failure is silent and the output looks like a heat budget.

There's also a hard blocker underneath the physics. The granules you have carry `THETA` and `SALT` only (`knowledge/podaac/fields/ecco-v4r4/temp-salinity.md`, stable). A 2026-07-04 CMR sweep recorded in the gotcha found **no 05DEG 3D flux collections exist at all** — so advection, diffusion, and the geothermal part of forcing have no source data on the 0.5° grid. Three of the four terms aren't merely degraded; they're unobtainable. There is no version of this I could assemble even if I ignored the rule.

## The native path for the subpolar North Atlantic

Per `knowledge/podaac/recipes/ecco-heat-budget.md` (stable, maintainer-verified 2026-09-20), the required collections are:

| Role | ShortName |
|---|---|
| Advective + diffusive fluxes | `ECCO_L4_OCEAN_3D_TEMPERATURE_FLUX_LLC0090GRID_MONTHLY_V4R4` |
| Surface forcing (`TFLUX`, `oceQsw`) | `ECCO_L4_HEAT_FLUX_LLC0090GRID_MONTHLY_V4R4` |
| Tendency bookends (`THETA`) | `ECCO_L4_TEMP_SALINITY_LLC0090GRID_SNAPSHOT_V4R4` |
| z* scale factor (`ETAN`) | `ECCO_L4_SSH_LLC0090GRID_SNAPSHOT_V4R4` |
| Geometry (`rA`, `drF`, `hFacC`, `Depth`, `Z`, `Zp1`) | `ECCO_L4_GEOMETRY_LLC0090GRID_V4R4` |

Plus one ancillary that is **not** a PO.DAAC collection: `geothermalFlux.bin` from the ECCO v4 tutorial's `misc/` directory. Per `knowledge/podaac/gotchas/ecco-geothermal-flux.md` (stable), omitting it makes deep and bottom cells fail while the surface still closes — and for the subpolar North Atlantic specifically, where you likely care about deep convection and the full column, that term is not optional.

Note the flux variable names are `DFxE_TH`/`DFyE_TH` (with the `E`), and both `DFrE_TH` and `DFrI_TH` are needed — dropping the implicit vertical term breaks near-surface closure.
