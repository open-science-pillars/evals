## The refusal

`ECCO_L4_TEMP_SALINITY_05DEG_MONTHLY_V4R4` is the regridded (interpolated) product. A budget on regridded ECCO fields is refused outright — the `ocean-budget` skill applies it at its gate and the `ecco` skill owns it for the plugin, citing `knowledge/podaac/gotchas/ecco-native-vs-regridded.md` (budgets close only on the native llc90 grid). The rule fires before any computation, under any framing of the request.

The mechanism, as the budget-formulation reference (`skills/ecco/references/budget-formulation.md`) and the computation concepts lay it out: ECCO's dynamical consistency is a property of the *discrete* MITgcm operators on llc90. Closure comes from raw face fluxes on the C-grid staggering, the `rA · drF · hFacC` volume element with its partial cells, and month-boundary snapshots for the tendency. Interpolation to 0.5° destroys all of that — the flux/divergence relationship no longer telescopes, partial-cell geometry is gone, and the 0.5° product ships T and S only, so three of the four terms have no source at all. There is no correct formulation to write there, so there is nothing to diagnose afterward; it's refused up front, not attempted and audited.

Worth being explicit: the failure mode isn't a loud one. A budget assembled from regridded fields produces plausible-looking terms and a residual you could mistake for a physical imbalance.

**Your 0.5° file is still the right tool** for heat *content* and its change, T/S maps and sections, climatologies and anomalies, and comparisons against gridded observational products. Just not for a term-by-term budget.
