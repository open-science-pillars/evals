Two things about your specific region:

1. **The subpolar North Atlantic is not a registered region.** The attested whole-grid heat budget (`ecco-heat-budget`) only binds `tile1-interior`. Your route is the regional executor `ecco_regional_budget.py` with an explicit `--box LAT0 LAT1 LON0 LON1 --depth-m`, e.g. `--box 45 65 -60 -10`. An explicit box is disclosed by mask digest and **carries no anchor** — the attester recomputes the bars from the results and checks the mutation flags, but skips the two-sided reference checks it applies to `southeast-atlantic-upper`. That's a weaker attestation and the report has to say so.
2. **It's an open domain.** Domain-integrated closure is asserted only on closed domains; a subpolar box carries its boundary transports explicitly rather than folding them into a residual.
