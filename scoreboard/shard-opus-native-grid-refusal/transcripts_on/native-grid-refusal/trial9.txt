## I can't compute this from that product — the refusal, and the route that works

The heat budget request is refused on regridded fields. This fires before any computation, per gotcha `knowledge/podaac/gotchas/ecco-native-vs-regridded.md`, which the `ecco` skill owns for the plugin (`skills/ecco/SKILL.md:52`, `:238`) and `ocean-budget` re-applies at its gate. `ECCO_L4_TEMP_SALINITY_05DEG_MONTHLY_V4R4` is a 0.5° interpolated product, so no correct budget formulation exists on it.

**Why, concretely.** The four-term budget is tendency = advection + diffusion + forcing, and every term is discretized on the llc90 C-grid: convergences are differences of *raw face fluxes* across cell faces, and the volume element is `rA * drF * hFacC` with partial bottom cells. Interpolation to a 0.5° lat-lon mesh destroys the face-flux/cell-volume pairing that makes those differences exact. What you would get is not a budget with a large residual — it is an expression with no closure property at all, so there is nothing to judge against a tolerance.

**A second, independent blocker.** Even on the native grid, that collection carries only THETA and SALT. A heat budget additionally needs the 3-D temperature flux terms, the surface heat flux terms, month-boundary snapshots, the geometry granule, and the geothermal input. None of those are in it.

### What the 0.5° product *is* good for

It is a legitimate product for things that don't require closure: subpolar North Atlantic THETA/SALT maps and sections, heat-content *change* between months (with the potential-temperature baseline caveat — absolute OHC is never quoted from it), seasonal cycles, T–S properties, mixed-layer structure. If any of that is what you actually want, say so and I'll run it on the data you already have.
