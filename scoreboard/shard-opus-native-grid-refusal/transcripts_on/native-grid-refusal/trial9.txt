### Why, concretely

Two independent reasons, either one sufficient:

**1. The formulation doesn't exist on that grid.** ECCO's budget closes to machine precision because the archived flux terms (`ADVx_TH`, `DFxE_TH`, …) are the model's own face-integrated transports across native C-grid cell faces, and the tendency of each cell is exactly their discrete divergence. The 0.5° product is a horizontal interpolation of *scalars*. A 0.5° cell boundary corresponds to no set of native faces, so there is no discrete divergence identity to satisfy — the residual would be the same order as the terms, and there is no correct formulation to write down and no meaningful tolerance to judge it against. The regridded fields also carry no `hFacC`, so the volume element `rA · drF · hFacC` (partial bottom cells) is unavailable; even the inventory integral isn't the model's.

**2. Three of the four terms aren't in that collection.** `ECCO_L4_TEMP_SALINITY_05DEG_MONTHLY_V4R4` holds THETA and SALT only. No advective or diffusive flux terms, no `TFLUX`/`oceQsw`, no month-boundary snapshots. Reconstructing them by finite-differencing interpolated monthly means is exactly the operation that produces the meaningless residual above.

Two things about your domain specifically:

- **It's an open domain.** Denmark Strait, Iceland–Scotland, the Labrador shelf and the southern face all carry transport. Domain-integrated closure is asserted only on closed domains; an open-domain integral must carry the boundary transports as explicit terms. Pointwise closure still holds everywhere.
- **The attested route takes an explicit box.** `ecco_regional_budget.py` binds `--budget heat` with either a registered region or `--box LAT0 LAT1 LON0 LON1 --depth-m`. A subpolar box (say `--box 50 65 -60 -10`) is disclosed by mask digest and **carries no anchor** — the attester checks the wet-cell count, volume and residual-per-volume two-sided only on its reference configuration. The report has to say so.

The executors read native granules from `--data-root` (fixture cache is **2010 only**; the science record covers 1992–2017), and the tree needs the `RECORD.json` stamp from `science_record_verify.py` — every attester refuses an unstamped tree.
