## The request is refused, and the reason is physical, not procedural

**`ECCO_L4_TEMP_SALINITY_05DEG_MONTHLY_V4R4` cannot produce a heat budget.** Not "will be approximate" — there is no correct formulation on it.

`knowledge/podaac/gotchas/ecco-native-vs-regridded.md` (status **stable**, severity high, human-verified by the maintainer 2026-09-04, current until 2027-01-04):

> "ECCO's conservation is a property of the native formulation: face fluxes (ADV\*, DF\*), partial-cell geometry (hFac), and tile topology together satisfy the model equations exactly. The 0.5 degree `05DEG` collections are produced by interpolation for display and comparison convenience; interpolation does not preserve flux divergences..."

Two independent blockers, either one fatal:

1. **The ingredients don't exist at 0.5°.** The concept records a CMR sweep (2026-07-04) that "found no 05DEG 3D flux collections." The `05DEG` family carries state variables only — `THETA` and `SALT`, per `knowledge/podaac/fields/ecco-v4r4/temp-salinity.md`. The advective term needs `ADVx_TH`/`ADVy_TH`/`ADVr_TH`; the diffusive term needs `DFxE_TH`/`DFyE_TH`/`DFrE_TH`/`DFrI_TH`. None are published on the interpolated grid. Three of the four terms are simply unavailable.
2. **Even with them, interpolation destroys the closure.** The failure is silent: "residuals of the same order as the physical terms; tendencies attributed to mixing or forcing are numerical artifacts of the interpolation. **Nothing errors; the numbers are simply wrong.**" The formulation traps table scores this case "closure fails everywhere, by construction."

The `05DEG` collection is the right product for maps, sections, and pointwise comparison against gridded observations (Argo, WOA). It is the wrong product for any conserved quantity.

Flagging that last row early because it bites: for a subpolar North Atlantic budget you'll be integrating to the seafloor over the Irminger and Labrador basins, and omitting geothermal makes closure "degrade with depth and fail at the seafloor," sitting three orders above the residual bar. It is the first thing the auditor checks on a heat-budget failure.

Note also that snapshots — not monthly means — bookend the tendency, and the `05DEG` family has no snapshot variant at all.
