## The refusal

A property budget on regridded ECCO fields is refused outright. The rule is owned by the `ecco` skill and gated again by `ocean-budget`:

> Never compute budgets or transports on regridded ECCO fields; refuse and offer the native-grid path. (Hard refusal: invariant, universal, fires without consulting anything.)

`ECCO_L4_TEMP_SALINITY_05DEG_MONTHLY_V4R4` is the 0.5° interpolated product. The mechanism behind the rule is concrete, not procedural fussiness:

- **The budget's closure is a property of the discretization, not of the fields.** ECCO closes to machine precision because the four terms are evaluated on the same LLC90 C-grid cells the MITgcm integrated. Interpolation to 0.5° breaks the divergence theorem — the advective flux through a regridded cell face is no longer the flux the model actually moved.
- **The flux terms don't exist in your file.** The budget needs `ADV*_TH`/`DF*_TH` face fluxes, `TFLUX`/`oceQsw`, and `ETAN`. The 0.5° temperature/salinity product carries `THETA` and `SALT` only. There is no formulation that recovers the missing terms from T and S.
- **The volume element is gone.** The budget integrates over `rA * drF * hFacC`; partial-cell `hFacC` does not survive regridding.

Anything I produced from this file would be a heat *content* time series with a residual silently absorbed into it, presented as a budget. The skill forbids that specifically ("never absorb a residual into a physical term").

## The native path that does answer your question

The plugin ships an attested regional budget executor that takes an arbitrary control volume — this is the right tool for a subpolar North Atlantic box.

**Collections to fetch** (from the executor's declared inputs, `skills/ocean-budget/scripts/ecco_regional_budget.py:76-82`):

| Collection | Supplies |
|---|---|
| `ECCO_L4_OCEAN_3D_TEMPERATURE_FLUX_LLC0090GRID_MONTHLY_V4R4` | `ADV*_TH`, `DF*_TH` |
| `ECCO_L4_HEAT_FLUX_LLC0090GRID_MONTHLY_V4R4` | `TFLUX`, `oceQsw` |
| `ECCO_L4_TEMP_SALINITY_LLC0090GRID_SNAPSHOT_V4R4` | `THETA` at month boundaries |
| `ECCO_L4_SSH_LLC0090GRID_SNAPSHOT_V4R4` | `ETAN` (free-surface scaling) |
| `ECCO_L4_GEOMETRY_LLC0090GRID_V4R4` | `rA`, `drF`, `hFacC`, `Depth`, `Z`, `Zp1` |

Plus **`geothermalFlux.bin`**, which is *not* a PO.DAAC collection — it comes from the ECCO v4 tutorial distribution. This matters for you specifically: the subpolar North Atlantic is deep, so your control volume will contain many bottom cells. The executor's own measurement notes that omitting geothermal still passes the absolute bar of 1e-10 and is caught only by the relative bar of 1e-6 — a dropped geothermal term is exactly the failure mode that looks like success.

Then, once the tree is fetched and stamped:

```bash
uv run "$PLUGIN_ROOT"/skills/ocean-budget/scripts/ecco_regional_budget.py \
  --budget heat --box 50 65 -60 -10 --depth-m 1000 --year 2010 \
  --data-root ~/ECCO_V4r4 --receipt /tmp/spna-heat-receipt.json
uv run "$PLUGIN_ROOT"/skills/ocean-budget/scripts/regional_budget_check.py /tmp/spna-heat-receipt.json
```

Two caveats on that box, both worth knowing before you fetch ~GBs:

1. **Tile-seam limit.** V1 requires the volume to fall inside one LLC90 tile's interior (faces 1–89). A 50–65°N, 60–10°W box may straddle a seam or touch a tile edge, in which case the executor refuses by design (`ecco_regional_budget.py:142-155`) rather than returning a wrong answer. Worth testing first with a smaller box.
2. **No anchor.** An explicit `--box` is disclosed by mask digest but carries no reference anchor; only `southeast-atlantic-upper` is registered. The attester still checks both bars and the mutation evidence, but the report has to say the volume is unanchored.
