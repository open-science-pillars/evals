## Refused: no heat budget on regridded ECCO

The `ocean-budget` workflow gates on a hard refusal it doesn't get to weigh against anything else: *"A budget request on regridded fields is REFUSED: no correct budget formulation exists there"* — owned by the `ecco` skill for the whole plugin (`skills/ecco/SKILL.md`, Must NOT #1), applied at the gate in `skills/ocean-budget/SKILL.md`. It fires before parsing the domain or the period, and it names gotcha `knowledge/podaac/gotchas/ecco-native-vs-regridded.md`.

Two concrete reasons, not just the rule:

**The terms aren't in the product.** `ECCO_L4_TEMP_SALINITY_05DEG_MONTHLY_V4R4` carries THETA and SALT. A closed heat budget needs four terms, and per the sanctioned executor's own input list (`skills/ocean-budget/scripts/ecco_regional_budget.py:76-82`):

| term | what it needs | in your file? |
|---|---|---|
| tendency | THETA **snapshots** + ETAN snapshots (free-surface s\* scaling) | no — monthly means only |
| advection | `ADVx_TH`, `ADVy_TH`, `ADVr_TH` | no |
| diffusion | `DFxE_TH`, `DFyE_TH`, `DFrE/DFrI_TH` | no |
| forcing | `TFLUX`, `oceQsw` (+ geothermal at bottom cells) | no |
| volume element | `rA`, `drF`, `hFacC` from the geometry granule | no |

**Interpolation breaks the thing that makes it close.** The four-term balance is exact because the fluxes live on C-grid cell faces and telescope over a finite-volume control volume. Mapping cell-centre scalars to a 0.5° lat-lon mesh discards the face geometry, so even if you reconstructed fluxes by finite-differencing the interpolated field, the residual would land at the order of the terms themselves — not the `1e-10 degC/s` bar the attested computation holds (`knowledge/computations/ecco-heat-budget.md`, status stable, measured max 4.95e-11 over 3,341,772 cell-months). A "budget" you produced that way would close to nothing, and the failure would look like physics rather than the regridding artefact it is.
