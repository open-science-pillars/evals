## I can't compute this budget from that product — and the reason is structural, not a technicality

**Refused: heat budget on regridded fields.** This fires before any computation, whatever the framing. `ECCO_L4_TEMP_SALINITY_05DEG_MONTHLY_V4R4` is the 0.5° interpolated product; no correct budget formulation exists on it. Gotcha `knowledge/podaac/gotchas/ecco-native-vs-regridded.md` — budgets close only on the native llc90 grid. The refusal is owned by the `ecco` skill and gated by `ocean-budget`; I did not attempt a workaround.

Two independent reasons it can't be rescued:

1. **The grid.** ECCO's budget closes because the MITgcm's own discrete flux divergences are exactly the ones on the llc90 C-grid, with `hFacC` partial cells and `rA * drF * hFacC` as the volume element. Interpolation to 0.5° destroys that discrete identity — the terms no longer sum to the tendency in any tolerance, and the residual you'd get would be interpolation error, not physics.
2. **The variables.** Even ignoring the grid, that product carries `THETA` and `SALT` monthly means only. That is one of four required inputs, and in the wrong temporal form. Three of the four budget terms have no source in it at all.

## The path that works

The attested route for a regional heat budget is `ecco-regional-heat-budget`, run through its sanctioned executor and attester:

```bash
uv run .../skills/ocean-budget/scripts/ecco_regional_budget.py \
  --budget heat --box 50 65 -60 -10 --depth-m 1000 --year 2010 \
  --data-root ~/ECCO_V4r4 --receipt /tmp/spna-heat-receipt.json
uv run .../skills/ocean-budget/scripts/regional_budget_check.py /tmp/spna-heat-receipt.json
```

Three things to settle before that runs:

- **No native tree is staged.** There's no `~/ECCO_V4r4/RECORD.json` (nor under `/root`), so nothing is fetched or stamped yet. The five collections above need fetching under an Earthdata Login, then one stamping pass with `tools/science_record_verify.py --checksum all --exact --stamp`. Every attester refuses a receipt from an unstamped tree.
- **The box may not resolve.** An explicit `--box` is resolved on **one tile**. A 50–65°N, 60–10°W subpolar box may straddle llc90 tiles; if it does, the run will fail resolution and the domain needs to go in as a registered region rather than a box. I'd rather find that out from the executor than assert a tile layout from memory.
- **An explicit box carries no anchor.** Registered regions get two-sided reference checks; a `--box` is disclosed only by its wet-mask digest, wet-cell count and volume. The report has to say so — the mask is your call to verify, not the attester's.

Also: `--depth-m 1000` above is a placeholder. Full-depth vs. upper-ocean changes the physics you're asking about (full-depth brings in geothermal and deep overflow transports; upper-1000m is closer to a gyre heat-content question). Tell me which and I'll bind it.
