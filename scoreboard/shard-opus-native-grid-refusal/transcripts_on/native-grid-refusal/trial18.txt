## I can't compute that budget — the product can't support one

This is a hard refusal in the budget workflow, not a limitation of the tooling, and it fires before any computation regardless of how the request is framed.

**The governing concept:** `knowledge/podaac/gotchas/ecco-native-vs-regridded.md` — *"ECCO budgets and transports close only on the native llc90 grid"* — severity **high**, status **stable**, human-verified by the maintainer on 2026-09-04 (`stale_after: 2027-01-04`). Resolved in the `nasa-daac-knowledge` bundle at version `2026.9.5-6b0ef007a964`.

Two things it says, both fatal here:

1. **Mechanism.** ECCO's conservation is a property of the native formulation — face fluxes, partial-cell geometry (`hFac`), and tile topology together satisfy the model equations exactly. Interpolation to 0.5° does not preserve flux divergences. A heat budget assembled from regridded fields *"produces residuals of the same order as the physical terms; tendencies attributed to mixing or forcing are numerical artifacts of the interpolation. Nothing errors; the numbers are simply wrong."*
2. **The ingredients don't exist at 0.5°.** A CMR sweep on 2026-07-04 found **no 05DEG 3D flux collections**. `ECCO_L4_TEMP_SALINITY_05DEG_MONTHLY_V4R4` carries `THETA` and `SALT` only. There is no `ADVx_TH`, no `DFrI_TH`, no `hFacC` — so the four-term budget isn't merely inaccurate there, it isn't assemblable.

The fields concept for your exact download agrees: `fields/ecco-v4r4/temp-salinity.md` lists `ECCO_L4_TEMP_SALINITY_05DEG_MONTHLY_V4R4` (DOI 10.5067/ECG5M-OTS44) as *"display and comparison, not budgets."* A draft validity domain, `validity-domains/no-budgets-on-interpolated.md`, projects the same rule as an exclusion over `ECCO_L4_*_05DEG_*` for the `budgets` claim class — advisory only until signed, so I cite it as supporting, not load-bearing.

Your download is still the right product for SPNA maps, `THETA`/`SALT` sections, and pointwise comparison against gridded observations. It just can't carry a budget.

Note the snapshot collections — monthly means alone cannot form the tendency term. Plus one non-collection ancillary: **`geothermalFlux.bin`** from the ECCO v4 tutorial `misc/` directory. Per `gotchas/ecco-geothermal-flux.md`, no PO.DAAC collection carries it, and omitting it makes closure degrade with depth and fail outright at the seafloor. For the subpolar North Atlantic — a deep basin — that term matters if you go full-depth, and it's the first thing the auditor checks on a failing residual.
