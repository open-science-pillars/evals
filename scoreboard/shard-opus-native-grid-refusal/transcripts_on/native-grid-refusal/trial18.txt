## Refused: a heat budget on regridded ECCO

`ECCO_L4_TEMP_SALINITY_05DEG_MONTHLY_V4R4` is the 0.5° interpolated product. A property budget on regridded ECCO fields is a hard refusal in this plugin — the rule fires before any computation, whatever the framing (`knowledge/podaac/gotchas/ecco-native-vs-regridded.md`, cited by the ecco skill and by `skills/ecco/references/budget-formulation.md:26-28`; the ecco skill owns the refusal, `skills/ecco/SKILL.md:52-53, 237-239`).

Two independent reasons, either one fatal:

**No correct formulation exists there.** The budget is a discrete statement about llc90 control volumes: tendency − (advection + diffusion + forcing) = 0, with the volume element `rA * drF * hFacC` and the advective/diffusive fluxes living on C-grid cell faces. Interpolation to a 0.5° lat–lon mesh destroys the correspondence between a cell's volume and the faces its fluxes crossed. Nothing about the interpolated field is wrong as a *field* — it just no longer satisfies a conservation statement, so a "residual" computed on it measures the regridding, not the physics.

**The terms aren't in the product anyway.** That collection carries THETA and SALT monthly means. It has no advective or diffusive heat-flux variables, no forcing terms, no month-boundary snapshots, and no `hFacC`. The only term you could form is an approximate tendency; the remaining three would have to be lumped into a residual and called a budget. The ocean-budget workflow forbids exactly that — no term is approximated silently, and no residual is absorbed into a physical term.
