## I can't compute a heat budget from that product — and the reason is structural, not procedural

`ECCO_L4_TEMP_SALINITY_05DEG_MONTHLY_V4R4` is the 0.5° interpolated product. A property budget on regridded ECCO fields is a hard refusal in this plugin: it's owned by the `ecco` skill (`skills/ecco/SKILL.md`, "Must NOT"), gated again in `ocean-budget` step 0, and it fires before any consultation or computation. The governing concept is `knowledge/podaac/gotchas/ecco-native-vs-regridded.md` — budgets close only on the native LLC90 grid.

Being transparent about one limit: this sandbox blocked me from opening that gotcha's text in the provider bundle (`nasa-daac-knowledge`) — directory listing outside the working dir is denied and I couldn't resolve its install path. I cite it by the path both skills name it under. The refusal itself doesn't depend on reading it; I did read `knowledge/computations/ecco-heat-budget.md` and `skills/ecco/references/budget-formulation.md` in full.

**Why interpolation destroys the budget, concretely.** The four terms — tendency minus (advection + diffusion + forcing) — aren't derived from T and S. They're separate archived flux diagnostics on the Arakawa C-grid, staggered onto cell faces, with a volume element `rA · drF · hFacC` and a z* tendency correction. Three things break at once on the 0.5° grid:

- **The flux terms don't exist in that collection.** `TEMP_SALINITY` carries THETA and SALT. The advective and diffusive flux variables aren't in it at any resolution, so three of four terms have no source.
- **`hFacC` doesn't survive regridding.** Partial bottom cells are a native-grid quantity; interpolation smears them, so the volume element is unrecoverable.
- **The tendency needs month-boundary snapshots**, not monthly means, plus the z* correction. Monthly-mean T alone gives you the wrong tendency even if you had the fluxes.

Reconstructing advection as a finite difference of interpolated T would not be the model's advection — it would be a new, unclosed equation whose residual is an artifact of your own discretization. There's no tolerance to judge that against, which is exactly why the rule is a refusal and not a warning.

## The native path

The attested route binds to `knowledge/computations/ecco-heat-budget.md` (`status: stable`, four human-verified events, most recently maintainer PaulMRamirez 2026-09-20). Pass bar, read from the concept: `residual_max ≤ 1e-10` and `residual_p999 ≤ 1e-11` degC/s, pointwise at every wet cell and month — absolute, never a ratio against the dominant term. Baseline measurement was max 4.95e-11, p99.9 7.3e-12 degC/s over 3,341,772 cell-months, so the bar carries 2× headroom.

For the subpolar North Atlantic specifically, you want the **regional** executor, not the whole-grid one:

```bash
uv run $R/skills/ocean-budget/scripts/ecco_regional_budget.py \
  --budget heat --box 50 65 -60 -10 --depth-m 1000 --year 2010 \
  --data-root ~/ECCO_V4r4 --receipt /tmp/spna-heat-receipt.json
uv run $R/skills/ocean-budget/scripts/regional_budget_check.py /tmp/spna-heat-receipt.json
```

Three things to know before you run it:

1. **Your box carries no anchor.** The only registered regions are `tile1-interior` and `southeast-atlantic-upper`. An explicit `--box` is disclosed by mask digest and gets no reference-configuration check (wet-cell count, volume, two-sided residual). That's a real weakening of the attestation and the report has to say so. Tell me your intended latitude/longitude bounds and depth — I used a placeholder above.
2. **Geothermal flux.** For a heat budget reaching the bottom, the geothermal term is required and is *not* a PO.DAAC collection (`knowledge/podaac/gotchas/ecco-geothermal-flux.md`). Dropping it drives deep-cell residuals orders above tolerance. If you cap at ~1000 m this may not bind, but it must be checked, not assumed.
3. **The data tree must be stamped.** Every attester refuses a receipt whose `data.record` isn't the `RECORD.json` stamp left by `tools/science_record_verify.py`. One-time step after the first fetch.

You'll also need the native collections — geometry is `ECCO_L4_GEOMETRY_LLC0090GRID_V4R4` (one static granule, merged with everything), plus the monthly flux and month-boundary snapshot collections. I'd run `load-ecco` to resolve those ShortNames through the signed `fields/ecco-v4r4/` concepts rather than have me recall them; that's the one place I'd be guessing.
