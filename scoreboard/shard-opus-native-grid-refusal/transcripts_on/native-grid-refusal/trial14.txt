The mechanism: ECCO's budgets close to machine precision because the MITgcm's *discrete* flux-divergence identity is exactly satisfied on the llc90 C-grid — fluxes live on cell faces, volumes are `rA · drF · hFacC`, and the terms cancel by construction. ## The native path, concretely

| term | source |
