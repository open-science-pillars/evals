## I can't compute a budget from that product — and it isn't a data-quality quibble

`ECCO_L4_TEMP_SALINITY_05DEG_MONTHLY_V4R4` is the regridded (0.5°, lat-lon) ECCO product. A property budget on regridded ECCO fields is a hard refusal in this plugin, and it fires before anything else is consulted:

> "Never compute any budget on regridded fields, under any framing." — `skills/ocean-budget/SKILL.md` ("The native-grid rule", 🔴 non-negotiable), owned for the plugin by `skills/ecco/SKILL.md` and resting on gotcha `knowledge/podaac/gotchas/ecco-native-vs-regridded.md`.

The reason is structural, not precision. ECCO closes budgets because the MITgcm's own discrete operators are satisfied exactly on the llc90 C-grid: the tendency, the raw face fluxes through cell faces, and the forcing are the model's own terms. Interpolation to 0.5° destroys the discrete divergence — the face fluxes no longer bound the cells they're attributed to, and the volume element `rA * drF * hFacC` doesn't exist there. There's no correct budget formulation on that grid to compute, so a number produced from it would be a residual of interpolation error, not a heat budget. The 0.5° product is fine for what it's for: maps, T/S climatology, heat *content* (not the budget terms).

Also, two practical notes: I don't see the 0.5° files anywhere under `/home/user/run/evals`, and neither native tree (`~/ECCO_V4r4`, `~/ECCO_V4r4_record`) is present, so nothing is staged locally yet either way.

## The native path, which is attested

A regional heat budget over a control volume has a sanctioned, attested computation: `knowledge/computations/ecco-regional-heat-budget.md` (status **stable**), executor `skills/ocean-budget/scripts/ecco_regional_budget.py`, attester `skills/ocean-budget/scripts/regional_budget_check.py`. It closes the budget across three *independent* collections (snapshot tendency, raw rim face fluxes, surface forcing + geothermal) against two bars — absolute ≤1e-10 °C/s and relative-to-largest-term ≤1e-6 — and writes mutation evidence into every receipt.

## Three things that will shape what you actually get

**1. The subpolar box will probably be refused by the v1 seam limit.** The executor resolves an explicit box to an index rectangle on *one* tile, interior faces 1–89, and refuses anything spanning a tile seam or touching a tile edge (`ecco_regional_budget.py:140-155`; the concept states this as a v1 limit). A 45–65N, 60W–10W box on llc90 very likely spans the northern Atlantic tile and the Arctic cap face. I can only confirm that once the geometry granule is on disk. If it does span, the options are a sub-box that fits one tile interior, or the pointwise budget (`ecco-heat-budget`, closure at every wet cell — which holds on any spatial subset) instead of a volume-integrated one.

**2. Geothermal is not optional here.** A subpolar box deep enough to matter has many bottom cells. The concept records the measurement: omitting geothermal lands at 1.24e-12, *inside* the absolute bar, caught only by the relative bar. A budget built from PO.DAAC collections alone omits it silently.
