## Why

A heat budget on regridded ECCO fields is refused outright — gotcha `knowledge/podaac/gotchas/ecco-native-vs-regridded.md`, cited by `skills/ocean-budget/SKILL.md` and `skills/ecco/SKILL.md` as the one rule that fires without consulting anything. Concretely, for `ECCO_L4_TEMP_SALINITY_05DEG_MONTHLY_V4R4`:

- It carries interpolated `THETA` and `SALT` and nothing else. Three of the four budget terms — advective convergence (`ADVx/y/r_TH`), diffusive convergence (`DFxE/DFyE/DFrE/DFrI_TH`), and forcing (`TFLUX`, `oceQsw`, geothermal) — have no counterpart in it at all.
- It has no `rA`, `drF`, `hFacC`, `Depth`, `Zp1`, so there is no volume element `rA·drF·hFacC` and no partial-cell geometry.
- It has no month-boundary snapshots, so even the tendency term can't be formed (a monthly-mean difference is not the tendency).
- Interpolation doesn't commute with the divergence operator. Even if you regridded the flux collections too, the convergences would no longer sum to the tendency, and the residual would be an interpolation artifact rather than a physical diagnostic. There is no correct formulation to write down here — this isn't a tolerance you could loosen.
