## I can't compute a heat budget from that product — and the reason isn't a technicality

**Request as parsed:** heat budget, subpolar North Atlantic, from `ECCO_L4_TEMP_SALINITY_05DEG_MONTHLY_V4R4`, period and depth range unspecified.

That input hits a hard refusal in the ECCO workflow, so I stopped before computing anything.

`knowledge/podaac/gotchas/ecco-native-vs-regridded.md` (**stable**, human-verified 2026-09-04 by the maintainer; severity high) names your exact collection in its sources. Two independent reasons:

1. **Interpolation destroys the conservation.** ECCO's closure is a property of the native formulation — face fluxes (`ADV*`, `DF*`), partial-cell geometry (`hFac`), and llc tile topology together satisfy the model equations exactly. The `05DEG` collections are interpolated for display and comparison; interpolation does not preserve flux divergences.
2. **The ingredients don't exist at 0.5°.** A CMR sweep on 2026-07-04 found **no 05DEG 3D flux collections**. The `05DEG` family carries state variables only. The four-term budget isn't merely inaccurate there — it isn't assemblable.

The failure mode is the dangerous kind: **nothing errors.** You get residuals the same order as the physical terms, and the tendencies you'd attribute to mixing or forcing are interpolation artifacts. The formulation traps table in `knowledge/podaac/conventions/ecco-budget-formulation.md` (**stable**) lists this row as "closure fails everywhere, by construction."

Your 0.5° product is the right tool for maps, time series, and pointwise comparison against gridded observations. Just not for a budget.

| Surface forcing (`TFLUX`, `oceQsw`) | `ECCO_L4_HEAT_FLUX_LLC0090GRID_MONTHLY_V4R4` | 10.5067/ECL5M-HEA44 |

One ancillary that is **not** a PO.DAAC collection: the static geothermal flux (`geothermalFlux.bin`, ECCO v4 tutorial repo `misc/`), applied at the bottom wet cell. Per `knowledge/podaac/gotchas/ecco-geothermal-flux.md` (**stable**, human-verified), omitting it is silent — surface layers close, deep cells fail. For a subpolar North Atlantic budget with any real depth extent, this term is not optional.

The tendency also needs snapshots, not monthly means, with the z* scale factor `s* = 1 + ETAN/Depth`; and the vertical diffusive term needs `DFrE_TH` **plus** `DFrI_TH`.
