## The request is refused as posed

A heat budget cannot be computed from `ECCO_L4_TEMP_SALINITY_05DEG_MONTHLY_V4R4`, or from any regridded ECCO field. This is a hard refusal in the ECCO skill and the ocean-budget workflow, and it fires before any computation regardless of framing:

> `knowledge/podaac/gotchas/ecco-native-vs-regridded.md` — budgets close only on the native llc90 grid.

Cited by `skills/ecco/SKILL.md` ("Never compute budgets or transports on regridded ECCO fields") and `skills/ocean-budget/SKILL.md` ("A budget request on regridded fields is REFUSED: no correct budget formulation exists there").

**The mechanism.** ECCO's budgets close to machine precision because MITgcm's discrete terms are exact on the C-grid: the tendency in a cell equals the sum of face fluxes through that cell's staggered faces, over the volume element `rA * drF * hFacC`. Regridding to 0.5° interpolates cell-centered scalars onto a new mesh and throws away the face geometry, the partial-cell `hFac` factors, and the flux/volume correspondence. There is no term set on that grid that sums to zero — any "residual" you got would measure interpolation error, not physics, and it would be large enough to swamp the signal you're after. On top of that, the T/S collection carries no flux or advection variables at all, so three of the four terms could only be reconstructed from gradients, which is a different (and non-closing) calculation.

Two further problems specific to your product: it has no month-boundary snapshots (the tendency term needs them, not monthly means), and no geothermal input (`knowledge/podaac/gotchas/ecco-geothermal-flux.md` — not a PO.DAAC collection at all, and its omission is measurable at 1.24e-12 degC/s, inside the absolute bar and caught only by the relative one).

## The native path

There is an attested regional heat budget for exactly this shape of question: `knowledge/computations/ecco-regional-heat-budget.md` (status stable, human-verified 2026-09-20). It closes over a control volume by comparing three independent collections — tendency from temperature and SSH snapshots, transport from raw face fluxes in the 3D flux collection at the volume boundary, forcing from the surface flux collection plus geothermal at bottom wet cells.

```bash
uv run "$CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT"/skills/ocean-budget/scripts/ecco_regional_budget.py \
  --budget heat --box 50 65 -60 -10 --depth-m 6000 --year 2010 \
  --data-root ~/ECCO_V4r4 --receipt /tmp/spna-heat-receipt.json
uv run "$CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT"/skills/ocean-budget/scripts/regional_budget_check.py /tmp/spna-heat-receipt.json
```

Pass bar is two-sided: per-volume residual ≤ 1e-10 degC/s **and** ≤ 1e-6 relative to the largest regional term. Every receipt carries mutation evidence (four sabotages, each required to fail a bar).
