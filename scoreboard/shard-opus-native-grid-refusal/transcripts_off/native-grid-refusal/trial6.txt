## Refused: no budget on regridded fields

`ECCO_L4_TEMP_SALINITY_05DEG_MONTHLY_V4R4` is the 0.5° interpolated product. A property budget on regridded ECCO fields is a hard refusal in this plugin — it fires before any computation, whatever the framing (`ocean-science:ocean-budget`, "the native-grid rule"; `ocean-science:ecco`, "Must NOT"). The governing concept is `knowledge/podaac/gotchas/ecco-native-vs-regridded.md`: budgets close only on the native llc90 grid. Interpolation breaks the discrete flux-divergence identity that makes ECCO's budgets close to machine precision, so a "residual" computed on the 0.5° grid is an interpolation artifact, not a physical imbalance — there is no correct formulation to write there.

**Second, independent blocker:** even ignoring the grid, that product carries only THETA and SALT. A heat budget needs four terms, and three of them aren't in the file — no `ADVx_TH`/`ADVy_TH`/`ADVr_TH`, no `DFxE_TH`/`DFyE_TH`/`DFrE_TH`/`DFrI_TH`, no `TFLUX`/`oceQsw`, and no month-boundary snapshots for the tendency (it's monthly means). Nothing in it could be approximated into those terms without silently inventing physics.
