## Refused: this budget cannot be computed on that product

`ECCO_L4_TEMP_SALINITY_05DEG_MONTHLY_V4R4` is the 0.5° interpolated product. A property budget on regridded ECCO fields is a hard refusal in this plugin — it fires at the gate, before any computation, whatever the framing of the request.

**Why** (`knowledge/podaac/gotchas/ecco-native-vs-regridded.md` — budgets close only on the native llc90 grid, cited by `skills/ecco/references/budget-formulation.md:29` and enforced by `skills/ocean-budget/SKILL.md:19-26`): the four-term formulation is defined on the C-grid cell and its faces. Interpolation to 0.5° breaks the discrete divergence — the advective and diffusive convergences no longer sum to the tendency for any cell — so there is no correct budget formulation on that grid. The residual you'd get back would not be a physical imbalance, it would be a regridding artifact, and it would not shrink with care.

A second, independent blocker: that collection carries only `THETA` and `SALT`. It has no advective flux, no diffusive flux and no surface forcing variables, so three of the four terms don't exist in it at all. Nothing recoverable by working harder on the file you have.
