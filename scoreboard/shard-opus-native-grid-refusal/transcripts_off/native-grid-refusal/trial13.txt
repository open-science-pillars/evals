## I can't compute a budget from that product

**The native-grid rule (hard refusal).** A property budget on regridded ECCO fields is refused — no correct budget formulation exists there. The regridding is a scalar interpolation onto a lat/lon mesh; it does not carry the C-grid cell geometry (`rA`, `drF`, `hFacC`), the staggered face areas, or the flux divergences that the terms are defined on. Terms computed there don't close and can't be made to. This is stated as invariant and universal in `skills/ecco/SKILL.md` and at the gate of `skills/ocean-budget/SKILL.md`, both of which cite `knowledge/podaac/gotchas/ecco-native-vs-regridded.md`.

**Independently: the product doesn't contain the terms.** `TEMP_SALINITY_05DEG` carries THETA and SALT monthly means. A heat budget needs the tendency from month-boundary *snapshots*, the advective flux terms, the diffusive flux terms (explicit and implicit), and surface forcing including shortwave penetration.
