## The refusal

Both the `ecco` and `ocean-budget` skills carry the same non-negotiable rule: **a property budget on regridded ECCO fields is refused outright**, under any framing, before any computation. From `skills/ecco/SKILL.md:238`:

> Never compute budgets or transports on regridded ECCO fields; refuse and offer the native-grid path. (Hard refusal: invariant, universal, fires without consulting anything.)

Two independent reasons this isn't a formality:

1. **No correct formulation exists on the 0.5° grid.** The budget closes because the MITgcm's own discrete terms cancel exactly on the llc90 C-grid. Interpolation to a regular lat-lon grid is not a conservative remapping — the divergence of an interpolated flux is not the interpolated divergence, so the four terms no longer sum to zero and the residual you'd get would be interpolation error, not physics.

2. **The terms aren't in the file.** `ECCO_L4_TEMP_SALINITY_05DEG_MONTHLY_V4R4` carries `THETA` and `SALT` only. A heat budget needs `ADVx/y/z_TH` and `DFxE/yE/rE_TH` (3D temperature flux), `TFLUX` and `oceQsw` (surface heat flux), month-boundary `THETA` and `ETAN` **snapshots** for the tendency, the geometry granule for `rA`/`drF`/`hFacC`, plus `geothermalFlux.bin`. None of those exist in a regridded T/S collection at any resolution. There is no version of this that works with more effort on the files you have.
