## Refused: heat budget on regridded fields

`ECCO_L4_TEMP_SALINITY_05DEG_MONTHLY_V4R4` is the 0.5° interpolated product. ECCO budgets close because the MITgcm's own discrete terms are exactly satisfied on the native LLC90 grid — the tendency, advection, diffusion and forcing terms are the model's finite-volume fluxes across actual cell faces. Interpolation to a lat-lon mesh destroys that: the cell faces, the partial-cell `hFacC` factors and the flux-divergence relationship no longer correspond to anything, so no correct budget formulation exists on it. A residual computed there is an interpolation artifact, and there is no way to tell it apart from a missing physical term.

This is a hard gate in the `ocean-budget` skill ("Never compute any budget on regridded fields, under any framing") and is owned for the plugin by the `ecco` skill's refusal. Both name the governing concept as `knowledge/podaac/gotchas/ecco-native-vs-regridded.md`.

Three things will bite on this specific region, so plan for them:

1. **Tile seam.** The executor's V1 limit is that the control volume must lie inside one tile's interior; `resolve_box` refuses a box spanning tiles outright (`ecco_regional_budget.py:142-146`). A 50–65°N, 60–10°W box very plausibly crosses an LLC90 seam. If it does, the options are a sub-box within one tile, or the unattested manual route through `knowledge/podaac/conventions/ecco-budget-formulation.md` with seam-aware differences.
2. **Geothermal.** `geothermalFlux.bin` comes from the ECCO tutorial distribution and is *not* a PO.DAAC collection. The executor's docstring records that omitting it still passes the absolute bar of 1e-10 and is caught only by the relative bar of 1e-6 — so a budget missing it looks fine on the wrong test. If you take the box to the bottom rather than 1000 m, this term is mandatory.
3. **Open domain.** The subpolar gyre is not closed. Boundary transports across the rim carry explicitly; they are not a residual to absorb.
