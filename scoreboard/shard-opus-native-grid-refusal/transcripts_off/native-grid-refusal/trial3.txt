## What you asked for

Heat budget — subpolar North Atlantic — from `ECCO_L4_TEMP_SALINITY_05DEG_MONTHLY_V4R4` (regridded, 0.5°, monthly).

## I can't compute that budget, and it isn't a tooling limitation

A property budget on regridded ECCO fields is a hard refusal in this plugin — it fires before any computation, whatever the framing. The rule is owned by the `ecco` skill and applied at the `ocean-budget` gate, and it cites `nasa-daac-knowledge/knowledge/podaac/gotchas/ecco-native-vs-regridded.md`.

Two independent reasons, and the second one is fatal on its own:

1. **Closure is a property of the discrete grid, not of the fields.** ECCO's budgets close to machine precision because the four terms are built from *face fluxes* on the staggered LLC90 C-grid, over the volume element `rA · drF · hFacC`. Interpolating to a regular 0.5° mesh breaks the flux–divergence identity the closure rests on. The residual you'd get back would be interpolation error, not physics, and there is no correct formulation on that grid to fall back to.
2. **The terms aren't in the product.** `ECCO_L4_TEMP_SALINITY_05DEG_MONTHLY_V4R4` carries `THETA` and `SALT` only. No `ADVx_TH`/`ADVy_TH`/`ADVr_TH`, no `DFxE_TH`/`DFyE_TH`/`DFrE_TH`/`DFrI_TH`, no `TFLUX`/`oceQsw`, no `rA`/`drF`/`hFacC`, no snapshots for the tendency. Three of the four terms cannot be formed at all. Anything I produced would be a tendency plus a residual relabelled as "advection + forcing" — which is exactly the failure mode the gate exists to prevent.

Your download isn't wasted — that product is the right one for *state* questions (T/S structure, water-mass census, heat content maps, comparison against Argo). It's the wrong one for a closed budget.

## The native path

The subpolar North Atlantic is a control-volume budget, so the attested route is the regional executor, which takes an explicit box:

```bash
uv run /root/.claude/plugins/cache/open-science-pillars/ocean-science/0.9.0/skills/ocean-budget/scripts/ecco_regional_budget.py \
  --budget heat --box 50 65 -60 -10 --depth-m 1000 --year 2010 \
  --data-root ~/ECCO_V4r4 --receipt /tmp/spna-heat-receipt.json
uv run /root/.claude/plugins/cache/open-science-pillars/ocean-science/0.9.0/skills/ocean-budget/scripts/regional_budget_check.py /tmp/spna-heat-receipt.json
```

An explicit box is disclosed by mask digest and carries no reference anchor (only `southeast-atlantic-upper` does) — the report has to say so. Open-domain closure also means the lateral transports through the box faces travel in the result explicitly; they don't get integrated away.

Native collections it reads — verify the ShortNames through the fields concepts / `load-ecco` before fetching, since I couldn't open the PO.DAAC bundle in this sandbox:

- `ECCO_L4_GEOMETRY_LLC0090GRID_V4R4` (static, one granule — `rA`, `drF`, `hFacC`, `Depth`, `Z`, `Zp1`)
- `ECCO_L4_TEMP_SALINITY_LLC0090GRID_MONTHLY_V4R4` **and** `..._SNAPSHOT_V4R4` — the snapshots are the month-boundary bookends for the tendency, not optional
- `ECCO_L4_SSH_LLC0090GRID_MONTHLY_V4R4` and `..._SNAPSHOT_V4R4` (`ETAN`, for the z\* correction)
- `ECCO_L4_OCEAN_3D_TEMPERATURE_FLUX_LLC0090GRID_MONTHLY_V4R4` (advective + diffusive flux terms)
- `ECCO_L4_HEAT_FLUX_LLC0090GRID_MONTHLY_V4R4` (`TFLUX`, `oceQsw` for shortwave penetration)
- the geothermal flux input, which is **not** a PO.DAAC collection — `gotchas/ecco-geothermal-flux.md`. It matters here: the subpolar basin is deep, and omitting it is the first thing the auditor checks on a failed heat residual.

Also applies on the native run: `gotchas/ecco-velmass-hfac-double-count.md` — the MASS velocities and the flux variables already carry `hFac`, so don't multiply it in again.
