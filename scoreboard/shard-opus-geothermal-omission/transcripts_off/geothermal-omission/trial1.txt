## Terms to compute

Control-volume form (what a basin budget needs), per `ecco_regional_budget.py`. Volume element `rA · drF · hFacC`; `rhoConst = 1029.0`, `c_p = 3994.0`.

1. **Tendency (LHS).** Σ over the volume of `d(s*·THETA)/dt · rA·drF·hFacC`, with the z* factor `s* = 1 + ETAN/Depth` from the *bracketing snapshots* and Δt in seconds per month. Snapshots, never monthly means — 13 of them for 12 months.
2. **Lateral rim transport.** Raw face fluxes `ADVx_TH + DFxE_TH` and `ADVy_TH + DFyE_TH` summed on the four boundary faces (west in, east out, south in, north out), read **at the faces, never derived from a divergence field** — the telescoping identity is algebra, not evidence.
3. **Vertical face flux.** `ADVr_TH + DFrE_TH + DFrI_TH` at the bottom face of the volume minus the surface face, bottom zero-padded.
4. **Surface forcing with shortwave penetration.** `TFLUX` at level 1 minus the penetrating fraction of `oceQsw`, plus the subsurface divergence of the penetrating shortwave: `q(z) = R·exp(z/ζ₁) + (1−R)·exp(z/ζ₂)` with `R = 0.62`, `ζ₁ = 0.6 m`, `ζ₂ = 20 m`, zeroed below 200 m. Divided by `rhoConst·c_p·hFacC·drF`.
5. **Geothermal.** Bottom-wet-cell mask (`mskC − mskC_shifted_down`) × `geothermalFlux.bin`, same normalization. Reported as its own integral (`geo_int`) in the receipt.
6. **Residual.** `LHS − (rim + vertical + forcing)`, monthly.

If you also want the pointwise field (closure at every wet cell, every month, as a map rather than an integral), that is `ecco_heat_budget.py`'s four-term form — same physics, convergences taken within the tile interior.

Optional companion, same input set: **Reynolds decomposition** of the advective term (`ecco_flux_decomposition.py`, `full-four-term` / `time-mean-eddy` / `anomaly`) to split the rim transport into mean and eddy contributions. Worth it in this basin — it's an eddy-energetic region — but it's a separate attested run.

## Pass bars and attestation

Both bars required, from the attester's contract:
- absolute: max monthly |residual| / volume ≤ **1e-10 °C/s**
- relative: max monthly |residual| / largest term ≤ **1e-6**

The executor reruns four sabotaged variants — `geothermal-omitted`, `rim-west-face-shifted`, `vertical-face-sign-flipped`, `vertical-faces-omitted` — and writes **no receipt at all** if a structural mutation passes or the correct run fails. Then `regional_budget_check.py` re-checks both bars from the receipt, the code hash, the collection list and constants, and the mutation consistency.

Two things to expect in the report: an explicit `--box` **carries no reference anchor** (the attester's wet-cell/volume/residual anchors apply only to `southeast-atlantic-upper`, 2010), and the resolved wet mask is disclosed by sha256 only — no oracle checks the mask is the water you meant, so the resolved lat/lon extent and cell count are the disclosure you have to eyeball.

## Decisions and one likely blocker

1. **🔴 Tile seam — the real risk.** The regional executor is v1-limited to a control volume inside **one tile's interior** (indices 1–89); a box spanning a seam is refused by design. The registered South Atlantic region (tile 1, i 20–60) spans about 18°W–22°E. Linearly extrapolating that to the tile edge puts tile 1's western limit near ~38°W — which would put most of the Argentine Basin (centred ~45°W) on tile 0 or straddling the 0/1 seam. That extrapolation is crude on a curvilinear grid and must be settled by running `resolve_box` against the geometry granule, which is the *first* thing I'd do after fetching collection 1. If it refuses, the options are: run the largest sub-box that resolves within one tile and state plainly it is not the whole basin; or fall back to the pointwise closure (which holds on any spatial subset) and forgo the integrated transports; or wait for the seam-calibrated section machinery.
2. **Full depth via an explicit box.** `--depth-m` snaps to the nearest stored cell face; passing ~6135 m resolves to all 50 levels. The index rectangle will include shoaling cells, which is correct — `hFacC` handles partial cells, and bottom cells drive the geothermal term.
3. **Period.** The executor binds **one year per run**. 2010 is the fixture year and the cheap smoke test; the full 1992–2017 record is 26 runs and 26 receipts.
