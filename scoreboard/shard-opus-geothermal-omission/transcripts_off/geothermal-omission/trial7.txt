## The rule that fires regardless

The budget is computed on the **native llc90 grid only**. No regridded/interpolated ECCO product (the `0p50DEG` family) can close a budget, under any framing — that refusal doesn't depend on any lookup. Everything below is native.

## Domain and period (my assumptions — say if wrong)

| | |
|---|---|
| Box ⚠️ | 55°S–35°S, 65°W–20°W (Argentine Basin proper; abyssal plain + Zapiola Rise) |
| Depth | Full column, surface to bottom (~6000 m; ECCO v4r4 has 50 levels ⚠️) |
| Period | Full record 1992–2017, monthly |
| Control volume | **Open** on all four lateral faces and closed at the bottom |

Two consequences that shape the whole plan:

1. **Open domain** → a domain-integrated closure statement is not assertable on its own. Lateral boundary transports across all four faces must be carried as explicit terms, not folded into a residual. (Pointwise closure at every wet cell is still the real test.)
2. **Full depth** → the **geothermal flux term is mandatory** at the bottom cell. This is the first thing `budget-auditor` checks on a failed deep heat budget, and the Argentine Basin is ~6 km deep over most of the box, so it is not negligible here.

### The one input that is *not* a PO.DAAC collection

**Geothermal heat flux.** ⚠️ It's an MITgcm forcing input (`geothermalFlux.bin`, from the ECCO v4r4 input/forcing archive), not a PO.DAAC granule — that's exactly the gotcha `ecco-geothermal-flux` exists to catch. A full-depth budget that skips it fails closure with a residual concentrated in bottom cells. This must be fetched separately and confirmed present *before* the run, not discovered at audit time.

## Terms to compute ⚠️

Per-cell, units °C/s, on volume element `rA · drF · hFacC`. The formulation authority is `knowledge/podaac/conventions/ecco-budget-formulation.md`, which I could not read — so this term set is reconstructed and must be checked against it before coding.

**G_total = G_advection + G_diffusion + G_forcing**

1. **Tendency `G_total`** — `∂(s*·θ)/∂t` from consecutive month-boundary θ snapshots, with `s* = 1 + ETAN/Depth` from the ETAN snapshots. The z\* correction is not optional; monthly means cannot substitute for snapshots here.
2. **Advection `G_advection`** — `−∇·(u θ)`: horizontal convergence of `ADVx_TH`, `ADVy_TH`; vertical convergence of `ADVr_TH`, bottom face zero-padded.
3. **Diffusion `G_diffusion`** — explicit `DFxE_TH`, `DFyE_TH`, `DFrE_TH` plus implicit vertical `DFrI_TH`, same convergence and padding.
4. **Forcing `G_forcing`** — three pieces:
   - surface heat flux `TFLUX` in the top cell,
   - **shortwave penetration**: `oceQsw` redistributed over depth rather than applied at k=0,
   - **geothermal** added to the bottom wet cell of each column.
   - Constants ⚠️: `rhoconst = 1029 kg m⁻³`, `c_p = 3994 J kg⁻¹ K⁻¹`.
5. **Lateral boundary transports** (because the box is open) — face-integrated `ADVx/ADVy` + `DFxE/DFyE` across each of the four box faces, reported as four explicit terms, never absorbed.
6. **Residual** — `G_total − (G_adv + G_diff + G_forcing)`, judged **pointwise in absolute units** at every wet cell and every month. Not as a fraction of the dominant term: on a float32 archive a relative ratio is not a closure criterion.

**Two traps to honor while coding:**
- **hFac double count** — `ADV*`/`DF*` and the `*VELMASS` velocities already carry `hFac`. Do not multiply by it again.
- **Tile seams** — horizontal differences must be tile-aware, or restricted to tile-interior cells. The Argentine Basin box straddles llc90 tile boundaries ⚠️, so this is a live concern here, not a formality.

## How I'd execute it

The attested route is `ecco_regional_budget.py --budget heat` with its attester `regional_budget_check.py`. Important limitation: the only **registered** region is `southeast-atlantic-upper`. The Argentine Basin is not registered, so it runs as an explicit box:

```
--budget heat --box -55 -35 -65 -20 --depth-m <full> --year <YYYY>
```

An explicit box is disclosed by **mask digest and carries no anchor** — meaning the attester checks the internal consistency bars but has no reference wet-cell count, volume, or residual to check against. The report has to say so plainly. (The anchored whole-grid heat budget `ecco-heat-budget`, with bars `residual_max ≤ 1e-10` and `residual_p999 ≤ 1e-11` °C/s, is `tile1-interior` only — it can't be pointed at this basin.)

Sequence: fetch 2010 only → stamp the tree with `science_record_verify.py` (every attester refuses an unstamped tree) → run one year → attest → only then pull the full 1992–2017 record.
