`geothermalFlux.bin` — the llc90 binary from the ECCO v4 tutorial distribution, read with `ecco_v4_py.read_llc_to_tiles(data_root, "geothermalFlux.bin")`.

This is the single most important item on the page for *your* domain. The executor's own measurement (`ecco_regional_budget.py:20-22`): omitting geothermal **passes** the absolute bar of 1e-10 and is caught **only by the relative bar**. In the plugin's 300 m reference box it is genuinely negligible (2.1e-7 relative) and the mutation records `applicable: false`. The Argentine Basin full-depth is the opposite case — every column has a bottom wet cell over abyssal plain, so geothermal is a first-order term for the deep layer. If your run's `geothermal-omitted` mutation comes back `applicable: false`, your mask is wrong, not the physics.

## 6. Terms to compute

Constants, verbatim from the sanctioned code: `rhoConst = 1029.0` kg m⁻³, `Cp = 3994.0` J kg⁻¹ K⁻¹, shortwave `R = 0.62`, `ζ₁ = 0.6` m, `ζ₂ = 20.0` m.
Volume element: `V = rA · drF · hFacC` (partial cells in, applied once).

**Term 1 — tendency.** From bracketing snapshots only.
```
s*   = 1 + ETAN/Depth                        (z* free-surface scale factor)
G_total = Δ(s*·THETA) / Δt                   Δt in seconds, per month
```

**Term 2 — advective convergence.** `ADVx_TH`, `ADVy_TH` (horizontal, at `i_g`/`j_g` faces), `ADVr_TH` (vertical, at `k_l`), bottom face zero-padded.
```
G_adv = −(∇_h·ADV_h + ∂_z ADVr_TH) / V
```

**Term 3 — diffusive convergence.** `DFxE_TH`, `DFyE_TH` horizontal; `DFrE_TH + DFrI_TH` (explicit + implicit) vertical.
```
G_dif = −(∇_h·DF_h + ∂_z(DFrE_TH + DFrI_TH)) / V
```

**Term 4 — forcing**, three pieces:
```
q(z)     = R·exp(z/ζ₁) + (1−R)·exp(z/ζ₂)     zeroed below 200 m
surface  = TFLUX − (1 − (q₁[0] − q₂[0]))·oceQsw
subsurf  = (q₁·mskC − q₂·mskC_dn)·oceQsw     shortwave penetration
geotherm = geoflx · mskB                      bottom wet cell only
G_forc   = (forcH + geotherm) / (rhoConst·Cp) / (hFacC·drF)
```

**Residual.** `G_total − (G_adv + G_dif + G_forc)`, evaluated pointwise on wet cells, in absolute units. Never a relative-to-dominant-term ratio on a float32 archive.

### The control-volume form — what "Argentine Basin" actually needs

An open basin is not a pointwise problem. The regional executor integrates and carries the boundary explicitly:

- **LHS** = Σ(`G_total` · V) over the volume
- **Rim** = raw face fluxes (`ADV*_TH + DF*_TH`) summed over the four lateral faces — read as *raw faces at the boundary*, never as the divergence field. The executor is emphatic (`:12-16`): the telescoping identity is algebra, not evidence. This is your lateral heat transport, and it is the physically interesting number: Malvinas/Brazil Confluence inflow in the north-west, Antarctic Circumpolar Current exchange in the south.
- **Vert** = top face − bottom face. **For a full-depth volume the bottom face is the seafloor**, so it zero-pads out and `Vert` reduces to the surface face. This is precisely why geothermal must live in the forcing term: it is the only non-zero bottom energy input.
- **Forc** = surface + shortwave penetration + geothermal, integrated.
- **Residual** = LHS − (Rim + Vert + Forc)

## 8. Closure criteria

Regional (control volume) — **both bars required**, measured 2026-08-31:

- absolute: max monthly |residual| / volume ≤ **1e-10** °C s⁻¹
- relative: max monthly |residual| / largest term ≤ **1e-6**

Pointwise (whole grid): `residual_max` ≤ 1e-10, `residual_p999` ≤ 1e-11 °C s⁻¹.

The executor also writes **mutation evidence** — it re-runs four sabotaged variants (geothermal omitted, rim west face shifted one cell, vertical face sign flipped, vertical faces omitted) and records that each failed a bar. If a structural mutation passes, or the correct run fails, **no receipt is written at all**. A test that cannot fail is not evidence.
