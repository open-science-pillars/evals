> "V1 LIMIT, stated: the volume must lie within one tile's interior (faces 1 through 89). A box crossing a tile seam is refused with a clear message" — `ecco_regional_budget.py:73-75`

The registered South Atlantic volume in that same file maps tile 1, `i` 20–60 → **18°W to 22°E** (`ecco_regional_budget.py:122-127`), i.e. roughly 1° per `i` cell, putting tile 1's western edge near **38°W**. ## 4. The input that is *not* a PO.DAAC collection

**`geothermalFlux.bin`** — ECCO tutorial distribution, read via `ecco.read_llc_to_tiles()` (`ecco_regional_budget.py:308-310`). The receipt records it as *"geothermalFlux.bin, ECCO tutorial distribution, not a PO.DAAC collection"* (`:410-412`).

This matters more for you than for most budgets: at full depth **every column contributes a bottom cell**, so geothermal is fully active. The executor's own measurement: *"omitting geothermal passes an absolute bar of 1e-10 and is caught only by the relative bar"* (`ecco_regional_budget.py:20-22`). A full-depth budget without this file will look closed on the absolute bar and be wrong.

## 5. Every term computed

All integrated over the control volume; heat terms in degC·m³/s, residual reported per unit volume in degC/s.

**Term 1 — Tendency (LHS).** `d(s*·θ)/dt` from bracketing snapshots, with the z* scale factor `s* = 1 + ETAN/Depth`, differenced over the true seconds between snapshots, times `rA·drF·hFacC`. (`:231-246`)

**Term 2 — Lateral rim transport.** Raw face fluxes on the four boundary faces: `ADVx_TH + DFxE_TH` on west/east, `ADVy_TH + DFyE_TH` on south/north (`:250-251`, `:277-283`). Read as **raw faces, never as a divergence** — *"the telescoping identity is algebra, not evidence"* (`:13-16`). Advection and diffusion are summed here; keep them separate if you want them reported individually.

**Term 3 — Vertical transport.** `ADVr_TH + DFrE_TH + DFrI_TH` at the top face minus the bottom face, bottom zero-padded (`:252-254`, `:275`, `:284-285`).

**Term 4 — Forcing.**
- surface: `TFLUX − (1 − (q1[0] − q2[0]))·oceQsw` at k=0
- subsurface shortwave penetration: `(q1 − q2)·oceQsw`, with `q(z) = R·exp(z/ζ1) + (1−R)·exp(z/ζ2)` evaluated on cell faces and **zeroed below −200 m** (`:294-298`)
- **geothermal** at bottom wet cells only, via `mskb = mskc − mskc_dn`
- all divided by `rhoConst·Cp` and by `hFacC·drF` (`:299-315`)

**Residual** = Term1 − (Term2 + Term3 + Term4), per month (`:350`).

**Constants** (`ecco_regional_budget.py:97-98`): `rhoConst = 1029.0` kg/m³, `Cp = 3994.0` J/kg/K, `R_SW = 0.62`, `ζ1 = 0.6` m, `ζ2 = 20.0` m.

Two traps the code encodes that are easy to get wrong by hand: the flux variables **already carry hFacC** — do not re-apply the partial-cell factor; and dry-cell values are zeroed before the vertical difference rather than trusted (`:274`).

## 6. Pass bars and evidence

Both required (`ecco_regional_budget.py:19-25`, `:99-103`):
- **absolute:** max monthly |residual| / volume ≤ **1e-10** degC/s
- **relative:** max monthly |residual| / largest term ≤ **1e-6**

The executor additionally reruns **four sabotaged variants** — geothermal omitted, rim west face shifted one cell, vertical face sign flipped, vertical faces omitted — and refuses to write a receipt if a structural mutation passes (`:44-55`, `:364-396`). At full depth I expect all four to be caught, including geothermal.

**Attester anchors do not apply to an explicit box.** `regional_budget_check.py:26-30` anchors wet-cell count, volume and residual only for `southeast-atlantic-upper`/2010. An explicit box attests on the bars, the contract and the mutation evidence, and is disclosed by mask sha256 + geometry sha256 — the report has to say the mask is disclosed, not oracle-checked (`ecco_regional_budget.py:449-451`).
