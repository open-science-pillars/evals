## Every term to compute

Constants, read from the executor: `RHOCONST = 1029.0`, `C_P = 3994.0`, `R_SW = 0.62`, `ZETA1 = 0.6`, `ZETA2 = 20.0`. Volume element `V = rA · drF · hFacC` (partial cells in — and `hFacC` is applied **once**; `*MASS` flux variables already carry it).

**1. Tendency — `G_total`**, from 13 month-boundary snapshots (not from monthly means):

```
s*      = 1 + ETAN/Depth          (z* free-surface scale factor)
G_total = Δ(s* · THETA)/Δt
```

**2. Advection — `G_adv`**: convergence of `ADVx_TH`, `ADVy_TH` horizontally and `ADVr_TH` vertically, ÷ V. Dry cells zeroed, **bottom face zero-padded**.

**3. Diffusion — `G_dif`**: convergence of `DFxE_TH`, `DFyE_TH` horizontally and **`DFrE_TH + DFrI_TH`** vertically — explicit *and* implicit, summed. Dropping `DFrI_TH` is a classic non-closure.

**4. Forcing — `G_forc`**, three pieces:

```
q(z)      = R_SW·exp(RF/ZETA1) + (1-R_SW)·exp(RF/ZETA2)   at cell faces,
            zeroed below Z < -200 m
surface:  TFLUX - (1 - (q1[0]-q2[0]))·oceQsw
subsurf:  (q1-q2)·oceQsw
bottom:   geothermalFlux.bin on bottom wet cells (mask = maskC - maskC_below)
G_forc    = (forcing + geothermal) / (RHOCONST·C_P·hFacC·drF)
```

**Residual:** `|G_total - (G_adv + G_dif + G_forc)|`, pointwise, at every wet cell and every month, in absolute units. Never a ratio to the dominant term on a float32 archive; never absorbed into a term.

For the **control-volume** (basin-integrated) form, the rim transports come from **raw face fluxes on the boundary faces**, never from the divergence field — the executor's reasoning is that the telescoping identity is algebra, not evidence. The Argentine Basin is an open domain, so those boundary transports are reported explicitly.

### Pass bars — verbatim from the attesters

Pointwise (`budget_residual.py`):
> `residual_max <= 1e-10 degC/s and residual_p999 <= 1e-11 degC/s, the tolerances recorded by the heat-budget recipe (measured 2026-07-04, 2x headroom over the measured max).`

Regional, **two bars, both required** (`ecco_regional_budget.py`):
> `absolute: max monthly |residual| / volume <= 1e-10 degC per s` … `relative: max monthly |residual| / largest term <= 1e-6.`
> `(measured 2026-08-31: omitting geothermal passes an absolute bar of 1e-10 and is caught only by the relative bar)`

That parenthetical is the single most important line for a full-depth budget: **an absolute-only check will not catch a missing geothermal term.** The executor also re-runs four sabotaged variants (geothermal omitted, rim west face shifted, vertical face sign flipped, vertical faces omitted) and refuses to write a receipt if a mutation passes. On a full-depth basin the `geothermal-omitted` mutation must come back `caught: true` — if it reports "not applicable," the mask is wrong, not the physics.
