# Heat budget plan — Argentine Basin, ECCO v4r4, full depth

## 2. Terms to compute

All integrated over the control volume; volume element `rA · drF · hFacC` (partial cells in). Constants from the sanctioned code: `rhoConst = 1029.0`, `c_p = 3994.0`, shortwave `R = 0.62`, `ζ₁ = 0.6 m`, `ζ₂ = 20 m`.

**Term 1 — tendency (LHS).** `d(s*·THETA)/dt` where `s* = 1 + ETAN/Depth`, differenced across bracketing snapshots and divided by the exact seconds in each month. Integrated: `Σ G_total · vol`. The z\* scale factor is what makes the free surface consistent; dropping it is a classic non-closure.

**Term 2 — lateral transport across the rim.** Raw face fluxes at the four boundary faces, *not* a divergence field:
`(ADVx_TH + DFxE_TH)` at west face `i0` minus east face `i1`, plus `(ADVy_TH + DFyE_TH)` at south face `j0` minus north face `j1`, summed over all levels and the face span. Using the divergence instead makes closure a telescoping algebraic identity rather than evidence — the executor is explicit about this.

**Term 3 — vertical transport.** `(ADVr_TH + DFrE_TH + DFrI_TH)` at the bottom face `k = K` minus the top face `k = 0`. At full depth (`K = 50`) the bottom face is the zero pad, so the volume is closed below and Term 3 reduces to the surface-face contribution.

**Term 4 — surface forcing with shortwave penetration.** Two-band exponential profile `q(z) = R·exp(z/ζ₁) + (1−R)·exp(z/ζ₂)` evaluated on cell faces, zeroed below 200 m; surface level gets `TFLUX − (1 − (q₁−q₂))·oceQsw`, subsurface levels get the penetrative difference. Divided by `rhoConst · c_p · hFacC · drF`.

**Term 5 — geothermal.** `geothermalFlux.bin` applied at bottom wet cells only (mask = `hFacC>0` minus its downward shift), same `rhoConst · c_p · hFacC · drF` normalization. Reported separately in the receipt as `geo_int`.

**Residual:** `Term1 − (Term2 + Term3 + Term4 + Term5)`, judged against **two** bars: absolute `max|residual|/volume ≤ 1e-10 °C/s`, and relative `max|residual|/largest_term ≤ 1e-6`.

**Mutation evidence (automatic, 4 sabotage reruns):** geothermal omitted, rim west face shifted one cell, vertical face sign flipped, vertical faces omitted. If a structural mutation *passes*, no receipt is written — a test that cannot fail is not evidence.

## 3. Two blockers specific to the Argentine Basin

**(a) It is not a registered region, so it gets no reference anchor.** The only registered control volume is `southeast-atlantic-upper`. The Argentine Basin must go in as an explicit `--box LAT0 LAT1 LON0 LON1 --depth-m D`, which the attester accepts but anchors nothing against — it checks the two bars and mutation consistency, and the wet-cell/volume/residual anchors apply only to the reference configuration. The receipt discloses a sha256 of the resolved wet mask; that disclosure *is* the answer, since no oracle can confirm the mask is the water you meant.

**(b) The box is an index rectangle on one tile, and the basin may not fit.** `resolve_box` takes the smallest index rectangle containing every cell centre in your lat/lon box, on **one tile**, with `1 ≤ j,i ≤ 89`. A box spanning a tile seam is refused by design.

This is a real go/no-go. The registered tile-1 volume spans ~44°S–10°S, 18°W–22°E at `j 20–60, i 20–60`. Extrapolating linearly — **an estimate, not a verified fact** — tile 1's western edge sits near ~38°W. The Argentine Basin (roughly 50°S–35°S, 60°W–20°W) would then run off the west side of tile 1 into tile 0 and be **refused**.

Settling this is step 0 and costs one static granule: load the geometry, print `XC`/`YC` per tile, and check whether your box lands inside one tile's interior.

If it does not fit, the options are: shrink to the tile-1 portion (loses the western basin, which defeats the purpose); or compute per the formulation convention with tile-aware operators (`ecco_v4_py.get_llc_grid`/xgcm) and report the result as **unattested** — the seam-calibrated machinery the executor defers to has not landed.

## 4. Why full depth changes the geothermal story

For the 300 m reference box, omitting geothermal leaves the residual at 2.1e-7 relative — below the bar, so the executor marks that mutation "not applicable." A full-depth Argentine Basin volume is nearly all bottom cells along the abyssal plain, so geothermal becomes a leading deep-cell term and the `geothermal-omitted` mutation **should be caught**. If your receipt comes back marking it inapplicable at full depth, that is a signal the volume didn't resolve the way you intended, not a pass.
