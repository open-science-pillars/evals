## 4. Terms to compute

Volume element `V = rA · drF · hFacC`. **Do not** multiply `hFac` in again on the flux or `*VELMASS` variables — they already carry it (`gotchas/ecco-velmass-hfac-double-count.md`). Constants, carried verbatim by the sanctioned code: `rhoConst = 1029`, `c_p = 3994`, `R_SW = 0.62`, `ζ1 = 0.6 m`, `ζ2 = 20 m`.

**Term 1 — tendency.** `G_total = d(s*·THETA)/dt`, with the z* scale factor `s* = 1 + ETAN/Depth`, differenced across bracketing month-boundary snapshots, `dt` in seconds. Never from monthly-mean THETA.

**Term 2 — advective convergence.** `G_adv = −[Δi ADVx_TH + Δj ADVy_TH + Δk ADVr_TH] / V`. For the regional integral these are read as **raw face fluxes on the rim** (west minus east, south minus north, plus the vertical faces), never from a divergence field — the telescoping identity is algebra, not evidence. With `K = 50` the bottom vertical face is the zero pad, so no flux through the sea floor.

**Term 3 — diffusive convergence.** `G_dif = −[Δi DFxE_TH + Δj DFyE_TH + Δk (DFrE_TH + DFrI_TH)] / V` — explicit and implicit vertical diffusion both, same rim treatment.

**Term 4 — forcing**, three pieces:
- surface: `TFLUX − (1 − (q1[0] − q2[0]))·oceQsw` at level 0;
- shortwave penetration below level 0: `(q1_k − q2_k)·oceQsw` with `q = R_SW·exp(z/ζ1) + (1−R_SW)·exp(z/ζ2)` at the cell faces, zeroed below 200 m;
- geothermal: `geothermalFlux` applied at the bottom wet cell (`mskb = mskC − mskC_down`);

all divided by `rhoConst · c_p · hFacC · drF`.

**Residual.** `G_total − (G_adv + G_dif + G_forc)`, evaluated pointwise at every wet cell and month, and volume-integrated over the control volume for the regional form. Never absorbed into a term, never averaged away.

**Optional add-on:** Reynolds decomposition of the advective flux into mean/eddy (`ecco-flux-decomposition`, `skills/ocean-budget/scripts/ecco_flux_decomposition.py`) — but note it binds `region` to registered names only (`southeast-atlantic-upper`), so an Argentine Basin box is not supported there without a registry entry.

## 5. Pass bars and attestation

From `ecco_regional_budget.py:20-42` — **both** required:

- absolute: `max monthly |residual| / volume ≤ 1e-10 °C s⁻¹`
- relative: `max monthly |residual| / largest term ≤ 1e-6`

The receipt carries `run_id`, `code_sha256`, `data` (tree + `RECORD.json` stamp), `bound_parameters`, `resolved_volume` (mask digest, geometry digest, wet- and bottom-cell counts), `results`, `mutation_evidence` (four sabotaged reruns: geothermal omitted, rim west face shifted, vertical face sign flipped, vertical faces omitted — if a structural mutation passes, no receipt is written) and `caveats`. An explicit box carries **no anchor**, so the attester's two-sided reference checks don't apply — the report must say so.
