# Plan: full-depth heat budget, Argentine Basin, ECCO v4r4

## Step 0 — the gate that fires before anything

Budgets are computed on the **native llc90 grid only**. The 0.5° regridded collections (`ECCO_L4_*_05DEG_*`) are a hard refusal for budget work — no correct formulation exists there. They are not in any list below, deliberately.

## Step 1 — resolve the domain before downloading anything (geometry only, ~8 MB)

This is the cheapest test and it gates the whole plan. The sanctioned executor has a **v1 single-tile limit**: the control volume must lie inside one tile's interior (`j`, `i` ∈ [1, 89]). A box crossing a tile seam is refused by design.

Proposed box — Argentine Basin bounded by the Rio Grande Rise, the Mid-Atlantic Ridge, the Falkland/North Scotia Ridge and the Argentine margin:

```
--box -50 -35 -60 -20 --depth-m 6135
```

**I can't tell you yet which tile that lands on, and there is a real chance it straddles a seam.** For reference, the one registered South Atlantic region (`southeast-atlantic-upper`) sits on tile 1 at `j 20–60, i 20–60` ≈ 44°S–10°S, 18°W–22°E — the Argentine Basin is well west of that, which is exactly where a seam becomes likely. Resolve it by pulling `ECCO_L4_GEOMETRY_LLC0090GRID_V4R4` and running the executor's box resolver against `XC`/`YC`. If it refuses, the fallbacks are: shrink the box to the tile-interior portion and report the truncated domain honestly, or wait for the seam-calibrated section machinery the executor says is not yet landed.

Two further properties of box resolution to own in the writeup: the resolver takes the **smallest index rectangle containing every cell whose center falls in the box**, which on a curvilinear grid is a *superset* of your geographic box; and an explicit box **carries no reference anchor**, so the attester's PASS is bars-plus-mutation-consistency only, weaker than the registered-region PASS.

`--depth-m 6135` snaps to the deepest stored face, giving `k_cells = 50` — genuinely full depth, and the bottom face is zero-padded so nothing leaks through the seafloor.

## Step 3 — terms to compute

Volume element `rA · drF · hFacC`. Constants `rhoConst = 1029.0`, `Cp = 3994.0`. Never reapply `hFacC` to a `*MASS` or flux variable — it is already carried.

**Tendency (LHS)** — ∂(s\* Θ)/∂t, with `s* = 1 + ETAN/Depth` from bracketing snapshots, differenced over the true seconds between them, integrated over V.

**RHS, four terms:**

1. **Horizontal rim convergence** — `(ADVx_TH + DFxE_TH)` on the west and east faces, `(ADVy_TH + DFyE_TH)` on south and north. Taken as **raw face fluxes at the boundary**, never derived from a divergence field — the telescoping identity is algebra, not evidence.
2. **Vertical faces** — `(ADVr_TH + DFrE_TH + DFrI_TH)` at the top face and at `k = K`. Full depth ⇒ the bottom face is zero.
3. **Surface forcing** — `TFLUX` with the penetrating shortwave removed at the surface, plus the shortwave divergence per subsurface layer: `q(z) = R·exp(z/ζ₁) + (1−R)·exp(z/ζ₂)`, `R = 0.62`, `ζ₁ = 0.6 m`, `ζ₂ = 20 m`, truncated below 200 m. Divided by `rhoConst·Cp`.
4. **Geothermal** — `geothermalFlux` applied at the bottom-most wet cell of each column, divided by `rhoConst·Cp`.

**Residual** = tendency − (1 + 2 + 3 + 4), evaluated monthly.

## Step 4 — closure bars

Both required, from the heat contract: **absolute** max|residual|/volume ≤ `1e-10` °C/s, **relative** max|residual|/largest term ≤ `1e-6`.

The executor also reruns four sabotaged variants and records that each tripped a bar — geothermal omitted, rim west face shifted one cell, vertical face sign flipped, vertical faces omitted. If a structural mutation passes, **no receipt is written**. On your full-depth box the geothermal mutation should be caught outright rather than marked inapplicable; if it comes back "not applicable," that is a signal your volume isn't reaching the seafloor.

## Step 5 — audit and report

`budget-auditor` runs on the result whether or not it closes. The report carries all four terms with units, the residual against both bars, the resolved volume disclosure (tile, index bounds, depth face, lat/lon extent, wet and bottom cell counts, mask SHA-256, geometry SHA-256), the attester verdict and run id, runtime `claude-code`, and an explicit note that an explicit box carries no reference anchor. A failed budget is reported as failed with the diagnosis — never with the residual absorbed into a term.
