# Full-depth ECCO v4r4 heat budget — Argentine Basin

## 1. Domain and the route

Argentine Basin ≈ **55°S–35°S, 60°W–20°W**, full depth.

The attested route is `ecco-regional-heat-budget` — `skills/ocean-budget/scripts/ecco_regional_budget.py`, attested by `regional_budget_check.py`. Not the pointwise `ecco_heat_budget.py`: that one binds `region` to a registry whose only value is `tile1-interior`, so it cannot express this basin.

The basin is not a registered region, so it is an **explicit box**:

```
--budget heat --box -55 -35 -60 -20 --depth-m 6135 --year YYYY
```

`--depth-m` snaps to the nearest stored cell face. Any value at or below the deepest face (ECCO v4r4's 50th face, ≈6134 m — confirm from `Zp1` in the geometry granule) yields `k_cells = 50`, i.e. full depth.

## 2. PO.DAAC collections to download

Native **llc90** only. Five collections, all `V4R4`:

| # | ShortName | Cadence | Variables used | Role |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `ECCO_L4_GEOMETRY_LLC0090GRID_V4R4` | static, 1 granule | `rA`, `drF`, `hFacC`, `Depth`, `Z`, `Zp1`, `YC`, `XC` | volume element, masks, box resolution |
| 2 | `ECCO_L4_TEMP_SALINITY_LLC0090GRID_SNAPSHOT_V4R4` | snapshot | `THETA` | tendency |

| 4 | `ECCO_L4_OCEAN_3D_TEMPERATURE_FLUX_LLC0090GRID_MONTHLY_V4R4` | monthly | `ADVx_TH`, `ADVy_TH`, `ADVr_TH`, `DFxE_TH`, `DFyE_TH`, `DFrE_TH`, `DFrI_TH` | rim + vertical transport |

Collection 1 is static, and per the access-quirk the ecco skill flags, it does *not* load through `ecco_access` with a date range; fetch it with `earthaccess`.

## 3. Terms to compute

Integrated over the control volume, in °C·m³/s. Volume element `rA · drF · hFacC`.

**LHS — tendency**

$$\text{LHS} = \sum_{\text{vol}} \frac{\Delta(s^*\,\Theta)}{\Delta t}\,\cdot V,\qquad s^* = 1 + \frac{\text{ETAN}}{\text{Depth}}$$

from **bracketing month-boundary snapshots** (13 per year: Jan 1 of *Y* through Jan 1 of *Y*+1), differenced in seconds.

**RHS term 1 — lateral rim convergence.** Raw face fluxes on the four boundary faces, never the divergence field:
- west − east: `ADVx_TH + DFxE_TH` at `i0` and `i1`
- south − north: `ADVy_TH + DFyE_TH` at `j0` and `j1`

**RHS term 2 — vertical faces.** `ADVr_TH + DFrE_TH + DFrI_TH`, bottom face at `k=K` minus top face at `k=0`. At full depth the `k=50` face is the sea floor, so this reduces to the surface face alone.

**RHS term 3 — surface forcing with shortwave penetration.**
- surface level: `TFLUX − (1 − (q₁(0) − q₂(0)))·oceQsw`
- subsurface: `(q₁·mskC − q₂·mskC_dn)·oceQsw`, with

$$q(z) = R_{SW}e^{z/\zeta_1} + (1-R_{SW})e^{z/\zeta_2}$$

zeroed below the level where `Z < −200 m`. Divided by `rhoConst·Cp·hFacC·drF`.

**RHS term 4 — geothermal.** `geothermalFlux` applied at bottom wet cells only (`mskb = mskC − mskC_dn`), divided by `rhoConst·Cp·hFacC·drF`.

**Residual** = LHS − (rim + vertical + forcing + geothermal).

**Constants** (carried verbatim from the signed convention into the executor, `ecco_regional_budget.py:97-98`): `rhoConst = 1029.0` kg/m³, `Cp = 3994.0` J/kg/K, `R_SW = 0.62`, `ζ₁ = 0.6` m, `ζ₂ = 20.0` m, shortwave cut at `Z < −200 m`.

## 4. Pass bars — both required

- **absolute:** max monthly |residual| / volume ≤ **1e-10** °C/s
- **relative:** max monthly |residual| / largest term ≤ **1e-6**

The relative bar exists precisely because omitting geothermal still passes the absolute bar (measured 2026-08-31). The executor writes **no receipt at all** if the correct formulation fails either bar.

## 5. Mutation evidence — and why full depth changes it

The executor reruns four sabotages and records that each tripped a bar: `geothermal-omitted`, `rim-west-face-shifted`, `vertical-face-sign-flipped`, `vertical-faces-omitted`.

`geothermal-omitted` is *applicability-aware*: in the registered 300 m box it leaves the residual at 2.1e-7 relative, below the bar, and is recorded `applicable: false`. **A full-depth Argentine Basin box is the opposite case** — every water column terminates on the sea floor, so `bottom_cells` is large and the geothermal omission should be genuinely *caught*. If your receipt comes back with `geothermal-omitted` marked not applicable at full depth, that is a signal something is wrong with the mask or the bottom-cell count, not a pass.

## 6. Gates and risks, in the order they will bite

1. **Single-tile limit (most likely blocker).** The volume must lie inside **one tile's interior**, `j` and `i` within 1–89. A box spanning two tiles, or touching a tile edge, is refused by design (`resolve_box`, lines 143–155) — seam-crossing volumes wait on the seam-calibrated section machinery. The Argentine Basin at 60°W–20°W may well straddle a face boundary or run up against the tile edge. **Resolve this first:** fetch collection 1 alone (~30 MB) and run the box through `resolve_box` before committing to any bulk download. If it refuses, the fallback is a narrower sub-box inside one tile, reported honestly as a sub-basin rather than the basin.

2. **A box is a rectangle, not a bathymetric basin.** `resolve_box` returns the smallest index rectangle containing every cell centre in the lat/lon box — so the control volume includes Argentine shelf and slope cells, not the ~5000 m-isobath basin an oceanographer means by "Argentine Basin". The budget still closes (closure is algebraic over any control volume), but the *science* label needs care. The receipt's `mask_sha256`, `lat_extent`/`lon_extent`, `wet_cells` and `bottom_cells` are the disclosure; no oracle checks the mask is the water you meant.

3. **No reference anchor.** The attester's two-sided anchors (27,921 wet cells, 4.1351e15 m³, residual 1.352e-14) fire only for `southeast-atlantic-upper`/2010. An explicit box PASSes on sanctioned code + both bars + consistent evidence, with no anchor. The report must say so.

4. **Native grid only.** Any 0.5° regridded ECCO collection is a hard refusal for budget work — no correct formulation exists there.

5. **No double-counting hFac.** `ADV*_TH`/`DF*_TH` already carry area and partial-cell thickness; they are summed raw across faces. `rA·drF·hFacC` enters only the tendency and forcing integration.

6. **`RECORD.json` stamp.** After fetching, run `tools/science_record_verify.py … --stamp` once. Every attester FAILs a receipt from an unstamped tree.

7. **Release mixing (V4R4 vs V4R4B).** The ecco skill flags a release-variant caveat; I could not read the concept here, so treat this as unverified — check the ShortName variants before fetching.

## 7. Volume and cadence

Granules are whole-grid (all 13 tiles); there is no server-side subsetting to the basin, so you pay for the globe:

- 3D temperature flux, 7 vars × 50 levels: **≈1.8 GB/yr** (dominant)
- `THETA` snapshots, 13/yr: ≈0.28 GB/yr
- heat flux + SSH snapshots (2D): ≈0.03 GB/yr
- geometry: ~30 MB, once

**≈2.2 GB per year** — consistent with the ~2.5 GB 2010 fixture. Full record 1992–2017 ≈ **55–60 GB**.

`--year` binds one calendar year, so a multi-year budget is one run per year, each needing its own 13 snapshots (Jan 1 *Y* → Jan 1 *Y*+1). The `sweep` skill can table the per-year receipts.
