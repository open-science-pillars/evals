## 1. Domain, and the gate it has to clear first

Argentine Basin, full depth (~0–6000 m, all 50 levels), native llc90 grid.

The attested regional budget takes either a registered region or `--box LAT0 LAT1 LON0 LON1 --depth-m D`. There is **one** registered region (`southeast-atlantic-upper`), so the Argentine Basin is an explicit box — proposed **50°S–36°S, 60°W–20°W, `--depth-m 6200`** (snapped to the nearest stored cell face; full depth → K=50).

Two constraints on that box, both from the executor:

- **Single-tile v1 limit.** `resolve_box` refuses a box whose cells fall on more than one tile, and refuses a rectangle touching the tile edge (it needs faces 1–89). The registered southeast-Atlantic region sits on tile 1 spanning roughly 18°W–22°E, which puts tile 1's western edge near ~38°W — so a 60°W box very likely lands on a second tile and **the run is refused by design**. This is the first thing to resolve, and it only needs the geometry granule. If it does span a seam, the options are: shrink the box to the eastern Argentine Basin on one tile, or wait for the seam-calibrated section machinery.
- **Rectangle, not a basin mask.** The explicit tier resolves to the smallest index rectangle containing the box's cells. You get a geographic box, not a bathymetric basin — it will include Malvinas shelf and Rio Grande Rise cells. The receipt discloses lat/lon extent, wet-cell count, volume and a sha256 of the resolved wet mask; nothing verifies the mask is the water you meant.

## 3. Terms to compute

Control volume `V = Σ rA·drF·hFacC` over the resolved rectangle, levels 0…K. All terms in **degC·m³/s** (multiply by ρ·Cp = 1029 × 3994 = 4.110×10⁶ J m⁻³ K⁻¹ for watts). Constants are the executor's: `rhoConst` 1029.0, `Cp` 3994.0, shortwave `R` 0.62, `ζ1` 0.6 m, `ζ2` 20.0 m.

**1. Tendency (LHS).** `s* = 1 + ETAN/Depth` from the bracketing SSH snapshots; `G = Δ(s*·THETA)/Δt` across each month boundary, integrated over the volume. 13 snapshots for 12 months — snapshots, never monthly-mean differences.

**2. Horizontal rim convergence.** `Fx = ADVx_TH + DFxE_TH`, `Fy = ADVy_TH + DFyE_TH`, evaluated as **raw face fluxes on the four boundary faces** (west `i0` minus east `i1`, south `j0` minus north `j1`), never derived from a divergence field — the telescoping identity is algebra, not evidence. At full depth this is where the Malvinas/Brazil confluence and the deep western boundary flow live, and it will dominate.

**3. Vertical convergence.** `Fr = ADVr_TH + DFrE_TH + DFrI_TH`, top face minus bottom face, with the bottom face zero-padded. At K=50 the bottom face is zero by construction — correct, since there is no advective or diffusive flux through the seafloor; the actual bottom boundary condition is term 5.

**4. Surface forcing with shortwave penetration.** `q(z) = R·exp(z/ζ1) + (1−R)·exp(z/ζ2)`, zeroed below 200 m. Surface layer: `TFLUX − (1 − (q1[0]−q2[0]))·oceQsw`. Sub-surface layers: `(q1[k]·mask − q2[k]·mask_down)·oceQsw`. Divided by `ρ·Cp` and by `hFacC·drF`.

**5. Geothermal.** `geothermalFlux.bin` applied at bottom wet cells (`mskb = mskc − mskc_down`), same `ρ·Cp` and `hFacC·drF` scaling. Folded into the forcing term in the receipt but tracked separately for the mutation test.

**Residual** = tendency − (rim + vertical + forcing incl. geothermal). Never absorbed into a term.

## 4. Closure bars and evidence

Both bars are required and the attester recomputes them from the receipt:

- absolute: `max|residual| / V ≤ 1×10⁻¹⁰` degC/s
- relative: `max|residual| / largest term ≤ 1×10⁻⁶`

The executor also reruns four sabotaged variants and records that each tripped a bar: `geothermal-omitted`, `rim-west-face-shifted`, `vertical-face-sign-flipped`, `vertical-faces-omitted`. If a structural mutation passes, or the correct run fails its own bars, **no receipt is written**.

One thing specific to going full depth: for the registered 323 m box, `geothermal-omitted` is allowed to come back `applicable: false` (measured 2.1×10⁻⁷ relative, under the bar — physics, not a broken test). For a full-depth Argentine Basin volume the bottom-cell count is large, so that mutation should be **caught**. If it comes back not-applicable at 6000 m, something is wrong with how the geothermal file was read, and that's the first thing the budget-auditor checks anyway.

The reference anchors in the attester (27,921 wet cells, 4.1351×10¹⁵ m³, residual 1.352×10⁻¹⁴) apply only to `southeast-atlantic-upper`/2010. An explicit box **carries no anchor** — it passes on the two bars and the mutation evidence, and is disclosed by mask digest. The report has to say so.
