## Every term to compute

Constants, verbatim from the executor: `RHOCONST = 1029.0`, `C_P = 3994.0`, `R_SW = 0.62`, `ZETA1 = 0.6`, `ZETA2 = 20.0`. Volume element `vol = rA · drF · hFacC`. Free-surface factor `s* = 1 + ETAN/Depth`.

**1. Tendency (LHS).** `G_total = d(s*·THETA)/dt` from the bracketing snapshots, `dt` in seconds per month; volume-integrated over the box. Snapshots, never monthly means — the month-boundary bookends are what make this exact.

**2. Advective convergence (rim).** Raw face fluxes at the control-volume boundary: `ADVx_TH` on the west/east faces (`i0`, `i1`), `ADVy_TH` on the south/north (`j0`, `j1`). Read as **raw face fluxes, never derived from the divergence field** — the executor is emphatic that the telescoping identity is algebra, not evidence.

**3. Diffusive convergence (rim).** `DFxE_TH`, `DFyE_TH` on the same four faces; `DFrE_TH + DFrI_TH` (explicit + implicit) vertically. Summed with term 2 into one rim integral in the regional formulation.

**4. Vertical faces.** Top face at `k=0` and bottom face at `k=K`, from `ADVr_TH + DFrE_TH + DFrI_TH`, with the array zero-padded below the last level. At full depth `K=50`, so the bottom face is the pad — flux through the sea floor is zero, and the geothermal input enters through forcing instead. That consistency is worth checking in the output.

**5. Surface forcing with shortwave penetration.** `q1`, `q2` from the two-band exponential on the `Zp1` faces, zeroed below the 200 m cutoff (`zcut`); surface level gets `TFLUX - (1 - (q1[0]-q2[0]))·oceQsw`, subsurface levels get the `q1/q2` difference times `oceQsw`. Divided by `RHOCONST·C_P` and by `hFacC·drF`.

**6. Geothermal.** `geothermalFlux` applied at bottom wet cells only, via `mskb = mskc - mskc_dn`, same `/(RHOCONST·C_P·hFacC·drF)` scaling. Reported as its own integral (`geo_int`) in the receipt — do not fold it silently into term 5.

**Residual:** `G_total - (advection + diffusion + forcing)`, judged against **both** bars, both required:
- absolute: max monthly |residual| / volume ≤ **1e-10** °C s⁻¹
- relative: max monthly |residual| / largest term ≤ **1e-6**

**Mutation evidence:** the executor reruns four sabotaged variants (geothermal omitted, rim west face shifted one cell, vertical face sign flipped, vertical faces omitted). If a structural mutation passes, or the correct run fails, **no receipt is written**.
