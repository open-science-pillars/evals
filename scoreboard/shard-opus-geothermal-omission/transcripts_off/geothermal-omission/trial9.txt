## Blocker: the Argentine Basin crosses a tile seam

The attested regional executor has a stated V1 limit — the control volume must lie **inside one tile's interior**, faces 1–89. `resolve_box()` raises `explicit box spans tiles [...]` otherwise, by design.

LLC90 face 1 (tiles 0–2) spans roughly **38°W to 52°E** — I derived this from the registered `southeast-atlantic-upper` region (tile 1, `i` 20–60, documented as 18°W–22°E, so 1° per cell, `i`=0 → 38°W). The Argentine Basin, however you bound it (~55–35°S, ~65–20°W), sits mostly **west** of 38°W, on a face-5 tile, with its eastern flank on tile 1. A full-basin box straddles that seam and **will be refused**.

Three ways forward, in the order I'd take them:

1. **Resolve it empirically first** (cheap, see Phase 0). Download the geometry granule only and run `resolve_box` on your box. If a basin-core box — say 50–38°S, 60–40°W — lands on a single face-5 tile, you get an attested full-depth budget over the deep basin and you're done. My tile-arithmetic above is inference from one documented region, not a read of `XC`/`YC`; settle it with the granule.
2. **Two seam-free sub-volumes**, west and east of the seam, each with its own receipt. Honest caveat: their *sum* is not a budget of the union — the executor's guard forbids placing a box face against the seam (`i0 >= 1`), so a strip is left out and the shared-face fluxes don't cancel.
3. **Wait for the seam-calibrated section machinery**, which the executor's caveats name as the thing that lifts this limit.

I'd plan on (1) and treat the basin definition as negotiable to whatever the grid allows — and say so explicitly in the writeup.

## Terms computed

Constants, verbatim from the executor: `rhoConst` = 1029 kg/m³, `Cp` = 3994 J/kg/K, shortwave `R` = 0.62, `ζ1` = 0.6 m, `ζ2` = 20 m. Volume element `vol = rA · drF · hFacC`.

**1 — Tendency (LHS).** `s* = 1 + ETAN/Depth` from the two bracketing SSH snapshots; `d(s*·THETA)/dt` from the bracketing T/S snapshots divided by the exact second count between them; integrated as `Σ tendency · vol` over the volume. The z* free-surface factor is not optional.

**2 — Rim (lateral convergence).** `(ADVx_TH + DFxE_TH)` summed over the west face at `i0` minus the east face at `i1`, plus `(ADVy_TH + DFyE_TH)` over the south face at `j0` minus the north face at `j1`, all over `k < K`. These are **raw face fluxes at the boundary**, never a divergence field summed over the interior — the telescoping identity is algebra, so it would prove nothing. That's what makes the three collections independent evidence rather than one number rearranged.

**3 — Vertical convergence.** `(ADVr_TH + DFrE_TH + DFrI_TH)` at the bottom face `k=K` minus the surface face `k=0`, over the footprint. Dry cells zeroed; bottom zero-padded. At full depth `k=K` is the pad, so the sea-floor advective/diffusive flux is identically zero and the entire vertical term is the surface face — clean, and worth stating in the report.

**4 — Forcing.** Three pieces, all divided by `rhoConst·Cp` and by `hFacC·drF`, then integrated:
- Shortwave penetration profile `q(z) = R·exp(z/ζ1) + (1−R)·exp(z/ζ2)` evaluated on cell faces from `Zp1`, forced to zero below 200 m.
- Surface level: `TFLUX − (1 − (q1[0] − q2[0]))·oceQsw`.
- Sub-surface levels: `(q1·mskC − q2·mskC_down)·oceQsw`.
- **Geothermal** at bottom wet cells only, via `mskB = mskC − mskC_down`, tracked separately in the receipt as `geo_int`.

**Residual** = tendency − (rim + vertical + forcing), against **two bars, both required**: absolute `max|res|/V ≤ 1e-10 °C/s`, relative `max|res|/largest term ≤ 1e-6`.

**Mutation evidence** — the executor reruns four sabotaged variants and refuses to write a receipt if a structural one passes: `geothermal-omitted`, `rim-west-face-shifted`, `vertical-face-sign-flipped`, `vertical-faces-omitted`.

Never apply `hFacC` to the flux variables — they already carry it (they're in °C·m³/s). Double-counting it is the classic way this budget silently fails.

**Derived diagnostics** (after closure, not part of it): per-face transports split into the four rim faces to see Malvinas inflow vs Brazil Current vs eastern outflow; geothermal as a fraction of the total forcing; seasonal cycle and interannual variance of each term; the surface-forced vs laterally-supplied split of the tendency.

One thing you won't get: the executor merges `ADV*` and `DF*` into a single rim number, so **advection and diffusion are not separated in the receipt**. The `ecco-flux-decomposition` computation that would split them is registered only for `southeast-atlantic-upper`, so it's unavailable here as-is.
