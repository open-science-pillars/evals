## Terms to compute

Control volume integral, every month, all 50 levels. Volume element `rA · drF · hFacC` (never re-apply hFac to a MASS variable or a flux — they already carry it).

**Tendency (LHS)** — `d(s*·THETA)/dt` from bracketing snapshots, `s* = 1 + ETAN/Depth`, differenced over the true seconds between snapshots, integrated over the volume.

**RHS, three terms:**
- **Lateral rim transport** — `(ADVx_TH + DFxE_TH)` on the west face minus the east face, plus `(ADVy_TH + DFyE_TH)` on the south face minus the north face, summed over k = 1…50 on the boundary faces only.
- **Vertical transport** — `(ADVr_TH + DFrE_TH + DFrI_TH)` at the bottom face minus the surface face. At full depth the bottom face is the zero-padded k=50 face, so this reduces to the surface face alone.
- **Forcing** — `TFLUX` with the penetrating shortwave removed at level 1, plus the subsurface shortwave convergence `q1 − q2` (R = 0.62, ζ₁ = 0.6 m, ζ₂ = 20 m, zeroed below 200 m) driven by `oceQsw`; **plus geothermal** at bottom wet cells. Both divided by `ρ_const·c_p` (1029 kg m⁻³, 3994 J kg⁻¹ K⁻¹) and by `hFacC·drF`.

**Residual** = tendency − (rim + vertical + forcing). Reported, never absorbed into a term.

**Pass bars, both required:** per-volume residual ≤ **1e-10 °C s⁻¹** (the signed pointwise tolerance) **and** residual/largest-term ≤ **1e-6**. Reference run (southeast-atlantic-upper, 2010): 1.352e-14 absolute, 1.533e-07 relative.

**Mutation evidence** the executor writes into every receipt: geothermal omitted, rim west face shifted one cell, vertical face sign flipped, vertical faces omitted. At full depth the volume is full of bottom cells, so the geothermal sabotage *must* be caught — `applicable: false` would itself be a red flag here.

**Disclosure** (no oracle can check a mask is the water you meant): resolved index bounds, tile, depth face, lat/lon extent, wet and bottom cell counts, volume, SHA-256 of the wet mask, SHA-256 of the geometry granule.

Note the units: the receipt's terms are °C m³ s⁻¹. Converting to watts is a post-step through `ρ_const·c_p`, outside the attested numbers.
