## The blocker: the Argentine Basin probably doesn't fit

`ecco_regional_budget.py:71-74` states a v1 limit — **the control volume must lie inside one tile's interior (faces 1–89)**. `resolve_box` exits with `"explicit box spans tiles [...]"` for a seam-crosser, and `ecco-regional-heat-budget.md:90-91` confirms: refused by design until seam-calibrated section machinery lands.

The Argentine Basin runs to ~60°W, so its western half falls off tile 1 and the box resolves to ≥2 tiles → refused.

## Terms to compute

Per month, integrated over the control volume `rA·drF·hFacC`. Constants are the executor's: `rhoConst 1029.0`, `Cp 3994.0`, shortwave `R 0.62, ζ₁ 0.6 m, ζ₂ 20.0 m`, penetration truncated below 200 m.

1. **Tendency** — `d(s*·THETA)/dt` from bracketing snapshots, `s* = 1 + ETAN/Depth`.
2. **Horizontal rim transport** — `ADVx_TH + DFxE_TH` on west/east faces, `ADVy_TH + DFyE_TH` on south/north, as **raw face fluxes**. Never from the divergence field: the telescoping identity holds for random noise and proves nothing (`ecco-regional-heat-budget.md:63-65`).
3. **Vertical face transport** — `ADVr_TH + DFrE_TH + DFrI_TH` at top face minus bottom face, bottom zero-padded. At full depth (K=50) the lower face is identically zero — the sea floor.
4. **Surface forcing** — `TFLUX` with the penetrating shortwave removed at level 1, `oceQsw` redistributed downward, over `rhoConst·Cp·hFacC·drF`.
5. **Geothermal** — `geothermalFlux` at bottom wet cells only (`mskb`).

**Residual** = (1) − [(2) + (3) + (4) + (5)], judged against **two bars, both required**: absolute `≤ 1e-10 °C/s` per volume **and** relative `≤ 1e-6` of the largest term.

**Why full depth changes the geothermal story.** For the shallow 300 m reference box the geothermal sabotage recorded `applicable: false` — too few bottom cells. Your full-depth volume is nearly all bottom cells, so omitting geothermal **must** be caught, and the measured proof that one bar is not a criterion (omission lands at 1.24e-12, inside the absolute bar) applies directly. Each run also reruns four sabotages — geothermal omitted, rim west face shifted, vertical face sign flipped, vertical faces omitted — and writes no receipt if a structural one passes.
