## Every term computed

Volume element throughout: `rA · drF · hFacC` (partial cells in). Constants: `rhoConst = 1029.0 kg/m³`, `c_p = 3994.0 J/kg/K`.

**Tendency (LHS)**
1. `s* = 1 + ETAN/Depth` at each of the 13 snapshot times
2. `G_total = Δ(s*·THETA)/Δt` per month, volume-weighted and summed → degC·m³/s

**Transport (rim faces, read raw — never from a divergence field; telescoping is algebra, not evidence)**
3. Horizontal advective: `ADVx_TH` at west/east rim, `ADVy_TH` at south/north rim
4. Horizontal diffusive: `DFxE_TH`, `DFyE_TH` at the same four faces
5. Vertical advective: `ADVr_TH` at the top face (k=0) and bottom face (k=K)
6. Vertical diffusive explicit: `DFrE_TH`
7. Vertical diffusive implicit: `DFrI_TH`

For full depth, K = 50 and the bottom face is the zero pad — the vertical term reduces to the surface face alone.

**Forcing**
8. Surface: `TFLUX − (1 − (q1[0]−q2[0]))·oceQsw` at level 1
9. Shortwave penetration at subsurface levels: `(q1·mskC − q2·mskC_dn)·oceQsw`, with `q(z) = R·exp(z/ζ1) + (1−R)·exp(z/ζ2)`, `R = 0.62`, `ζ1 = 0.6 m`, `ζ2 = 20 m`, forced to zero below 200 m
10. Geothermal: `geo · mskB` at the bottom wet cell of each column
11. All forcing divided by `rhoConst·c_p`, then by `hFacC·drF`, then volume-integrated

**Residual**
12. `residual = tendency − (rim + vertical + forcing)`, per month

**Also in the receipt:** the 12-month residual series, largest term, wet-cell count, bottom-cell count, volume in m³, resolved index bounds, lat/lon extent, depth face, mask sha256, geometry granule sha256.
