**The control volume must lie inside one LLC90 tile's interior.** The sanctioned executor refuses a seam-crossing box by design (`ecco_regional_budget.py:142-155`): it errors if the box selects cells on more than one tile, and again if the resolved index rectangle touches a tile edge (requires `1 <= j0`, `j1 <= 89`).

The Argentine Basin (~50°S–35°S, **60°W–20°W**) sits west of that, and plausibly straddles the face boundary between the Atlantic face and the American face.

**First action, before any bulk download:** pull the geometry granule alone (one static file) and read `YC`/`XC` to find which tile(s) the basin occupies and whether an interior rectangle exists. Three outcomes:

## 2. Terms to compute

Four terms, `tendency − (advection + diffusion + forcing) = residual`, with the constants carried verbatim by the sanctioned code (`ecco_regional_budget.py:97-98`): **rhoConst = 1029 kg/m³, Cp = 3994 J/kg/K, R_sw = 0.62, ζ₁ = 0.6 m, ζ₂ = 20 m**.

**① Tendency (LHS)** — lines 231-246
- `s* = 1 + ETAN/Depth` from the bracketing SSH snapshots
- `G = d(s*·THETA)/dt`, differenced across consecutive snapshots over the true `dt` in seconds
- masked to `hFacC > 0`, multiplied by the volume element **`rA · drF · hFacC`**, summed over the volume

**② Rim transport (horizontal advection + diffusion)** — lines 250-251, 277-283
- `fx = ADVx_TH + DFxE_TH`, `fy = ADVy_TH + DFyE_TH`
- **raw face fluxes at the four boundary faces**: west-in − east-out + south-in − north-out
- Deliberately *not* derived from a divergence field: "the telescoping identity is algebra, not evidence" (`ecco_regional_budget.py:12-15`). This is what makes the closure a three-collection cross-check rather than arithmetic.

**③ Vertical flux at the top and bottom faces** — lines 252-254, 275, 284-285
- `fr = ADVr_TH + DFrE_TH + DFrI_TH` (advective + explicit + **implicit** diffusive; dropping `DFrI_TH` is a classic miss)
- bottom face (k=K) minus top face (k=0), array zero-padded below the bottom

**④ Forcing** — lines 290-316. Two parts:

*Surface heat flux with shortwave penetration:*
- `q(z) = R·exp(z/ζ₁) + (1−R)·exp(z/ζ₂)`, evaluated on cell faces, **truncated to zero below 200 m**
- surface level: `TFLUX − (1 − (q₁[0] − q₂[0]))·oceQsw`
- sub-surface levels: the `oceQsw` divergence between faces
- converted to degC/s by `/(rhoConst·Cp)/(hFacC·drF)`

*Geothermal at bottom wet cells:*
- bottom mask `mskb = mskc − mskc_shifted_down`
- `geo · mskb /(rhoConst·Cp)/(hFacC·drF)`, integrated and reported separately as `geo_int`

**Residual** = ① − (② + ③ + ④).

## 3. Pass bars — both required

From `knowledge/computations/ecco-regional-heat-budget.md` (status stable, three human-verified events, most recent 2026-09-20) and `BARS` in the executor:

- **relative:** max monthly |residual| / largest term ≤ **1e-6**
