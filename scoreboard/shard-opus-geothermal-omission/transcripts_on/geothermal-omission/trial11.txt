## Terms to compute

Volume element `rA × drF × hFacC`; free-surface factor `s* = 1 + ETAN/Depth`.

**Tendency (LHS):** `d(s*·THETA)/dt` from bracketing snapshots, volume-integrated.

**RHS, three independent sources so agreement is evidence rather than algebra:**

1. **Horizontal rim flux** — `ADVx_TH + DFxE_TH` on the west/east faces, `ADVy_TH + DFyE_TH` on south/north, read as **raw face fluxes at the boundary**, never from the divergence field (the telescoping identity is algebra, not evidence).
2. **Vertical flux** — `ADVr_TH + DFrE_TH + DFrI_TH`, top face minus bottom face, bottom zero-padded.
3. **Surface forcing** — `TFLUX` with shortwave penetration removed at the surface, plus the subsurface shortwave divergence `(q1−q2)·oceQsw`, cut off below 200 m.
4. **Geothermal** — at bottom wet cells only, via the bottom mask `hFacC − hFacC_below`.

Terms 3–4 are scaled by `1/(ρ·c_p)` and `1/(hFacC·drF)`. Constants carried in source: **ρ = 1029.0 kg/m³, c_p = 3994.0 J/(kg·K), R_sw = 0.62, ζ₁ = 0.6 m, ζ₂ = 20 m.**

**Residual** = tendency − (rim + vertical + forcing). Never absorbed into a term.
