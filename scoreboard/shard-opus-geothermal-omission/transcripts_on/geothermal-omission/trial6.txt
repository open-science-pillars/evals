### 5. Every term computed

Volume element throughout: `vol = rA · drF · hFacC` (partial cells in). Constants, verbatim from the sanctioned code: `rhoConst = 1029.0`, `Cp = 3994.0`, shortwave `R = 0.62`, `ζ₁ = 0.6 m`, `ζ₂ = 20 m`.

**Tendency.** `s* = 1 + ETAN/Depth` from the bracketing snapshots; `G_total = Δ(s*·THETA)/Δt` per cell, volume-integrated over the control volume. The z* scale factor is what makes a free-surface budget close; dropping it is a formulation error, not a rounding one.

**Advection.** Horizontal `ADVx_TH`, `ADVy_TH`; vertical `ADVr_TH`, bottom face zero-padded.

**Diffusion.** Horizontal `DFxE_TH`, `DFyE_TH`; vertical explicit + implicit, `DFrE_TH + DFrI_TH`.

For the regional control volume these two are evaluated as **raw face fluxes on the volume's boundary** — never as the divergence field, since the telescoping identity holds for random noise and proves nothing:
- rim (lateral): `(ADVx_TH+DFxE_TH)` west − east, `(ADVy_TH+DFyE_TH)` south − north, summed over `k < K`;
- vertical: `(ADVr_TH+DFrE_TH+DFrI_TH)` at the bottom face minus the top face.

For the pointwise budget they are convergences per cell, `-(∂ₓ + ∂ᵧ)` and the vertical difference, divided by `vol`.

**Forcing.** Surface level: `TFLUX − (1 − (q₁[0] − q₂[0]))·oceQsw`. Sub-surface: `(q₁ − q₂)·oceQsw` with `q(z) = R·exp(z/ζ₁) + (1−R)·exp(z/ζ₂)`, truncated to zero below 200 m. Plus **geothermal** `geothermalFlux` applied on the bottom-wet-cell mask (`mskb = mskc − mskc_down`). Whole term divided by `rhoConst·Cp·(hFacC·drF)`.

**Residual.** `tendency − (advection + diffusion + forcing)`, i.e. `lhs − (rim + vert + forc)` regionally. Reported, never absorbed into a physical term.

Units: regional terms in degC·m³/s, residual normalised per volume in degC/s.

### 6. Pass bars and what a receipt must carry

Regional (`ecco-regional-heat-budget`) — **both** bars required: absolute residual per volume ≤ **1e-10 degC/s**, and residual relative to the largest regional term ≤ **1e-6**. One bar is not a criterion; that is the whole point of the geothermal measurement above.

Pointwise (`ecco-heat-budget`, whole-tile) — `residual_max ≤ 1e-10 degC/s` and `residual_p999 ≤ 1e-11 degC/s`; baseline measured max 4.95e-11, p99.9 7.3e-12 on 3,341,772 cell-months. Note its `region` registry holds only `tile1-interior`, so there is no pointwise Argentine-Basin variant — the basin cells on tile 1 are covered by that run, the rest are not.

Every run also emits **mutation evidence**: four sabotages (geothermal omitted, rim west face shifted one cell, vertical face sign flipped, vertical faces omitted) each rerun and recorded as failing a bar. If a structural sabotage passes, no receipt is written at all. At full depth I expect the geothermal sabotage to be `applicable: true` and caught — if it comes back `applicable: false`, the depth resolution went wrong.

The `ecco-flux-decomposition` computation (Reynolds mean/eddy split) accepts **registered regions only**, so it is unavailable for an explicit Argentine Basin box unless the region is registered upstream.
