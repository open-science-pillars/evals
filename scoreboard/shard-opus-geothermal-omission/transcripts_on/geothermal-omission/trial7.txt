## Two blocking issues, before any download

**1. The Argentine Basin probably crosses a tile seam — and that's a hard refusal, not a warning.**

The attested regional budget has a stated v1 limit: the control volume must lie **within one tile's interior, faces 1–89**. `resolve_box` refuses a box spanning tiles ("volumes crossing a tile seam need the seam-calibrated section machinery and are refused here by design") and *also* refuses a rectangle that merely touches a tile edge.

Tile 1 is the South Atlantic tile, and the one registered region on it (`southeast-atlantic-upper`) is the **eastern** South Atlantic, ~18°W–22°E. The Argentine Basin sits west of that, roughly 60°W–20°W. Whether it lands wholly inside tile 1's interior is the question the whole plan hinges on, and I won't guess it. It's a one-shot check against the geometry granule alone (no budget data needed):

```python
# geometry granule only, ~one static file
import xarray as xr, numpy as np
g = xr.open_dataset("GRID_GEOMETRY_ECCO_V4r4_native_llc0090.nc")
inbox = (g.YC>=-55)&(g.YC<=-35)&(g.XC>=-60)&(g.XC<=-20)
print(np.unique(np.where(inbox.values.any(axis=(1,2)))[0]))  # want exactly one tile
```

If it returns more than one tile, the attested route is closed for this domain today. The honest fallbacks, in order: (a) shrink to the sub-basin that fits one tile interior and say so; (b) use the **pointwise** attested budget instead (`ecco_heat_budget.py`) — it closes at every wet cell and the concept states pointwise closure holds on any spatial subset, so you get verified per-cell closure over the basin but *not* an attested control-volume integral with rim transports; (c) wait for the seam-calibrated section machinery.

**2. Full depth makes geothermal decisive, and it is not a PO.DAAC collection.**

`geothermalFlux.bin` comes from the ECCO v4 tutorial distribution. The concept records a measurement from 2026-08-31: omitting geothermal lands at **1.24e-12 degC/s — inside the absolute bar of 1e-10** — and is caught only by the relative bar of 1e-6. A PO.DAAC-only budget omits it silently and still looks clean on one bar.

This matters far more for you than for the reference run. That run was a 300 m box with few bottom cells, where the executor correctly records geothermal as `applicable: false`. A **full-depth** Argentine Basin volume is bottom cells everywhere — geothermal will be a leading-order term at depth, and the `geothermal-omitted` mutation should come back `caught: true`. If it doesn't, something is wrong with the mask, not with physics.

## Terms to compute

Control-volume form, all constants from the sanctioned executor. Volume element `rA · drF · hFacC` (partial cells in). `s* = 1 + ETAN/Depth`.

1. **Tendency** — `d/dt ∫ s*·θ dV`, from 13 month-boundary snapshots differenced across each month. Snapshots, never monthly means.
2. **Horizontal rim transport** — `(ADVx_TH + DFxE_TH)` on the west and east faces, `(ADVy_TH + DFyE_TH)` on south and north, summed over `k = 0…K` at the boundary indices only.
3. **Vertical faces** — `(ADVr_TH + DFrE_TH + DFrI_TH)` at the volume's top and bottom faces, bottom zero-padded. Full-depth means `K = 50`, so the bottom face is the pad and this term reduces to the surface face.
4. **Surface forcing** — `TFLUX` with the penetrating shortwave removed at level 1, plus `oceQsw` distributed over depth: `R = 0.62`, `ζ₁ = 0.6 m`, `ζ₂ = 20 m`, penetration zeroed below 200 m. Divided by `ρ_const · C_p · hFacC · drF`, with `ρ_const = 1029 kg/m³`, `C_p = 3994 J/kg/K`.
5. **Geothermal** — `geothermalFlux` applied at bottom wet cells only, via the bottom mask `hFacC_mask − hFacC_mask_shifted_down`, same normalization as term 4.
6. **Residual** — `tendency − (rim + vertical + forcing + geothermal)`, reported per month, never absorbed into a term.

## Bars and attestation

**Two bars, both required.** Absolute: max monthly |residual|/volume ≤ **1e-10 degC/s**. Relative: max monthly |residual|/largest term ≤ **1e-6**.

The executor also reruns four sabotages and records each failing a bar — geothermal omitted, rim west face shifted one cell, vertical face sign flipped, vertical faces omitted. If a structural sabotage *passes*, no receipt is written at all: a test that cannot fail isn't evidence.
