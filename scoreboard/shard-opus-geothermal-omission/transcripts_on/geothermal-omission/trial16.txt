## The blocking issue: tile seam, before anything else

The regional executor has a stated v1 limit — the control volume must lie inside **one tile's interior** (faces 1–89); a box crossing a seam is refused by design (`ecco_regional_budget.py:140-155`). The registered `southeast-atlantic-upper` pins tile index 1 to ~18°W–22°E across i=20–60, which puts tile 1's western edge near ~38°W. A natural Argentine Basin box (≈55°S–35°S, 60°W–20°W) would then **straddle tiles 0 and 1 and be refused**.

That inference is arithmetic from one note, not a signed fact. **First action, before any flux granule:** download only `ECCO_L4_GEOMETRY_LLC0090GRID_V4R4` (8.2 MB, one static granule) and resolve the box. If it straddles, the options are a one-tile sub-box (eastern basin, ~37°W–20°W), or the whole basin unattested, or wait for the seam-calibrated section machinery. Worth knowing now rather than after 29 GB.

Also: `--box` resolves to the smallest **index rectangle** containing the requested cells, so the volume will include water outside the basin proper (Brazil Basin to the north, shelf to the west). The receipt discloses lat/lon extent, wet/bottom cell counts, volume and a mask sha256 — no oracle checks it's the water you meant; disclosure is the answer.

## Terms, exactly as the sanctioned code computes them

Constants: `rhoConst = 1029.0 kg/m³`, `c_p = 3994.0 J/kg/K`; shortwave `R = 0.62`, `ζ₁ = 0.6 m`, `ζ₂ = 20 m`. Volume element `rA · drF · hFacC` (partial cells in; do not re-apply hFac to `*VELMASS` or the flux variables — they already carry it).

**LHS — tendency.** `s* = 1 + ETAN/Depth` from the SSH snapshots; `G_total = Δ(s*·THETA)/Δt` across each pair of the 13 snapshots; integrated over the volume. Snapshots, not monthly means — a tendency from monthly means is a different quantity.

**RHS, three terms:**

1. **Rim (lateral) transport** — raw face fluxes at the volume boundary, `ADV*_TH + DF*_TH`, west face minus east face plus south minus north, summed over all 50 levels and the face cells. Never derived from a divergence field: the telescoping identity holds for random noise and proves nothing.
2. **Vertical faces** — `ADVr_TH + DFrE_TH + DFrI_TH` at the bottom face minus the surface face. **For a full-depth volume the bottom face is zero-padded**, so this reduces to the surface face alone.
3. **Surface forcing + geothermal** — `TFLUX` with the shortwave part removed at the surface and redistributed downward by the two-exponential absorption profile from `oceQsw`, truncated below 200 m; plus `geothermalFlux` applied at bottom wet cells (`hFacC>0` minus the level below). Both divided by `ρ·c_p` and by `hFacC·drF`.

**Residual** = tendency − (rim + vertical + forcing). Never absorbed into a term, never averaged away.

Reporting extras worth computing once closure passes: split rim into advective vs diffusive and per-face; convert terms to W via `ρ·c_p`; report the geothermal integral separately (it's a headline number for a full-depth basin).

## Pass bars and what the receipt must carry

Two bars, **both** required (`ecco-regional-heat-budget`): absolute `max|residual|/V ≤ 1e-10 °C/s` (inherited from the signed pointwise tolerance) **and** relative `max|residual|/largest term ≤ 1e-6`. One bar is not a criterion.

Every receipt carries mutation evidence — four sabotages rerun and recorded: geothermal omitted, rim west face shifted one cell, vertical face sign flipped, vertical faces omitted. A structural sabotage that fails to fail aborts the run with no receipt. On a full-depth basin volume (many bottom cells) the geothermal mutation must be **caught**, `applicable: true` — unlike the shallow 300 m demonstration box.

The attester `regional_budget_check.py` runs on the receipt before any number is quoted, and refuses a receipt from a data tree with no `RECORD.json` stamp. **Open item:** the only manifest shipped in this package is `fixtures-2010-manifest.json`; a multi-year Argentine Basin run needs a record manifest covering those years before anything attests. Worth checking with you.
