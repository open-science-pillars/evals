## Terms computed

Residual is `tendency − (rim + vertical + forcing)` (`:350`), all integrated over the control volume:

1. **Tendency** — `d(s*·THETA)/dt` from bracketing snapshots, where `s* = 1 + ETAN/Depth` is the z* free-surface scaling (`:235`, `:242-243`). Integrated with volume element `rA · drF · hFacC` (`:226`).
2. **Rim transport** — raw face fluxes on the four lateral boundary faces, `(ADVx_TH + DFxE_TH)` and `(ADVy_TH + DFyE_TH)` (`:250-251`, `:277-281`). Read as raw faces, **never** from a divergence field: the telescoping identity holds for random noise and proves nothing.
3. **Vertical transport** — `ADVr_TH + DFrE_TH + DFrI_TH` differenced across the top and bottom faces, bottom zero-padded (`:252-254`, `:275`, `:284-285`).
4. **Surface forcing** — `TFLUX` with shortwave penetration removed at the surface and redistributed over the sub-surface levels via the two-band exponential, cut off below 200 m (`:294-304`).
5. **Geothermal** — bottom-cell flux `geo · mskb / (ρ·Cp·hFacC·drF)` (`:312-314`), reported separately as `geo_int`.

Constants, verbatim from the code: `rhoConst = 1029.0`, `Cp = 3994.0`, `R_sw = 0.62`, `ζ₁ = 0.6 m`, `ζ₂ = 20.0 m` (`:97-98`).

**Two pass bars, both required:** absolute `max|residual|/V ≤ 1e-10` degC/s and relative `max|residual|/largest_term ≤ 1e-6` (`:99-103`). One bar is not a criterion.

**Four mutations** rerun into every receipt: geothermal omitted, rim west face shifted, vertical face sign flipped, vertical faces omitted. A structural mutation that fails to be caught aborts with no receipt written (`:392-395`).
