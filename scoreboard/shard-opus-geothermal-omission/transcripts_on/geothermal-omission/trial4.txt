## The required input that is not a PO.DAAC collection

**`geothermalFlux.bin`**, from the ECCO v4 tutorial distribution, read with `ecco.read_llc_to_tiles` from the data root. This is the one input that cannot be downloaded from PO.DAAC, and for a **full-depth** budget it is the term that matters most: every water column in the volume contributes a bottom cell.

`ecco-regional-heat-budget.md` records the measurement (2026-08-31): omitting geothermal lands the residual at 1.24e-12, **inside** the absolute bar of 1e-10, and is caught only by the relative bar. That is why the contract requires two bars. A concrete check for your case: in the reference 300 m box the `geothermal-omitted` mutation recorded `applicable: false` because there were too few bottom cells. For a full-depth basin volume it must come back **`caught: true`** — if your receipt says `applicable: false`, the resolved mask is not the water you meant.

## Terms to compute

Tendency minus (advection + diffusion + forcing), integrated over the control volume; volume element `rA · drF · hFacC`.

1. **Tendency (LHS).** `d(s*·THETA)/dt` from bracketing snapshots, `s* = 1 + ETAN/Depth`, differenced over the actual snapshot interval in seconds, integrated over the volume.
2. **Horizontal rim flux.** `ADVx_TH + DFxE_TH` on the west and east faces, `ADVy_TH + DFyE_TH` on the south and north faces — raw face fluxes at the boundary, never the divergence field (the telescoping identity holds for random noise and proves nothing).
3. **Vertical face flux.** `ADVr_TH + DFrE_TH + DFrI_TH` through the top and bottom faces of the volume, bottom face zero-padded — which for a full-depth volume means the seafloor face is exactly zero and all vertical exchange is at the surface.
4. **Surface forcing.** `TFLUX` with the shortwave part removed at the surface and redistributed downward by the two-exponential penetration profile: `R = 0.62`, `ζ₁ = 0.6 m`, `ζ₂ = 20 m`, zeroed below 200 m; normalized by `ρ₀·Cp` and by `hFacC·drF`, with `rhoConst = 1029.0 kg m⁻³`, `Cp = 3994.0 J kg⁻¹ K⁻¹`.
5. **Geothermal forcing.** `geothermalFlux.bin` applied at bottom wet cells only (bottom mask `mskb = mskc − mskc_down`), same `ρ₀·Cp` and `hFacC·drF` normalization. Added to term 4 in the forcing total, and reported separately as `geo_int`.

**Residual** = (1) − [(2) + (3) + (4+5)].

## Closure bars and attestation

Both required, per `ecco-regional-heat-budget.md`:
- absolute: max monthly |residual| / volume ≤ **1e-10 °C s⁻¹** (the signed pointwise tolerance)
- relative: max monthly |residual| / largest term ≤ **1e-6**

Every receipt carries mutation evidence — the executor reruns four sabotages (geothermal omitted, rim west face shifted one cell, vertical face sign flipped, vertical faces omitted) and records each failing a bar; a structural sabotage that cannot fail aborts with no receipt. Run `regional_budget_check.py` on every receipt and report its verdict as read. Never edit the executor — the attester hashes it, and a changed hash fails by construction. `budget-auditor` runs on the result whether or not it passes.

For reference, the pointwise computation's own bar is `residual_max ≤ 1e-10`, `residual_p999 ≤ 1e-11` °C s⁻¹, baseline 4.95e-11 / 7.3e-12 / median 5.7e-14 over 3,341,772 cell-months (year 2010, `tile1-interior`).
