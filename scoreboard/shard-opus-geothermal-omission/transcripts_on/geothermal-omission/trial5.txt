# Heat budget plan — Argentine Basin, ECCO v4r4, native llc90

## Route

Two attested routes exist; only one can bind your domain.

- **`ecco-heat-budget`** (pointwise, `knowledge/computations/ecco-heat-budget.md`, stable) accepts `region` from a registry whose only member is `tile1-interior`. It cannot be pointed at the Argentine Basin without editing the executor, which fails attestation by construction. Not the route.
- **`ecco-regional-heat-budget`** (`knowledge/computations/ecco-regional-heat-budget.md`, stable, `stale_after: 2027-01-04`) takes `--box LAT0 LAT1 LON0 LON1 --depth-m`. **This is the route.** It closes a control-volume budget across three independent collections, reading the boundary as *raw face fluxes* rather than from the divergence field — the design note's point being that the telescoping identity holds for random noise and proves nothing.

## Terms to compute

Volume element throughout: `rA · drF · hFacC` — partial cells enter **once**. `ADV*`/`DF*` and any `*VELMASS` already carry `hFac`; re-applying it is the double-count trap.

Constants, from the sanctioned code: `rhoConst = 1029` kg m⁻³, `c_p = 3994` J kg⁻¹ K⁻¹, shortwave `R = 0.62`, `ζ₁ = 0.6` m, `ζ₂ = 20` m.

**Pointwise, degC s⁻¹** — `G_total = G_adv + G_diff + G_forc`

1. **Tendency** `G_total = Δ(s*·THETA)/Δt`, with the z* scale factor `s* = 1 + ETAN/Depth` applied to each bracketing snapshot before differencing, `Δt` in seconds per month. Snapshots, never monthly means.
2. **Advection** `−∇·(ADVx_TH, ADVy_TH, ADVr_TH) / vol`; vertical faces zero-padded at the seabed, dry-cell values zeroed before differencing.
3. **Diffusion** `−∇·(DFxE_TH, DFyE_TH, DFrE_TH + DFrI_TH) / vol` — explicit **and** implicit vertical diffusion.
4. **Forcing** `(forcH + geothermal) / (rhoConst·c_p) / (hFacC·drF)`, where `forcH` is `TFLUX` at the surface less the shortwave that penetrates past it, plus the subsurface shortwave divergence `q(z) = R·exp(z/ζ₁) + (1−R)·exp(z/ζ₂)` evaluated on cell faces and cut to zero below 200 m; **geothermal enters at the bottom wet cell only**, via the mask `mskC − mskC_shifted_down`.

**Volume-integrated over the basin, degC m³ s⁻¹** — `LHS = rim + vert + forc`

1. **LHS** Σ over the volume of tendency × cell volume.
2. **Rim** raw face fluxes `(ADV*_TH + DF*_TH)` on the four lateral boundary faces — west minus east, south minus north — summed over the depth range.
3. **Vertical** `(ADVr_TH + DFrE_TH + DFrI_TH)` at the bottom face minus the top face. For a full-depth volume the bottom face is the zero-padded seabed, so this reduces to the surface face.
4. **Forcing** the pointwise forcing integrated over the volume, with the geothermal contribution also reported separately as `geo_int`.

**Residual** `LHS − (rim + vert + forc)`, judged against **two bars, both required**: absolute `|res|max / V ≤ 1e-10` degC s⁻¹, and relative `|res|max / largest term ≤ 1e-6`.

## Why full depth makes geothermal the whole ballgame

Measured 2026-08-31 and recorded in the regional computation concept: omitting the geothermal flux lands the residual at **1.24e-12** degC s⁻¹ — comfortably *inside* the absolute bar. It is caught only by the relative bar. A PO.DAAC-only budget therefore omits it silently and passes the tolerance you'd most naturally quote.

Your domain is full-depth, so every water column's bottom cell is inside the control volume and geothermal is unambiguously applicable. The executor's `geothermal-omitted` mutation **must** come back `caught: true`; if it returns `applicable: false`, the volume didn't resolve the way we intended and the mask needs re-examining. This is also why `geothermalFlux.bin` has to be staged before the first run — it isn't something the download step can produce.

## The likely blocker: the tile seam

The executor carries a stated v1 limit — the control volume must lie inside **one tile's interior** (indices 1–89). A box spanning tiles is refused by design, as is a rectangle touching a tile edge.

Extrapolating from the one registered region (`southeast-atlantic-upper`: tile 1, j 20–60, i 20–60 ≈ 44°S–10°S, 18°W–22°E), tile 1 appears to span roughly 38°W–51°E. The Argentine Basin reaches west to about 60°W. **A full-width basin box very likely spans tiles 0 and 1 and gets refused.** That inference is geometry-by-extrapolation, not verified — resolving it is step 1 below, and it's cheap, since the geometry granule is one static file.

If it does straddle the seam, the options are: restrict to the eastern portion that sits on one tile (and name it honestly as a sub-basin, not "the Argentine Basin"); wait for the seam-calibrated section machinery, which hasn't landed; or commission a registered Argentine Basin region added upstream to the sanctioned code, which changes its hash and needs re-attestation.

One further honesty point: `--box` resolves to the *smallest index rectangle containing every cell whose center falls in the box*. On a curvilinear grid that rectangle will include Argentine shelf and ridge flank that no one would call the basin. Land drops out via the wet mask; shallow shelf does not. The receipt discloses the resolved lat/lon extent, wet-cell and bottom-cell counts, volume, and a SHA-256 of the wet mask — because, as the concept puts it, no oracle can check that a mask is the water you meant. A true bathymetric mask (say, `Depth > 4000 m` within the box) is **not** supported by the attested executor and would need a new contract.
