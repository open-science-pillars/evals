## 2. Terms to compute

Formulation inherited verbatim from the attested pointwise budget (`knowledge/computations/ecco-heat-budget.md`, stable, maintainer-signed). Constants: `RHOCONST = 1029.0`, `C_P = 3994.0`, `R_SW = 0.62`, `ZETA1 = 0.6 m`, `ZETA2 = 20.0 m`. Volume element `rA · drF · hFacC` — partial cells in, and **not** re-applied to the `*MASS` velocities, which already carry `hFac`.

**LHS — tendency**
- z* scale factor `s* = 1 + ETAN/Depth` from the SSH snapshots
- `G_total = d(s* · THETA)/dt`, differenced across the 13 bracketing snapshots, `dt` in seconds per month
- integrated as `Σ G_total · rA · drF · hFacC` over the volume

**RHS term 1 — horizontal rim transport**, from **raw face fluxes at the boundary**, never from the divergence field (the telescoping identity holds for random noise and proves nothing):
- `fx = ADVx_TH + DFxE_TH`, `fy = ADVy_TH + DFyE_TH`
- `rim = Σfx(i0) − Σfx(i1) + Σfy(j0) − Σfy(j1)`

**RHS term 2 — vertical face transport**
- `fr = ADVr_TH + DFrE_TH + DFrI_TH`, zeroed on dry cells, bottom face zero-padded
- `vert = Σfr(k=K) − Σfr(k=0)`

**RHS term 3 — surface forcing with shortwave penetration**
- `q1 = R_SW·exp(RF/ZETA1) + (1−R_SW)·exp(RF/ZETA2)` at the upper face, `q2` at the lower; penetration truncated below `Z < −200 m`
- surface level: `TFLUX − (1 − (q1[0] − q2[0]))·oceQsw`
- sub-surface: `(q1·mskC − q2·mskC_down)·oceQsw`
- scaled by `1/(RHOCONST·C_P)` and `1/(hFacC·drF)`

**RHS term 4 — geothermal at the bottom cell**
- bottom-cell mask `mskB = mskC − mskC_down`
- `geothermalFlux · mskB / (RHOCONST·C_P) / (hFacC·drF)`

**Residual** = `LHS − (rim + vert + forcing + geothermal)`, reported per month, never absorbed into a term.

## 3. Closure bars — both required

| bar | threshold | units |
|---|---|---|
| absolute | max monthly \|residual\| / volume ≤ **1e-10** | degC/s |
| relative | max monthly \|residual\| / largest term ≤ **1e-6** | — |

One bar is not a criterion — that's the measured geothermal finding above.

Every receipt also carries **mutation evidence**: the executor re-runs four sabotages (geothermal omitted, rim west face shifted one cell, vertical face sign flipped, vertical faces omitted) and records each failing a bar. If a structural sabotage passes, or the correct run fails, **no receipt is written**. On a full-depth volume the geothermal sabotage will be `applicable: true` (unlike the 300 m reference box, where it correctly records `applicable: false`).
