## Why the region defeats the product

From `knowledge/podaac/datasets/grace-fo-mascons.md` (status stable, human-verified 2026-07-04 and 2026-09-13):

- The solution is 4,551 equal-area **3-degree spherical caps — order 300 km**. It is *distributed* as 0.5° grids, but "the native information scale is the mascon, not the grid cell."
- "A basin whose area approaches or falls below the mascon scale is resolution- and leakage-dominated."

The southeast Greenland shelf is a band of order tens to ~150 km wide between the coast and the shelf break (my own general knowledge, not from a concept). That is **narrower than a single mascon everywhere along it**. There is no set of whole ocean mascons that lies on that shelf — every candidate either straddles the coastline or sits within one block of it.

And `knowledge/podaac/gotchas/grace-coastal-leakage.md` (severity **high**, stable, human-verified 2026-07-04) names this exact case:

> Strong land signals (Greenland and glacier ice loss, large-basin hydrology) therefore leak into adjacent ocean mascons... **apparent coastal sea level or ocean-mass trends near Greenland, Alaska, or major river basins can be dominated by leakage rather than ocean change. Nothing errors; the series just is not what its coordinates claim.**

The CRI filter partitions straddling mascons using independent information and "reduces leakage substantially but does not eliminate it." The concept's prescription for nearshore work is a buffer from strong land sources — and southeast Greenland is the strongest land source in the northern hemisphere. The dataset concept also notes the formal per-mascon uncertainty grids **do not include** leakage or GIA, and that both exceed the formal errors regionally. So the error bar the product ships would understate the real one, in the one direction that matters here.

## Two things that do go your way

- **Your window is clean of the big gap.** 2019–2024 sits entirely inside GRACE-FO, so the eleven-month July 2017–May 2018 hole and the August/September 2018 months never enter the fit (`knowledge/podaac/gotchas/grace-intermission-gap.md`, severity high, stable). - **Use a file whose series runs past March 2025.** The July 2025 fix corrected the GAD mass added back to **the ocean part of land/ocean mascons** — precisely the mascons a southeast Greenland analysis would lean on — and only files extending past March 2025 carry it (`knowledge/podaac/recipes/grace-mass-to-sea-level.md`, stable).
