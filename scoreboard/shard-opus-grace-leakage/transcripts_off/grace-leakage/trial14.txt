## Why the number would have been misleading anyway

The request asks for an *ocean* mass trend in the one place where GRACE-FO's *land* signal is largest and its resolution is worst. Three things compound:

**1. There is no such thing as a shelf mascon there.** JPL's solution is ~4,500 equal-area 3° spherical-cap mascons (~300 km), posted on a 0.5° grid for convenience — the 0.5° cells inside one mascon carry identical values, so the fine grid is cosmetic, not resolution. The southeast Greenland shelf is roughly 50–150 km wide. Every mascon covering it also covers ice sheet.

**2. Southeast Greenland is among the largest concentrated mass-loss signals on Earth.** The Kangerdlugssuaq–Helheim sector loses mass at rates that, expressed as equivalent water thickness over a mascon, run to hundreds of mm/yr. A plausible ocean-mass trend on that shelf is a few mm/yr (the barystatic term plus regional redistribution). Order-of-magnitude, leakage across the coastline is one to two orders of magnitude larger than the signal you're asking about. The answer wouldn't be "noisy" — it would be a measurement of Greenland, wearing an ocean label, and it would come out strongly negative, opposite in sign to what the physics of that shelf implies.

**3. CRI does not rescue this.** The coastline-resolution-improvement filter partitions land and ocean *within* a straddling mascon using a priori assumptions. It's an allocation, not added resolution, and its residual error scales with the land signal it's separating from — which here is the worst case globally.

Two further issues that would bite even on a clean patch of ocean: the standard TELLUS mascon grid is not an ocean-bottom-pressure product as distributed (the de-aliasing product has to be handled deliberately before a manometric reading), and the trend carries the product's baked-in GIA model. And 2019–2024 is six years starting in Greenland's extreme 2019 melt year, with a handful of missing GRACE-FO months — the window itself loads the result before any leakage.

## What I'd do instead

Pick by what you're actually after:

- **Freshwater input to the SE Greenland shelf** — this is almost certainly the real question, and it's a *land-ice* problem, not an ocean one. GRACE-FO mascons over the SE Greenland drainage basins, with proper basin masks. That's the signal that's contaminating the ocean answer; measure it directly. The `land-ice` plugin exists for exactly this and isn't installed here.
- **Manometric sea level / OBP on the shelf** — GRACE is the wrong instrument at this scale. In-situ bottom pressure recorders, or a state estimate, with GRACE used only over the open Irminger/Labrador interior, several hundred km clear of the coast.
- **Total sea level on the shelf** — altimetry (NASA-SSH) with a steric estimate, noting that Argo doesn't sample shelf water.
